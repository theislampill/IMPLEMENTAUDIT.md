Consequently there is no honest sole source mutation in the inspected owner that inverts that complete predicate while leaving two valid controls intact.

- Changing DIRECT_RETURN text and then searching for it tests spelling, not behavior.

- Treating unknown or renamed requesters as valid direct callers would silently broaden a governed-only interface and erase the entry distinction under test.
