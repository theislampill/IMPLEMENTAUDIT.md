# Composed work and evidence

Use this reference through `audit-category-matrix.md` when a consequential
decision depends on compatible product, workflow, actor, and observation facts.
Specialize the existing audit-object finding and acceptance criteria; do not
create another controller, lifecycle, mandatory worksheet, or methodology mode.
One adequate atomic request/effect contract can discharge several concerns.

## Select the operative question

Name the actual claim or proposed effect, its intended recipient, existing
owner, hard constraints, and relevant alternatives. Retaining the present
system, manual coordination, a conventional implementation, narrower analysis,
non-use, and retirement remain legitimate outcomes. A property or technique
from another field is not automatically a required product mechanism.

Carry only the distinctions that can change this decision:

| Boundary | Preserve or establish |
|---|---|
| Product and configuration | Requested selection, realized artifact, relevant asset/generator/dependency versions, supported variants, and consequential interface assumptions. |
| Work and time | Definition/version, instance, work item, external object, logical delivery, pending work, and the meaning of completion. A route ID does not uniquely identify an external effect. |
| Actor and authority | Actual principal, relevant delegation and revocation state, capability, availability, permitted effect, and recipient acceptance. Assignment, intent and a role prompt do not enforce these boundaries. |
| Observation and inference | Event interpretation, explicit versus inferred links, missing/ambiguous cases, plausible order, population, observer/model/reference version, uncertainty, and supported use. |

An identical artifact digest does not establish unchanged semantics, permission,
or applicability. If records describe different scopes or regimes, establish a
justified mapping, narrow the claim, obtain a discriminator, or withhold only the
unsupported use. Preserve their original identities and evidence ceilings.

## Check the consequential composition

At the existing meaningful JOIN, inspect materially reachable combinations of
configuration, version, data, pending work, and responsibility. Pairwise
compatibility, local protocol conformance, model consistency, component passes,
and a list of controls do not independently establish the joint claim.
Discharge the interaction premises, prevent the unsupported combinations, or
record the unresolved obligation. Keep local evidence at its justified scope.

Distinguish a reachable exit, deadlock freedom, nonblocking behavior and eventual
completion. A global progress claim needs the relevant environment, resource,
fairness and refinement assumptions. A dependency on a peer's progress can form
a cycle even when each local model admits completion in isolation.

Keep informational relations separate from execution and acceptance
prerequisites. Co-change, process-mining correlation, shared labels and a shared
eventual product do not establish a hard scheduling edge. Preserve safe
unrelated work while buffering results whose acceptance truly depends on a JOIN.

## Interpret evidence without promoting its meaning

A logged declaration of performance is not recipient acceptance. A mined model
does not acquire normative authority from observed frequency. A deviation can
indicate harmful behavior, stale expectations, an authorized improvement, an
observation defect, or insufficient evidence; revise the correct owner.
Route a counterexample to the requirement, model, protocol, plan or source
decision responsible for the invalid assumption; recording a local gap alone
does not establish that the responsible owner received it.

When event order is uncertain, show that the conclusion holds across the
remaining plausible orders or qualify its dependence on an order. Preserve
case/object identity and extraction uncertainty instead of forcing ambiguous
events into a convenient total sequence.

For time or capacity claims, distinguish elapsed duration, worker effort and
resource occupancy; identify queues, concurrent work and the measurement unit.
For improvement claims, bind case mix, configuration, workflow/policy version,
observer, comparison unit and relevant regime changes. Aggregate improvement
can hide worse outcomes in every case class. Prediction or simulation alone is
not evidence that an intervention caused the claimed received benefit.

When learned or collective systems are actually in scope, retain relevant
task/seed/version variation, training versus deployment populations, and fixed
versus adapting or strategic partner assumptions. Cooperative guarantees do not
automatically apply to conflicting incentives; equilibrium or stability is not
welfare or legitimacy. These are claim qualifications, not a requirement to add
learning, game theory, an agent runtime, or a mining engine.

For a consequential predictive claim, check relevant leakage, censoring,
calibration and abstention assumptions using an adequate existing evidence
record. Popularity or longevity does not establish comparative effectiveness.
Mental-state labels or prompts do not establish implemented storage, updates,
commitment rules or conformance. Require those only when that capability is
actually claimed; a conventional implementation remains a legitimate choice.

Repeated sources, data, observations, models or research programs do not become
independent corroboration through different reports, workers or packet names.
Record common origins where they affect the claimed strength of evidence.

## Preserve recovery, succession and retirement obligations

After change, invalidate or narrow only evidence whose consequential assumptions
changed, and retain unaffected evidence. A newer boundary can supersede present
use without making an earlier verdict false at its original decision.
Recover a coherent version/instance/effect account before retry or replacement;
unknown completion is not proof of non-execution.

Separate local rollback, external compensation, forward repair and accepted
residual loss. Restoring code or a local record does not undo a received message
or another irreversible consequence. Before declaring closure or retiring a
helper, workflow version, worker or control, account for pending descendants,
late replies, supported old products and transferred obligations. Ending new
admission does not end existing support.

Changes to validators, policy, event interpretation, models or assurance
assumptions require justification under the governing predecessor authority.
A successor cannot be its own sole admission criterion. Reuse the existing
bounded change, evidence-transfer, Andon and reconciliation routes; this
reference grants no mutation, effect, review, installation or release credit.
