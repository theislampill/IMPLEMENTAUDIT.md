## Within-tradition system

The system coordinates definitions, instances, work items, data, resource eligibility/availability, messages, commitments and outside effects. It connects explicit admissibility with authorised capable actors, effect-bearing actions and evidence consumed by completion/recovery decisions. Its ordinary enactment, exception, reconciliation and authorised revision loops can overlap with concurrency and repeated work. It is not a forced serial pipeline.

The smallest coherent form can be a manual procedure with adequate identity, next/completion meaning, an accountable performer and proportionate result/exception evidence. Richer forms are triggered by consequential concurrency, shared data, scarce or constrained resources, autonomous participants, long-lived recovery, running-instance change, scientific reuse or learned/semantic uncertainty. Selecting, substituting, omitting or retiring a mechanism can be the correct synthesis outcome.

## Native concepts and abstraction levels

A workflow definition is not its running instance; an engine is not the organisational process; a work item is not every real action undertaken by its performer; and an event log is not the external world. Control-flow, data, resource and interaction semantics are related perspectives, not interchangeable views of one already-complete model. Classical soundness, assignment satisfiability, case completion, external acceptance and scientific result validity have different predicates and assumptions.

Criteria include completion, soundness, assignment feasibility, observation correspondence and contextual adequacy. Operating mechanisms include binding, enabling rules, work-item lifecycle, messaging, scoped handlers, retries, persistence, migration and discretionary planning. A criterion needs an establishing method and a consumer; a procedure can be empty ceremony without a consequential use. One integrated mechanism may satisfy several criteria while preserving separate evidence and genealogy.

## Required later equivalence and composition tests

Semantic equivalence requires matching the actual controlled failure, trigger, admissible states/transitions and observation claim—not only the same label. Complementarity requires compatible inputs/outputs and assumptions. Redundancy requires evidence that one mechanism already protects both concerns without losing authority or external-effect boundaries. Abstraction-level comparisons must separate organisational criteria, executable rules, formal models, data observations and real results.

A composition can introduce new obligations. For example, combining conformance feedback with adaptive migration creates a separate authority and live-state continuity question; combining a case goal with workflow termination creates a residual/acceptance question; combining analytics with durable history creates retention and semantic-version questions. These are tests to be performed later, not answers inferred for unread lanes. Preserve incompatible alternatives when a common mechanism is not justified.

## Selection, substitution, omission and retirement

The alternative configurations are native to this lane and remain conditional. Prescribed execution can be replaced or complemented by case/constraint support when valid next actions cannot be enumerated adequately. Definition coexistence can substitute for risky migration. Local atomic transactions can dominate compensation where their boundary actually includes the relevant effect. Safe recomputation can replace durable execution for ephemeral reversible work. A shared record can realise several distinctions in a small task.

Omission is legitimate when a trigger, risk or consumer is absent; retirement requires checking that no live instance, unresolved external action, accepted commitment or evidence consumer still depends on the removed mechanism. A later synthesis must not erase historical IDs or adverse criticism merely because several properties are realised together. Do not require one new control, one owner, one service or one artefact per retained source row.
