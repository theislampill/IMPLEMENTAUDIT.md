## Native subject and within-tradition result

Process mining relates recorded event evidence to explicit process representations through discovery, conformance, enhancement and associated operational analysis. Its plural genealogy includes software-process inference, workflow-log mining, formal net-based discovery, heuristic/inductive/region/evolutionary and declarative alternatives, model comparison, organisational/performance analysis, drift/streams and object-centric developments. Native concepts include event identity, trace/case notion, object participation, activity abstraction, process language, replay/alignment, fitness, precision, generalisation, model repair and process drift. Exact meanings and assumptions resolve in the domain models and source table.

The within-tradition synthesis is a conditional system rather than a union of all techniques. Interpretable evidence supports a selected model/constraint and a bounded inference. Domain challenge, source correction, model/reference revision, operational decisions and changed-regime evaluation form loops. Discovery can be omitted when a meaningful reference is supplied; prediction and streaming are optional; non-use is legitimate. Distinguishing behaviour, possessing authority/capability and achieving an effect remain separate.

EPM-084 is a new analytical composition-induced property: material validity conditions must survive consequential analytical handoffs. It prevents a downstream claim from silently changing the unit, semantics, time scope, uncertainty or authority of its input. Its proposed usefulness is argued, not established by an independent field trial of this whole system.

## Criteria, operating mechanisms and their coupling

A criterion such as temporal eligibility or reference authority is inert unless a method establishes it; a replay or prediction procedure is ceremonial if no consumer uses its result within the supported scope. Event checks, transformations, model selection, comparison, participant challenge and prospective evaluation are operating mechanisms. Their criteria state the necessary distinctions and limits. The export preserves both without requiring one new implementation per source property. A shared mechanism can satisfy several criteria, but its genealogy and criticisms must remain traceable.

## Questions for the Business Process Management lane

How does its independently researched account establish the purpose, process boundary, relevant participants and authority for redesign, and which of those are prerequisites rather than outputs of mining? When mining finds a discrepancy, does the BPM lane distinguish a descriptive update, process correction, legitimate exception and authorised change to an intended process? What actual outcome evaluation would discriminate a useful analysis from a successful redesign? Where does its evidence address organisational participation and costs rather than treating mined insight as self-executing? These questions arise from EPM-008, EPM-031, EPM-036, EPM-053, EPM-064 and EPM-084; they are not answers on behalf of the unread lane. [EPM-S001; EPM-S016; EPM-S017; EPM-S032]

## Questions for the Workflow Management lane

Which event and lifecycle semantics does enactment actually generate, and how do they correspond to the evidence required by EPM-001, EPM-005 and EPM-060? Which execution guarantees apply to the exact workflow model, and are they compatible with the model class used for discovery or conformance? How are partial cases, version changes, exceptions, resource authority and actual actions represented? Can a model be descriptive in one lane and operative in another without silently changing its authority or semantics? What is required to connect case-centric enactment with shared-object relations? These are reconciliation questions grounded in EPM-012–EPM-015, EPM-031–EPM-038, EPM-054–EPM-058 and EPM-084, not claims about an uninspected workflow corpus. ## Required later reconciliation tests

Test semantic equivalence by comparing object, trigger, protected failure, mechanism and output—not a shared slogan. Test complementarity by identifying an actual receiving dependency. Test redundancy before adding machinery, preserving IDs even when implementations merge. Test abstraction levels before equating a data format, discovery method, organisational criterion and authority rule. Test incompatible assumptions, especially case/object identity, model soundness, temporal windows, uncertainty, privacy and causal interference. Test shared ancestry and datasets before claiming independent corroboration. Finally, examine whether connecting the lanes creates new costs or obligations beyond each component; EPM-084 offers a conditional example, not a pre-approved answer for every interface.

## Selection, substitution, omission and retirement

Select only mechanisms whose triggers hold and whose evidence/assumptions match the receiving question. Substitute an existing or cheaper mechanism when it achieves the protected postcondition. Omit irrelevant branches, and retire controls whose consumer, risk or dependency disappears while preserving justified historical evidence. Unresolved or rejected candidates must not be converted into universal target requirements. Any adoption, architecture change or target-native mapping belongs in a new separately evidenced crosswalk.
