# Process Mining — public-documentation intake

Revision `EPM-R1-2026-09-06`; evidence cut-off 2026-09-06. “Evolved Process Mining” names a researcher’s criticism-tested synthesis, not a recognised historical school.

Process mining is the established field that links event data to explicit process representations through discovery, conformance checking, enhancement and related operational analysis. Its early history is plural: 1998 work studied software-process inference, workflow-log mining and probabilistic/algorithmic AS-IS discovery; later contributions developed formal workflow mining, organisational analysis, research tools and alternative discovery methods. The Manifesto consolidated the field’s scope and challenges rather than creating every component. [EPM-S001; EPM-S002; EPM-S003 and EPM-S004, abstract/metadata; EPM-S005]

The tradition developed through criticism as well as invention. Noise-sensitive reconstruction motivated heuristic and alternative methods; incomprehensible models motivated abstraction; declarative constraints needed activation-aware interpretation; conformance diagnostics gained costed and multi-perspective semantics; case flattening motivated object-centric representations. More recent work studies uncertain events, drift and streams, predictive and causal support, privacy/federation and checked language assistance. These branches retain different assumptions and costs rather than forming a single universal best method. [EPM-S006–EPM-S014; EPM-S025; EPM-S034; EPM-S035; EPM-S042]

## Explanatory thesis

The surviving core is an accountable relation between event evidence, an explicit process representation and a bounded use. A log is not the whole organisation; a discovered model is not uniquely identified ground truth; a conformance result is not automatically a judgement of legality or fairness; and a prediction is not evidence that a suggested intervention will improve outcomes. Richer methods are selected because a consequential distinction requires them, not because more models, agents, dashboards or formality indicate maturity.

The composed account links event interpretation, case/object choice, model bias, validation and real consumers. It includes source-correction loops, participant challenge, distinct descriptive/reference revision, post-intervention re-evaluation and legitimate non-use. Its additional handoff invariant, EPM-084, requires material assumptions and limits to remain visible when results pass between methods or actors. This is an analytical proposal grounded in component research, not a claim of independently measured effectiveness of the whole system.

## Strongest properties to explain

Meaningful events and traceable extraction prevent data formats from being mistaken for observed work. A justified case/object notion prevents projection artefacts from becoming claims of rework or delay. Explicit model and reference semantics keep discovery and comparison within their guarantees. Separate quality dimensions and valid experimental units prevent metric shortcuts. Correct temporal inputs and a causal boundary prevent retrospective prediction from becoming an unwarranted intervention strategy. Reproducibility, domain challenge, privacy and contestability make claims examinable without equating an audit trail with truth. These can often be realised by small existing mechanisms.

## Criticisms that changed the synthesis

The analysis rejects universal object-centric superiority, universal deep-model superiority, universal positive economic return, perfect fitness as sufficient validity, conformance as sufficient legality/fairness, and authority inferred from event data. Original alpha is retained historically and as a scoped baseline but not as an unrestricted default. Dashboard possession is not a general property. A duplicate activity-refinement candidate remains visible rather than increasing the retained count. Broad realised causal benefit from prescription and broad external validity of uncertain object-centric analysis remain examined but unresolved.

## Evidence and current frontier

The corpus contains 84 examined candidates and 58 exact source records, not a statistically independent sample of 58 deployments. Formal proofs, specifications, retrospective benchmarks, qualitative accounts, field responses and simulations establish different claims. Reused public logs, bootstrap samples and conference/journal versions do not multiply independent organisational replications. Five records have narrower access, and the full source table states what was actually inspected.

Current work includes dynamic object identities, object-centric conceptual integration, sequential causal optimisation, collaborative prediction, privacy-preserving federation, uncertainty-aware prediction and text-to-event extraction. The 2026 text-extraction work explicitly lacks a fully operational business deployment; current preprints are not presented as settled replicated findings. Language-model evaluation is task- and judge-dependent. Secure computation does not automatically protect information revealed in outputs. These are concrete research limits, not reasons to erase the capabilities themselves. [EPM-S026–EPM-S029; EPM-S033; EPM-S044; EPM-S055; EPM-S057]

## Suggested page outline

Open with the event-data/process-model relation and the plural genealogy. Explain how extraction and case/object choices shape evidence. Introduce discovery and conformance as different operations, then show why model evaluation, performance attribution and prediction require additional assumptions. Present criticism-driven changes with a few concrete counterexamples. Explain the conditional composed system and its external consumers. Close with current research, explicit limits and access to the complete denominator rather than a top-properties substitute.

## Claims not to make

Do not invent an origin or academic consensus for the compound “Evolved” label. Do not assign the whole field to one author or equate a citation with documented derivation. Do not extend a proof beyond its exact model assumptions, an abstract beyond inspected content, a specification into adoption, a benchmark into deployed benefit, or one industrial response into a universal return. Do not equate popularity, tool possession, perfect fitness, privacy terminology or fluent generated content with maturity. Do not imply an unverified host-system adoption, an already completed cross-tradition synthesis, or authority to change real work merely because a model can describe it.
