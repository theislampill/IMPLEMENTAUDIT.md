# Evolved Lineage Depth-Reopen Prompt Template

Reopen a frozen lineage only when a named evidence gap could change a property boundary, status, denominator, criticism, source selection or engineering decision.

## Required reopen record

- exact frozen packet identity and affected property IDs;
- the specific unresolved question or contradictory evidence;
- why existing sources cannot decide it;
- bounded new source classes and selection rules;
- prior conclusion preserved verbatim;
- revised conclusion, if any, with causal explanation;
- denominator and schema impact;
- independent review of the delta;
- a new immutable packet identity without overwriting history.

Do not reopen merely to increase source volume, modernise wording, or force transfer into a target repository.
