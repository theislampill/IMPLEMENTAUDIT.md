# Evolved Lineage Research Prompt Template

Use this template to research one engineering lineage without presuming adoption by a target repository.

## Contract

1. Define the lineage boundary and distinguish historical schools, later descendants, hybrids and caricatures.
2. Build a source register with exact titles, dates/versions, locators, claim supported, contrary evidence and evidence class.
3. Preserve the complete candidate-property denominator; do not retain only favourable results.
4. For every property record origin, problem, failure mode, mechanism, trigger, non-trigger/cheap path, dependencies, authority boundary, consumer, payoff, criticisms, evolved form, status, evidence strength and open questions.
5. Separate source genealogy, formal evidence, empirical comparison, incident evidence, field practice and criticism.
6. Strip ceremony when the underlying engineering mechanism survives; record rejected and superseded forms in the denominator.
7. Produce a human report, machine-readable property ledger, source table/register, audit intake and public-documentation intake where warranted.
8. Freeze exact bytes, member identities, denominator counts and a terminal receipt.

Do not assign target-repository owners or implementation status in the lineage packet.
