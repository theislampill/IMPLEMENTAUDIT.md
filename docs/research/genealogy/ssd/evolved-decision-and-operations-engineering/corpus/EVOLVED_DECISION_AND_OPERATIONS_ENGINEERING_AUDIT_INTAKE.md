## CONTEXTS_WHERE_PROPERTY_SHOULD_NOT_TRIGGER

- P026: Use a transparent priority/ageing rule with explicit exceptions; avoid queueing optimisation when consequences clearly order work.
- P033: Use a minimum-improvement threshold and protect near-term committed work unless safety or feasibility changes.
- P034: Start the highest-value conflict-free work; remain idle when no eligible work has positive net consequence.
- P036: Use a policy threshold from authoritative service requirement and observed variability; do not estimate a full cost curve when not identifiable.
- P039: Check whether the incumbent and bound imply different real actions; stop if not and model uncertainty dominates.
- P040: Inspect binding constraints and perform a small finite change scenario rather than treating an infinitesimal dual as truth.

- P054: Prefer a transparent calibrated predictor plus robust/simple decision rule when end-to-end learning has no demonstrated incremental decision value.
