## Evidence partitions to preserve

Provenance, theory, comparative evidence, field evidence, replication, contrary findings and transferability remain separate in every record. A strongly retained distinction is not a high-percentage effect estimate. Formal results for trade, voting, protocol completion, constraint optimisation, value mixing and fault-tolerant DCOP can have strong within-model justification but weak or absent deployment validation. The later system must establish the model assumptions and implementation correspondence.

The airport report supports documented operational use, not an estimate of prevented attacks. The disaster-response work describes staged field trials and practitioner feedback, not real-disaster deployments. Current LLM/MARL comparisons share models, code, tasks or judges and may not match all training/inference resources. The small 2026 human-explanation study’s null finding is neither equivalence nor proof of no benefit. These distinctions must survive any target crosswalk. [EMAS-S024–028; EMAS-S035–043; EMAS-S051; EMAS-S054]

The four composition-induced candidates EMAS-071–074 are analytical coupling requirements. They have not been validated as one complete architecture. A later target must provide evidence for compatible semantic bindings, actual authority, material effects, collective outcomes and continuity under change; the mere presence of all source-inspired components is insufficient.

## Neutral system-level questions

What work, participants and resources are actually interdependent, and why are the selected agent boundaries preferable to a feasible simpler alternative? Which guarantees concern messages, intentions, public obligations, assignments, learned policies or external outcomes? Who observes the relevant consequence, who may act or revise an obligation, and what happens when those roles disagree or information is missing?

When a participant, incentive, protocol, norm or communication assumption changes, which claims cease to apply and how are outstanding effects/obligations handled? Are the actual failure modes distinct from those assumed by a replica, trust or recovery mechanism? Which costs, people and outside effects are absent from the reported objective? These questions do not presuppose that a new subsystem, document or owner is needed.
