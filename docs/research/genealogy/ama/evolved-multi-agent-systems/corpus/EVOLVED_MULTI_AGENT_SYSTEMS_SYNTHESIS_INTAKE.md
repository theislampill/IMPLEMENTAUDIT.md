## Within-tradition synthesis

The composed MAS system selects mechanisms for actual interdependence among decision makers, using explicit information, incentive, authority and failure assumptions. It keeps communication meaning, private plans, public obligations, resources, action and collective consequences distinct while specifying their necessary couplings. The result is a conditional relational system with concurrent activity, feedback, interrupts and human decisions, not a serial checklist or universal central coordinator.

The smallest coherent form consists of a defined dependency/population, an adequate interaction, capable and authorised actors, a consequence criterion, an observation path and an appropriate response to material failures/assumption changes. Richer forms are alternatives: cooperative teamwork, open strategic interaction, structured distributed optimisation, adaptive collectives and a human/organisational overlay. The selected system can legitimately use a single scheduler or ordinary services for part of the work.

Four composition-induced properties preserve cross-layer obligations: EMAS-071 observation–obligation–action closure; EMAS-072 regime-change handoff; EMAS-073 minimum adequate coordination; EMAS-074 dependency-coupled adaptation. These are this study’s analytical contributions, not separately established academic doctrines or an independently evaluated combined architecture.

## Native concepts and documented intersections

Contract Net addresses distributed task connection; partial global planning addresses interacting local plans; joint intentions and SharedPlans address different aspects of cooperative undertaking; public social commitments and information protocols provide externally inspectable interaction objects. Roles, missions and normative conditions connect organisational models to action, but external authority remains independent. Constraint optimisation, game-theoretic mechanisms, voting, argumentation and learning answer distinct questions.

Imports must retain provenance: economics and social choice supply bounded strategic results; abstract argumentation comes from AI/non-monotonic-logic work, with an explicit Dung-inspired inter-agent dialogue adaptation in Amgoud, Maudet and Parsons (2000); Gaia documents an organisation/engineering intersection. An LLM role or conversation is only analogous to one of these concepts unless the underlying semantics, state and operations are actually present. The key coupled chain is not an execution order: a state distinction requires appropriate evidence; an obligation or permission needs an agreed semantic and authority basis; an actor needs capability; and the claimed consequence needs observation. Any of these can be absent while neighbouring components function. Revisions, failures and new participants feed back into affected dependencies, not necessarily the whole system.
