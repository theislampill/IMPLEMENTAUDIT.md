## D. Canonical AOSE genealogy

AOSE is the established family of software-engineering approaches that uses agents and their interactions in requirements, analysis, design, implementation and associated lifecycle reasoning. It is not identical to agent-oriented programming, any particular agent platform, an arbitrary multi-agent application, or asking a coding agent to produce software. The canonical genealogy preserves a plurality of origins and imports rather than retrospectively naming all earlier work AOSE. [EAOSE-CS018; EAOSE-CS023; EAOSE-CS049; EAOSE-CS060]

Intentional programming and BDI/practical-reasoning foundations address computational commitment, beliefs, goals, plans, failure and reconsideration. Actor and distributed task-allocation approaches are important neighbouring or precursor abstractions, but an actor is not automatically a goal-directed autonomous agent and a Contract Net protocol is not a complete development method. MAS-CommonKADS, DESIRE and team-oriented approaches add knowledge, inference, compositional and organisational concerns that cannot all be reduced to role diagrams. [EAOSE-CS005; EAOSE-CS001; EAOSE-CS006; EAOSE-CS002; EAOSE-CS012; EAOSE-CS010; EAOSE-CS013]

The requirements branch models intentional actors, goals, softgoals, strategic dependencies and vulnerabilities. Tropos extends this style of analysis into later design; Secure Tropos and adaptive/interactive descendants add specific security and evolution concerns. The point of continuity is preserving meaning and responsibility through transformations, not proving requirements correct by drawing a complete graph. Stakeholder discovery, contested priorities, model understandability and actual acceptance remain substantive obligations. [EAOSE-CS032; EAOSE-CS039; EAOSE-CS011; EAOSE-CS044; EAOSE-CS056; EAOSE-CS048; EAOSE-CS052]

Gaia, MaSE/O-MaSE, Prometheus, PASSI/Agile PASSI, INGENIAS and Agent OPEN provide different scopes, concepts, phases, transformations and usable prescriptions. The original Gaia scope cannot be silently strengthened by later open/dynamic research. O-MaSE's method engineering is not identical to its MaSE predecessor, and a process standard-view document is not the long journal account. Prometheus workshop and proceedings dates need not mean distinct research findings. A named phase, metamodel or CASE tool does not establish that the corresponding engineering task is automatically performed. [EAOSE-CS022; EAOSE-CS035; EAOSE-CS024; EAOSE-CS055; EAOSE-CS057; EAOSE-CS029; EAOSE-CS030; EAOSE-CS041; EAOSE-CS043; EAOSE-CS053; EAOSE-CS033; EAOSE-CS040]

Roles, organisations and public interactions form several related but non-identical branches. MOISE and multidimensional integration separate agents, organisations and environments; ASPECS supplies holonic specialisation; ADELFE supplies a cooperative self-organisation strategy. AUML/FIPA representation and message standards do not themselves establish shared public meaning, authentication, actual authority or compatible transport behaviour. Commitment and information-protocol work addresses public accountability without outlawing useful private BDI deliberation inside a participant. [EAOSE-CS028; EAOSE-CS074; EAOSE-CS051; EAOSE-CS037; EAOSE-CS020; EAOSE-CS027; EAOSE-CS014; EAOSE-CS080; EAOSE-CS098; EAOSE-CS061]

Verification, model consistency, model-driven generation, agent testing and testability evolve under different limitations. A language-specific proof concerns a precise semantics, while a partial-design checker can locate defects without claiming sound and complete coverage of deployed behaviour. Requirements testing still needs a meaningful oracle. Environmental validity, action/percept mappings, manually implemented obligations, scheduling and source dependence prevent a local result from becoming a complete system certificate. [EAOSE-CS059; EAOSE-CS068; EAOSE-CS072; EAOSE-CS047; EAOSE-CS042; EAOSE-CS064; EAOSE-CS083]

The AOSE/DALT/ProMAS-to-EMAS trajectory demonstrates continuing engineering research, not universal adoption or maintenance of every historical method. Contemporary work on roles, timing, norms, explanations, communication adapters and learned-plan hybrids is evaluated under its own publication/version and evidence boundaries. Conventional deployment, testing and DevOps practices are deliberate imports or extensions where warranted; they are not invented features of every early AOSE methodology. [EAOSE-CS110; EAOSE-CS109; EAOSE-CS104; EAOSE-CS079; EAOSE-CS112; EAOSE-CS099; EAOSE-CS124; EAOSE-CS111; EAOSE-CS102; EAOSE-CS096]

Thirty canonical genealogy nodes and 32 typed edges preserve all 49 A/B node observations. Edges explicitly distinguish documented ancestry, methodological import, peer comparison, criticism-response, contemporary convergence and this analytical synthesis. No popularity or source-count inference is substituted for that history. [CANONICAL_RESEARCH_COVERAGE#/genealogy]

## H. Re-derived composition: conditional configurations and typed decisions

The canonical model contains **95 newly derived guarded relations** and **10 alternative configurations**. It does not union the two input edge sets or create a mandatory AOSE pipeline. Needs inquiry can interrupt design; an implementation failure can reopen a requirement interpretation; several agents can realise one role, and one agent can realise several roles; monitoring, deliberation and interaction may proceed concurrently. Every relation states its trigger, assumptions, authority/evidence flow, failure witness and cheaper/non-trigger path. [CANONICAL_COMPOSITION_MODEL]

The normal operating question is not “which method comes next?” It is “what claim or action is being attempted, which mechanism can establish it in this configuration, what evidence and authority does it require, and what failure or change would reopen it?” Requirements and social dependencies constrain role/protocol/internal design, but implementations and observations also expose incorrect requirements or insufficient scope. A method can therefore include AO requirements analysis and conventional implementation, a stable cooperative organisation, BDI deliberation, public commitments, dynamic organisation, a specialised cooperative/holonic option, stronger assurance or a bounded learned hybrid. None of these alternatives is automatically best.

The state distinctions are deliberately not collapsed:

**intention ≠ obligation ≠ authority ≠ permission ≠ capability ≠ issued action ≠ executed action ≠ observed effect ≠ accepted outcome.**

An intention can exist without permission. A role can carry a duty without the current ability to fulfil it. A legitimately authorised command can fail to execute; an executed action can have an unintended effect; a beneficial effect can remain unauthorised or fail the actual acceptance condition. A formal permission in a model also needs a warranted bridge to the actual authority arrangement. These are operative distinctions, not a demand to build nine separate databases.

Prospective action admission must not require observation of an outcome that cannot yet exist. A bounded authorised experiment may be admitted on an appropriate expectation and risk basis while its outcome remains unsettled. Retrospective outcome acceptance then needs evidence of the relevant consequence. The model distinguishes these decisions and does not convert uncertainty into success or paralysis by conflating their evidence requirements. [COMPOSITION_INDUCED_RECONCILIATION; EAOSE-C086]

Selective revalidation is an invalidation-of-support mechanism, not a rule of automatic conclusion falsity. A changed assumption may make one warrant inapplicable while an independent sufficient warrant survives. Recheck affected configurations and dependencies; do not erase unrelated evidence. Organisational change similarly has at least two questions: is the new role/capability allocation valid, and what happens to already-incurred obligations? Satisfying one does not answer the other. [EAOSE-C103; EAOSE-C024; EAOSE-C105]

## EVOLVED_AGENT_ORIENTED_SOFTWARE_ENGINEERING_SCOPE_AND_CARICATURES

The preserved conventional branch is part of a defensible account of abstraction choice, not an admission that AOSE never helps. The canonical boundary permits shared primary literature while forbidding sibling-corpus conclusions.
