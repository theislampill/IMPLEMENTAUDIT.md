## Native concepts and within-tradition result

Native objects include situated agent/environment relations; observations and beliefs; goals, desires, intentions and plan recipes; conditions/effects and capabilities; reactive policies, BDI interpreters and deliberative/hybrid architectures; time/resource-bounded choice; learning state and experience; communication and transfer of control; and task/constraint evaluation. These objects occur in plural schools and are not assumed to share one formal semantics.

The composed result couples goal and authority conditions to sufficient observation, an adequate decision branch and monitored real action, with feedback to continuation, repair, retirement or bounded revision. Communication acts through other actors and requires actual response/acceptance where consequential. Evaluation and formal evidence remain separate from authorisation. Learning, reflection and high autonomy are optional. The complete machine-readable relation model preserves guards, alternatives, incompatibilities and unresolved obligations rather than imposing one serial pipeline.
