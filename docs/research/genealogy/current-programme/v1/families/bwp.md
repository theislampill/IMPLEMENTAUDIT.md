## Already-public event and comparison examples

The public [R0040 event-meaning record](https://github.com/theislampill/IMPLEMENTAUDIT.md/blob/e78f7cb21af4197d490f10bc95917a4c75f748aa/docs/research/implementaudit/r0040-event-meaning-qualification.md#b-identical-bytes-and-changed-meaning) includes a synthetic event with identical `done` bytes under two definitions: normal function return and recipient acceptance. Without a justified acceptance mapping, the normal-return observation remains usable while the promoted acceptance claim is withheld. Changing one dependent interpretation does not invalidate every unaffected observation. Stable meaning with adequate evidence can retain the direct path without migration or intervention.

Its [constructed case-mix control](https://github.com/theislampill/IMPLEMENTAUDIT.md/blob/e78f7cb21af4197d490f10bc95917a4c75f748aa/docs/research/implementaudit/r0040-event-meaning-qualification.md#c-prediction-comparison-causal-benefit-and-external-validity) raises aggregate success from 54% to 76% while easy-case success falls from 90% to 85% and hard-case success from 45% to 40%. The example exposes why a changed case population can defeat a causal-benefit claim even when the aggregate improves. These are deliberately constructed observations, not measured deployed effects or a real causal estimate.
