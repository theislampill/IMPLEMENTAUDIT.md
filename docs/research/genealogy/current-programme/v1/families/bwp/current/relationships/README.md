# BWP — full relationships, bounded negatives and alternatives

How can a claim about purpose or performed work change a workflow or event-based inquiry without proving the whole receiving conclusion? The accepted BWP synthesis answers through specific source–receiver judgments. A contribution can supply a question, constrain a case interpretation, expose a discrepancy or inform selection. It becomes material only in its stated use. The opposite direction needs its own produced, communicated and consumed result. A shared source, common vocabulary or possible co-use supplies neither that result nor independent confirmation.

This reader completes the detailed current population alongside the [eleven original synthesis blocks](../synthesis/BWP-S.md) and the full [scientific account](../BWP-S.md). It preserves 15,841 unique direction records, 18,221 pair judgments and 217 bounded-negative blocks from the exact accepted recovered successor. These are analytical records and memberships, not independent trials. The [source qualification](../SOURCE_QUALIFICATION.json) and original evidence limits continue to govern them.

## Actual partial inputs and a separately guarded return

The three final forward additions retain different functions: [EWM-031 testimony](directions/relations-159.md#BWP-R7S003-0001), [EBPM-037 workaround explanation](directions/relations-159.md#BWP-R7S003-0002) and [EWM-061 inspected execution/data semantics](directions/relations-159.md#BWP-R7S003-0003) can change the EPM-012 case premise. A label, channel, version badge or unused account is insufficient. The applicable account must be produced, communicated, credible and relevant, and consumed for the same question, work population and interpretation period. Other identity, relation, receipt, authority and field-validity obligations remain separate. Rejection of a bad case correlation can be useful even when a unique replacement is still unknown.

No new reverse follows from those additions. The separately retained r6 case-count return concerns an actually established count that is delivered and consumed within the matching measurement scope. The account’s example changes 15/3 = 5 to 15/2 = 7.5 only after the two-case denominator is supported. A partial exclusion does not supply that denominator, an appropriate numerator, causal benefit or intervention authority. The [material input and selector cards](material-inputs.md) also retain the alternatives: an adequate existing identifier, short codebook, direct conversation or small relation table can suffice; non-use or no change can be legitimate. Their 483 selector cards and 966 orientations are not a global minimum proof or permission to discard protected evidence.

## Reading and reconstruction

Every selected field, scalar type and list order from these twelve scientific source records is retained after specifically scoped safety normalization. Repeated identical values are stored once in [shared scientific data](../data/relationships/shared-records.json); a `$scientific_ref` resolves recursively through its definitions. Human readers link to the same full values. The [record index](../data/relationships/RECORD_INDEX.json) binds each population to its actual source digest, while the [navigation data](../data/relationships/NAVIGATION.json) locates every direction, pair and negative block.

Literal native-relative source filenames preserve scientific identity and historical context; they do not assert that an old working directory is a public file or that a missing original was recovered. Genuine private worker context identifiers are replaced by their attributed roles. Public bibliographic document identifiers are preserved. Original author-stage pending or qualification fields remain dated history and do not override the later exact source qualifications.

This is a faithful documentary reconstruction of existing science. It does not rerun the source programs, reconstruct the lost predecessor freeze, establish an independent empirical sample, or turn scientific qualification into native implementation or current publication acceptance. The original exit-1 case execution and all other adverse limits remain in the [scientific account](../BWP-S.md).
