## Tradition and genealogy

Business Process Management connects process purpose, evidence about actual work, legitimate change and observed consequences. Its scientific predicate is not the possession of a process map, owner title or maturity score. Those artefacts matter when they perform a justified function for a real recipient and decision.

The public predecessor describes a plural genealogy: industrial engineering and quality management; BPR and cross-functional redesign; office-information and workflow systems; representation, analysis and enactment; organisational capabilities and governance; and newer responsible, flexible and sustainability-oriented approaches. It distinguishes documented influence from shared ancestry and analytical reconstruction. “Evolved Business Process Management” is the packet’s analytical label, not the name of a universally recognised historical school.

## Distinct predicates and within-lineage composition

The historical account distinguishes warranted understanding, legitimate and feasible action, and observed consequences. Purpose and beneficiaries determine a proportionate material boundary. Triangulated accounts of performed work qualify a representation. A representation must fit its communication, analysis or execution use. Effective authority, resources and knowledge qualify who can act. Plural outcome measures and a justified comparison qualify what can be claimed about improvement. A valid diagram does not settle organisational truth; deployment does not settle recipient benefit.

The public within-lineage composition permits evidence, exception and outcome feedback to reopen earlier decisions. It preserves lightweight stewardship, structured repeatable work, case-oriented professional work, negotiated inter-organisational work and evidence-intensive decision support as alternative configurations. Formal checking, simulation, automation or a new governance arrangement needs a real trigger. A concise shared account and a capable existing actor can be adequate.

The public strongest-property set is EBPM-001 (purposes and beneficiaries), 006 (triangulated performed work), 010 (purpose-fit representation), 014 (modelling/execution contract), 017 (outcome-valid plural measures), 018 (elapsed time and work effort), 022 (causal restraint), 031 (effective authority) and 044 (decision-linked monitoring). ## Counterexamples, alternatives and evidence limits

A department can reduce its local handling time while moving delay or burden to another actor. A readable process model can support discussion while lacking an execution contract. A workaround can expose a real mismatch while also evading a necessary control. These distinctions explain why purpose, evidence, authority and consequence require separate questions.

The public predecessor contains 73 property candidates: 58 conditionally usable, fourteen non-retained and one unresolved. Its 48 source records are not 48 independent effectiveness experiments. Its longitudinal example preserves 25 intended processes in ten organisations, seven implemented processes in five organisations, and five raw lead-time improvements versus two deteriorations. Those nested and context-dependent populations establish neither universal benefit nor a general failure rate. EBPM-053 remains a historical unresolved candidate; a goal prompt supplies no organisational authority.
