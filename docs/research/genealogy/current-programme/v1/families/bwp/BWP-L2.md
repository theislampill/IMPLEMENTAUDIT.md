## Tradition and genealogy

Workflow Management represents, coordinates and enacts work across people and applications. Its central distinctions include definitions, running instances, work items and outside objects; enabling, authority and capability; and completion declaration versus achieved effect.

The public genealogy joins office information systems and document routing, transactions, cooperative work, language-action approaches, formal workflow nets and patterns, adaptive and declarative work, case management, scientific workflows and durable execution. The WfMC reference-model draft of 29 November 1994 and final release of 19 January 1995 are distinct artefacts. That consolidation is not a claim of sole origin for the whole tradition.

## Distinct predicates and within-lineage composition

A workflow definition describes behaviour; a running instance has actual state, data and outstanding obligations. A work item can be enabled without an available authorised performer. Possible completion, proper completion, deadlock freedom and inevitable termination are different claims. An engine’s completion status and a recipient’s accepted outcome are different predicates. Control, data, resources, messages and external effects must be interpreted within the actual model and environment.

The historical composition preserves concurrency, loops, interruption, recovery and revision. A small stable task can use a concise procedure, accountable performer and adequate result check. Long-lived cross-application work can require persistence, correlation, retry and version contracts. Prescribed, declarative, case-centred, scientific and replay-based forms are alternatives with distinct assumptions, not a mandatory combined stack.

The public account highlights definition/instance identity, explicit semantics, model/engine correspondence, case/message correlation, work-item ownership, authority versus readiness, achieved effects, interrupt scope, timeout uncertainty, compensation, duplicate-effect control, migration validity and observation limits. Each has its own trigger, consumer, cheaper adequate path and evidence ceiling in the complete reader.

These paragraphs describe the published predecessor, with exact property, composition and source-table locators available below. A named property or diagram is not an implementation verdict.

## Counterexamples, alternatives and evidence limits

A sound new definition can be invalid for a running case if a newly required step is already behind its state, a required datum has no producer or a pending callback loses its target. A timeout can leave an external effect unknown. Compensation can fail and is not universal rollback. A test-execution success can coexist with an incorrect scientific output. These are different receiving predicates, not one generic workflow PASS.
