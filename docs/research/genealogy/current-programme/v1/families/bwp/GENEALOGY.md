# BWP source and scientific genealogy

## Layer 1 — the established traditions

The three lineages are related but not interchangeable. Business Process Management examines purpose, actual work, intervention, governance and consequences. Workflow Management examines representation and enactment, including state, resources, interactions, exceptions and versions. Process Mining examines the relation between event evidence, process representations and bounded analytical use. Each has a plural genealogy and distinct source/evidence types. Their public original accounts link historical nodes, criticism, mechanisms, domain boundaries and source-specific limits. [BPM](BWP-L1.md), [Workflow Management](BWP-L2.md), [Process Mining](BWP-L3.md).

“Evolved” is the studies’ analytical label. It does not establish a new historically recognised academic school, sole founder or universal method. Documented import, explicit extension, shared ancestry, analogy and contemporary criticism are different genealogy relations. A source appearing in two lineages does not become two independent empirical replications.
