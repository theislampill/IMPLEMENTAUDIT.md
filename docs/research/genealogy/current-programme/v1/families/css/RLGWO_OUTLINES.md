A stop request is different from a contained state; a restored artifact is different from a restored safety constraint and observability.

### Composition questions, alternatives and falsifiers

1. Are all claimed joint predicates attached to the same relevant state, configuration, time and consumer? A clear handoff about one state, a measurement about another and an effective stop in a third do not establish one joint outcome.

3. Is a repeated observation, reviewer, case or shared source being counted once at the appropriate predicate, or recycled as three independent confirmations?
4. Is a local success being promoted to whole-system safety, native authority, scientific qualification or current acceptance?
5. Does a direct authoritative comparison, an existing stronger control, a smaller representation, no change or a bounded safe stop settle the consumer's actual question?
6. Are negative, rejected, unresolved, deferred, nontriggered and research-only states retained as substantive outcomes with an accessible continuation where one is warranted?
