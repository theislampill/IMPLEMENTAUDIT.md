This is a source-layered genealogy. The historical public corpus remains the original 62 + 65 + 68 record population, and the original historical synthesis cannot be reconstructed merely by relabelling a later neutral study. The selected current scientific subjects are identified separately below.

### Cognitive Systems Engineering

The public predecessor describes overlapping traditions rather than a single family tree. Classical human factors made displays, controls, workspaces and human performance engineering concerns. Supervisory control studied a person directing automation that executes lower-level work. Cognitive Systems Engineering, work-domain analysis and joint cognitive systems extended attention to the work performed through people and tools; distributed cognition traced memory and calculation across people, representations and time. Common-ground and team-cognition research supplied coordination, mutual prediction and discrepancy repair. These strands share problems and sometimes authors, but that does not make them one uninterrupted lineage. [Cognitive Page][c-page] · [Source table][c-sources]

A recurring correction concerns residual work: automation can remove routine activity while retaining monitoring, diagnosis and rare recovery demands. More visible information need not make those remaining demands manageable. The historical corpus therefore separates the engineering property—usable state, feasible intervention and joint performance—from named instruments such as a situation-awareness measure, workload score or full work-analysis programme. Handoff bundles, crew-resource-management methods and domain interface designs retain the limits of the settings in which they were studied. The public intake explicitly forbids claiming universal synergy or direct unchanged transfer into agent workflows. [Public intake, genealogy and evidence limits][c-intake]

### Statistical Engineering

Statistical Engineering has a plural history. The public predecessor distinguishes Shewhart's statistical control, Dodge–Romig acceptance sampling, Fisherian experimental design, error-controlled and sequential decision theory, metrology, reliability and robust design. A later management programme such as Six Sigma reorganised inherited methods and organisational roles; it did not originate every method it used. Modern adaptive evaluation and dataset-shift research can converge on similar evidence problems without demonstrating direct descent from a particular industrial school. [Statistical Page][s-page] · [Public intake, direct-lineage boundary][s-intake]

Criticism sharpened the distinctions that matter. Precision can coexist with bias; repeatability can coexist with an invalid construct; a large collection can have a small independent-unit population; a control limit is different from an engineering requirement; and failure to find a statistically significant difference is different from establishing equivalence. The corpus retains useful inferential methods under their assumptions while rejecting universal thresholds, mandatory statistical ceremony and the idea that more green reruns automatically supply more truth. [Statistical ledger, P001–P013, P016–P019, P032, P036–P038 and P058–P065][s-ledger]

### Systems Safety

The safety history combines accident prevention, reliability and failure analysis, process-deviation methods, lifecycle hazard management, regulated-domain assurance, organisational research and control-oriented reformulations. The predecessor explains why failure-mode analysis, fault trees and process-deviation searches remain useful for their causal classes while control-oriented analysis addresses unsafe interactions, timing, feedback and process-model mismatch, including cases without a failed component. It does not establish that one technique replaces all others. [Safety Page][h-page] · [Safety source table][h-sources]

Later assurance and change-management work emphasised whether an argument still applies to the actual configuration and operation. A recognised standard establishes an in-scope practice; an accident exposes a possible mechanism; a controlled study supports a bounded comparison. These evidence types do not automatically yield a comparative effect size, complete hazard discovery or proof that a different system is safe. The public intake keeps domain assurance levels, certification artifacts and legal regimes separate from the more general loss–hazard–constraint–feedback mechanism. [Safety public intake, evidence limits and lineage distinctions][h-intake]

### How the convergence is used

A shared term is not sufficient evidence of either ancestry or equivalence. “Independence” can refer to experimental units, informative judgement or failure-separated barriers. “Feedback” can support coordination, measurement or control, each with different assumptions. CSS links the supported mechanisms while retaining their consumers, guards and limits. The synthesis should expose a real receiving relation—what one lane contributes to another's decision—rather than treating every pair of properties as interchangeable. [Historical CSS composition][css-page] · [Current composition boundary][current-css]
