## P001 — Define the decision-relevant measurand or construct before quantification

### Current status

STRONGLY_RETAINED

### Mature or evolved form

No metric governs until its intended property, scope, consumer, and admissible interpretation are fixed enough to be challenged.

### Mechanism

Bind each metric to a stated measurand/construct, unit of analysis, operating conditions, intended use, and disconfirming observations.

### Trigger or context

Any measurement used to accept, reject, optimise, compare, or monitor an engineering state.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Domain definition, decision owner, scope, and an explicit mapping from observations to the property of interest.

### Measurement preconditions

The operational definition must preserve the distinctions the decision actually requires; an evaluator must not collapse relevant states.

### Assumptions

The construct is sufficiently coherent for the intended decision and its operationalisation does not change materially across samples.

### Decision or consumer

Design authority, test owner, regulator, operator, or other consumer of the decision.

### Non trigger or cheap path

When an authoritative deterministic fact directly answers the decision, record that fact rather than inventing a surrogate score.

### Known failure modes

Ambiguous labels; changing definitions between baseline and candidate; composite scores whose weights hide trade-offs.

### Important criticisms

Metrology can define a measurand precisely while still leaving broader validity contested; formal latent constructs add model dependence.

### Contrary evidence

Construct-validity evidence is domain-dependent; no universal statistical test establishes that a metric means what stakeholders intend.

### Open questions

How should construct adequacy be stress-tested when the desired property is partly normative or changes with deployment context?

### Ceremony vs property

A template or ontology is optional; the property is the explicit decision-linked definition.

### Current assessment

**Bearer:** A proposed measurement result and decision

**Criterion:** Specify the intended property, scale, population and consequence; an operational score must preserve the distinctions the decision needs.

**Criterion type:** QUALITATIVE_CONDITIONAL_METHOD_CRITERION

**Quantifier and horizon:** For each application/claim within its explicitly declared population, decision and time horizon. This is not an assertion that every application already satisfies the criterion.

**Evaluation:** Read the criterion with all native applicability, measurement, variation and experiment profiles. Assess the actual claim and context; do not infer a numeric confidence from a status label.

**Discriminating witness:** A precisely reproducible count of the wrong construct fails; a clear direct physical quantity can pass with a short definition.

**Cheap path and reopening:** Use the simplest operational definition adequate to the decision; reopen on changed construct or consumer.

## P002 — Establish measurement-system fitness before metric governance

### Current status

MEASUREMENT_PRECONDITION_PROPERTY

### Mature or evolved form

Demonstrate enough discrimination for the intended decision, not abstract “good measurement,” and recheck after material evaluator change.

### Mechanism

Estimate bias, repeatability, reproducibility, resolution, stability and uncertainty relative to the decision margin; block use when discrimination is inadequate.

### Trigger or context

Metrics near thresholds, comparisons whose expected effect is small, multi-operator/evaluator systems, or costly decisions.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Defined measurand, representative items/conditions, reference or comparison basis, and a stated decision margin.

### Measurement preconditions

The study must span the real operating range and relevant operators, instruments, environments and preprocessing paths.

### Assumptions

Measurement error is reasonably estimable over the intended range and the study itself has not selected unusually easy samples.

### Decision or consumer

Anyone relying on a metric, threshold, trend or experiment outcome.

### Non trigger or cheap path

For a deterministic binary check with an authoritative oracle and negligible ambiguity, verify the oracle and use it directly rather than perform full MSA.

### Known failure modes

Narrow part range; nonrepresentative operators; stable repeatability but unknown bias; passing a generic GR&R percentage unrelated to the decision.

### Important criticisms

Gauge R&R is only one design; destructive, automated, subjective and probabilistic evaluators require other designs.

### Contrary evidence

No single GR&R threshold is universally optimal; measurement investment depends on decision loss and achievable resolution.

### Open questions

How should measurement fitness be quantified for adaptive, generative or partly subjective evaluators?

### Ceremony vs property

The form, software package, fixed 10×3×3 design or percentage cutoff is ceremony; decision-linked discrimination is the property.

### Current assessment

**Bearer:** A measurement process in a declared operating range

**Criterion:** Show that relevant bias, variability, resolution and interaction are adequate for the decision margin; chart calibration and latent-process interpretation are separate questions.

## P003 — Calibration and metrological traceability where decision-relevant

### Current status

MEASUREMENT_PRECONDITION_PROPERTY

### Mature or evolved form

Require the level of traceability needed for comparability and decision risk, alongside—not instead of—fitness validation.

### Mechanism

Use suitable standards/reference methods, record calibration state and uncertainty contributions, and maintain traceability at the result level.

### Trigger or context

Physical/chemical measurements, regulated tolerances, interlaboratory exchange, or any decision requiring comparability to a standard.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Valid calibration method, competent execution, suitable reference, environmental controls, and an uncertainty chain.

### Measurement preconditions

Calibration points and standards must cover the range, matrix and conditions of use.

### Assumptions

The reference hierarchy and calibration model remain applicable; no unmodelled transport or nonlinear effects dominate.

### Decision or consumer

Laboratory, manufacturer, customer, regulator and downstream analysts.

### Non trigger or cheap path

For purely ordinal/local decisions with a verified internal reference and no cross-context claim, full national/international traceability may add no value.

### Known failure modes

“NIST-traceable” asserted for an organisation; calibration certificate without uncertainty; traceability outside the working range.

### Important criticisms

NIST explicitly rejects treating traceability as a guarantee of fitness for purpose or absence of mistakes.

### Contrary evidence

Some digital/subjective measurements lack a meaningful SI chain; analogous provenance does not equal metrological traceability.

### Open questions

What is the minimum defensible reference chain for nonphysical evaluators and rapidly changing benchmarks?

### Ceremony vs property

A certificate, logo or fixed interval is not the property; the evidence chain and decision fitness are.

### Current assessment

**Bearer:** A specific measurement result

## P004 — Keep bias/accuracy distinct from precision

### Current status

MEASUREMENT_PRECONDITION_PROPERTY

### Mature or evolved form

Decision-specific decomposition of systematic and random error, with correction only when validated.

### Mechanism

Compare to suitable references/alternatives for bias while estimating dispersion under repeatability and reproducibility conditions.

### Trigger or context

Whenever magnitude or direction relative to truth/standard matters, especially near limits.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Reference value or defensible triangulation, representative operating range and stable calibration/evaluator state.

### Measurement preconditions

Enough resolution and coverage to detect both offset and dispersion; uncertainty in the reference included.

### Assumptions

Bias is reasonably stable or modelled over range/context; reference is more informative than the system under test.

### Decision or consumer

Measurement owner and decision consumer.

### Non trigger or cheap path

If the decision is invariant to a known constant offset and only ranks within one stable system, bias correction may be unnecessary—but must be explicit.

### Known failure modes

Using correlation as accuracy; reporting only standard deviation; reference standard shares the same bias.

### Important criticisms

The property is bounded by its stated assumptions and known failures: Using correlation as accuracy; reporting only standard deviation; reference standard shares the same bias. Contrary evidence/limit: For complex construct measures, “true value” may be unavailable; bias must then be bounded through convergent/criterion evidence rather than asserted.

### Contrary evidence

For complex construct measures, “true value” may be unavailable; bias must then be bounded through convergent/criterion evidence rather than asserted.

### Open questions

How can shared-method bias be detected when all available reference systems inherit common training data or assumptions?

### Ceremony vs property

Extra decimal places and repeated readings are ceremony when the dominant error is systematic.

### Current assessment

**Criterion:** Separate systematic error from repeat variation and from uncertainty about the reference; do not infer accuracy from a small repeat spread.

## P005 — Distinguish repeatability, reproducibility and validity

### Current status

MEASUREMENT_PRECONDITION_PROPERTY

### Mature or evolved form

Claim only the level of stability actually tested, and never infer validity from repeatability alone.

### Mechanism

Design crossed/nested comparisons over relevant operators, instruments, sites, times and methods; assess validity separately.

### Trigger or context

Multi-operator, multi-site, automated evaluator, stochastic test or claimed general result.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Representative factors and levels, randomisation/order control, clear agreement target and adequate item range.

### Measurement preconditions

Measurement outputs are comparable and evaluator versions are fixed/documented during the study.

### Assumptions

Factor effects and interactions can be estimated; repeated conditions approximate intended routine use.

### Decision or consumer

Measurement owner, experimenter and anyone claiming portability.

### Non trigger or cheap path

For one-use, one-instrument local control with no transfer claim and a wide margin, a limited stability check may suffice.

### Known failure modes

Confusing reproducibility with exact equality; narrow sample range; no validity criterion; treating reruns as independent item evidence without their actual design/dependence basis.

### Important criticisms

The property is bounded by its stated assumptions and known failures: Confusing reproducibility with exact equality; narrow sample range; no validity criterion; treating reruns as independent item evidence without their actual design/dependence basis. Contrary evidence/limit: Definitions of “reproducibility” vary across metrology and science; the factors changed must always be stated.

### Contrary evidence

Definitions of “reproducibility” vary across metrology and science; the factors changed must always be stated.

### Open questions

How should reproducibility be defined for non-deterministic systems whose output distribution, not individual output, is the object?

### Ceremony vs property

A standard GR&R worksheet is optional; the factor-appropriate variance/agreement design is the property.

### Current assessment

## P006 — Match resolution, sensitivity, specificity and threshold discrimination to the decision

### Current status

MEASUREMENT_PRECONDITION_PROPERTY

### Mature or evolved form

Use the cheapest discriminator whose error profile is adequate for the decision and freeze/version its threshold unless independent recalibration evidence exists.

### Mechanism

Characterise resolution and class-conditional error across the operating range; choose thresholds using consequence-weighted false-positive/false-negative risks.

### Trigger or context

Threshold decisions, defect detection, rare-event screens, small expected improvements, or changing class prevalence.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Stable labels/reference adjudication, representative prevalence/spectrum, stated loss asymmetry and threshold version.

### Measurement preconditions

Reference labels are sufficiently accurate and the validation set represents intended use; resolution is not inflated by preprocessing leakage.

### Assumptions

Future cases are drawn from a relevant population or shift is monitored; class definitions remain stable.

### Decision or consumer

Decision owner, safety/quality gate, monitoring operator.

### Non trigger or cheap path

When a deterministic authoritative predicate directly distinguishes states, use it instead of estimating sensitivity/specificity.

### Known failure modes

Selecting threshold on the same test set; ignoring prevalence; changing expected answers after failures; resolution below tolerance width.

The property is bounded by its stated assumptions and known failures: Selecting threshold on the same test set; ignoring prevalence; changing expected answers after failures; resolution below tolerance width. Contrary evidence/limit: ROC-like summaries may obscure calibration, prevalence shift and heterogeneous costs; no single threshold is universally optimal.

## P007 — Carry measurement uncertainty into engineering decisions

### Current status

MEASUREMENT_PRECONDITION_PROPERTY

### Mature or evolved form

Use proportionate uncertainty analysis sufficient to show whether the next action is discriminated.

### Mechanism

Build an uncertainty model/budget, propagate components and use intervals, guard bands or explicit loss-based rules.

### Trigger or context

Measurements close to specifications, comparisons with small effects, extrapolations, and regulated claims.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Defined measurand/model, traceable components where applicable, covariance handling, and a stated coverage/decision rule.

### Measurement preconditions

Inputs and correlations are characterised over the intended regime; software/preprocessing uncertainty included when material.

### Assumptions

The propagation model is adequate; uncertainty is not silently conditional on fixed but uncertain calibration/model choices.

### Decision or consumer

Conformance authority, designer, customer or risk owner.

### Non trigger or cheap path

When the result is far from every relevant boundary and a conservative worst-case bound suffices, a detailed budget may be unnecessary.

### Known failure modes

Double-counted components; omitted covariance; confidence interval mislabeled as tolerance interval; uncertainty reported without decision use.

### Important criticisms

The property is bounded by its stated assumptions and known failures: Double-counted components; omitted covariance; confidence interval mislabeled as tolerance interval; uncertainty reported without decision use. Contrary evidence/limit: GUM-style propagation can be strained by nonlinearity, asymmetric tails, model uncertainty and adaptive algorithms; simulation/Bayesian or interval methods may be needed.

### Contrary evidence

GUM-style propagation can be strained by nonlinearity, asymmetric tails, model uncertainty and adaptive algorithms; simulation/Bayesian or interval methods may be needed.

### Open questions

How should epistemic model uncertainty and evaluator retraining uncertainty be integrated without false numerical precision?

### Ceremony vs property

A long budget is ceremony if a dominant bound already resolves the decision; conversely, “±” without a model is decorative.

### Current assessment

## P008 — Evaluate inter-rater/evaluator agreement with a design matched to use

### Current status

MEASUREMENT_PRECONDITION_PROPERTY

### Mature or evolved form

Report disagreement structure and coefficient meaning. Withhold claims of stable evaluator-independent decisions when consequential disagreement is unresolved; an explicit adjudication or risk policy may still support action.

### Mechanism

Use paired/crossed items, blinded/randomised order, agreement-appropriate ICC/kappa/difference analyses, disagreement adjudication and subgroup checks.

### Trigger or context

Subjective ratings, manual inspection, model judging, multi-laboratory tests, or operator-dependent measurements.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Representative difficult/easy items, sufficient rater diversity, stable rubric, and a declared agreement target.

### Measurement preconditions

Raters/evaluators are independent enough to expose variation rather than copied or trained on identical answers.

### Assumptions

The selected agreement statistic matches fixed/random raters, absolute agreement/consistency and scale properties.

### Decision or consumer

Test owner, adjudicator, quality gate and downstream experimenter.

### Non trigger or cheap path

A deterministic reference rule with auditable outputs may need spot verification rather than a full agreement study.

### Known failure modes

Using Pearson correlation; prevalence-sensitive kappa without context; shared rubric bias; adjudicating after seeing candidate identity.

### Important criticisms

The property is bounded by its stated assumptions and known failures: Using Pearson correlation; prevalence-sensitive kappa without context; shared rubric bias; adjudicating after seeing candidate identity. Contrary evidence/limit: Agreement can be high because cases are homogeneous or decisions trivial; validity and representativeness remain separate.

Agreement can be high because cases are homogeneous or decisions trivial; validity and representativeness remain separate. Coefficient-specific qualification: homogeneity can inflate raw percent agreement while reducing or making undefined a chance-adjusted or variance-ratio coefficient; neither result alone establishes construct validity.

### Open questions

A fashionable reliability coefficient or arbitrary cutoff is not the property.

## P010 — Monitor evaluator/instrument drift and require independent recalibration evidence

### Current status

RETAINED_IN_EVOLVED_FORM

### Mature or evolved form

Recalibrate when independent evidence shows measurement defect or context change; preserve old/new bridge and re-evaluate prior conclusions where material.

### Mechanism

Use stable check/reference samples, bridge studies, version control, drift limits and independent change justification before recalibration.

### Trigger or context

Long-lived controls, automated scoring, model-based evaluators, changing laboratories or any metric with material maintenance.

### Dependencies or preconditions

Reference/check material stable enough to detect drift, bridge overlap between versions, and governance independent of candidate outcome.

### Measurement preconditions

Check samples represent important ranges and are protected from optimisation/contamination.

### Assumptions

Reference drift is slower or independently detectable; repeated checks are not all affected by the same environmental cause.

### Decision or consumer

Measurement owner, release/acceptance authority and audit consumer.

### Non trigger or cheap path

For a one-shot destructive test with contemporaneous references, ongoing drift monitoring may be impossible; preserve calibration evidence and uncertainty instead.

### Known failure modes

Rebaselining after alarms; editing expected answers after seeing failures; check-set overfitting; no version bridge.

### Important criticisms

The property is bounded by its stated assumptions and known failures: Rebaselining after alarms; editing expected answers after seeing failures; check-set overfitting; no version bridge. Contrary evidence/limit: Reference materials and holdouts can themselves age, leak or become unrepresentative; drift detection has no absolute anchor without external refresh.

### Contrary evidence

Reference materials and holdouts can themselves age, leak or become unrepresentative; drift detection has no absolute anchor without external refresh.

### Open questions

How should old conclusions be revisited after a material evaluator recalibration?

### Ceremony vs property

A calendar recalibration interval or automatic threshold refresh is not the property.

### Current assessment
