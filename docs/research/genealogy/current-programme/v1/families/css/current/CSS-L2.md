### 1. Documentary conclusion and boundaries

The accepted Statistical Engineering r3 subject preserves 65 historical and current property identities while making the evidence discipline more exact. ### 3. What the 65 properties mean

P001–P014 concern the measurement process before a result becomes evidence: the intended measurand or construct, fitness for the decision margin, calibration and traceability, accuracy and precision, repeatability and reproducibility, detection and thresholds, uncertainty, evaluator agreement, invariance, drift, reference material, data provenance, missingness and measurement-induced intervention. A precise number can measure the wrong construct. A calibration chain does not establish every downstream use. Agreement can coexist with shared bias, and a reference set can be unrepresentative or contaminated. The lightest adequate discriminator remains preferable when it truly covers the decision. [STAT-007](SOURCES.md#stat-007)

P015–P024 concern process evidence: distinguishing a signal from a causal explanation, rational subgrouping and dependence, specifications versus control limits, regime adequacy, conditional capability indices, avoiding tampering, monitoring economics, time structure, mixtures and specialized detectors. Monitoring observed measurements and inferring the latent process are different claims. A calibrated observed-stream chart may remain meaningful despite substantial measurement noise while losing power or failing to support a latent-process attribution. A signal alone does not identify a cause, responsible actor or appropriate permanent repair. [STAT-008](SOURCES.md#stat-008)

P025–P042 concern decision-relevant design and inference: sampling, lots and processes, information planning, estimands, randomization, blocking, replication, interactions, aliasing, sequential optimization, practical magnitude, multiplicity, stopping, interference, transport, model sensitivity, information value and direct deterministic discrimination. They require explicit populations, units, horizons and guarantees. A hierarchical model represents dependence; it cannot manufacture independently assigned experimental units. Randomization does not repair all measurement or transport failures. A Bayesian decision account does not automatically supply frequentist coverage. An authoritative complete deterministic discriminator can settle its exact claim, but validity alone cannot establish that it was the cheapest acquisition option. [STAT-009](SOURCES.md#stat-009)

P043–P057 preserve distinct burdens for robustness, lifetime and degradation, acceleration, reliability growth, zero-event bounds, tails, distribution shift, multidimensional outcomes, proxy gaming, evaluator change, candidate/evaluator separation, protected confirmation, model adequacy, integrative strategy and control retirement. An evaluator correction needs independent construct evidence or legitimate context revision and must preserve the old witness and affected conclusions. Mean performance, variability, tail behavior, reliability and causal transfer do not become one claim because they appear on the same dashboard. Retiring a quiet control requires attention to the rare failure it may still protect against. [STAT-010](SOURCES.md#stat-010)

The eight final rows, P058–P065, reject or condition universal significance thresholds, universal charts, branded defect-rate targets, mandatory DMAIC/DMADV, belt hierarchies, more-runs-as-more-truth, universal diagnostics and Taguchi recipes. They preserve narrower uses when assumptions, consumers and costs justify them. A fixed alpha can belong to a valid confirmatory design; an organizational framework can support coordination; an S/N ratio can match a particular loss relation. The adverse disposition concerns universal substitution of those forms for evidence, not a blanket assertion that they are always useless. [STAT-011](SOURCES.md#stat-011)

### 5. Constructed evidence, adverse alternatives and novelty

The nine earlier constructed cases and two r3 controls are finite analytical demonstrations. In the dependence control, 100 readings with covariance decaying as 0.5 to the separation have mean variance approximately 0.0296, compared with 1 for one reading and the invalid independent-reading value 0.01. A different shared-dependence structure gives 0.505, while perfect duplication provides no new observation. The construction shows why dependence cannot be reduced to either independent replication or useless repetition. [STAT-016](SOURCES.md#stat-016)

The information-value example has prior probabilities 0.6/0.4, utility one for a correct choice and baseline expected utility 0.6. A signal with accuracy 0.8 has gross value 0.2; a cost of 0.1 leaves net value 0.1 even though one outcome can leave the original action unchanged. An exact oracle has gross value 0.4, but cost 0.6 makes acquisition net negative, whereas cost 0.05 makes it net positive. After the relevant state is already known, further evidence has no decision value for that same settled claim. These stipulated utilities demonstrate the distinction; they do not price a real engineering decision. [STAT-017](SOURCES.md#stat-017)

Other cases preserve equally narrow conclusions. Correct calibration for an observed stream can retain a nominal false-alarm rate despite dominant measurement noise; a latent-process interpretation needs more. Fifty independent tests at 0.05 give a familywise false-positive probability about 0.9231, while the specified alpha-spending construction bounds its own family, not every repeatedly analyzed cumulative statistic. Zero failures in 100 independent Bernoulli trials leave a 95% upper bound near 0.02951; copying one trial does not supply 100 trials. A fractional design aliases a main effect with an interaction until a discriminating design separates them. An additive candidate/evaluator crossing decomposes the stipulated matrix only under its assumptions. Temporary containment can be justified by its own losses without identifying a cause. Raw agreement of 98% can coexist with negative kappa, and all-negative ratings can make kappa undefined. [STAT-018](SOURCES.md#stat-018)

The study models 22 relations, eight configurations, three higher-order interactions and three rejected novelty candidates. The configurations cover uncertainty-aware conformance, drift attribution, monitoring, causal experimentation, sequential adaptation, reliability, evaluator/proxy change and integrated strategy. Dependency-aware sequential evidence, an evaluator bridge and economically adequate evidence are useful combinations, but the novelty tests find subsumption or failed parent necessity. No new composition property is selected. The distinction prevents a conjunction of known requirements from masquerading as a newly validated method. [STAT-019](SOURCES.md#stat-019)
