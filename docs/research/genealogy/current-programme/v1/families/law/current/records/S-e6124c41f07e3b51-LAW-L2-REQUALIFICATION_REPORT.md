Two corrections are proposed for review. First, the original closure table labels a sequence from regression verification through integration and refactoring to release readiness and exposure a frozen partial order. ## 3. History without a single ancestry tree

Iteration predates the 2001 declaration. Basili and Turner’s 1975 account supplies a concrete software antecedent: an evolving working subset and revisable control list. It supports antecedence, not an assertion that every later Agile author learned from that paper. A history can therefore acknowledge shared problems and recurring mechanisms without inventing direct transmission. [N01: primary paper](https://www.cs.umd.edu/users/basili/publications/journals/J04.pdf).

The received early Scrum paper is the version updated on 5 April 1996, reached through the official Scrum.org locator. It explicitly includes planning and architecture before iterative development, and a closure phase. Beck’s 1999 XP account describes interdependent technical and collaborative practices and names several roots. These sources make a universal “no architecture first, architecture restored later” story untenable; the original packet’s more careful recognition of plural schools should govern its compressed diagrams. [H05: official locator](https://www.scrum.org/resources/scrum-development-process); [H06: primary paper](https://www.cs.kent.edu/~jmaletic/cs63902/Papers/Beck99.pdf).

DSDM, Crystal, FDD and ASD are not background synonyms for Scrum. The inspected DSDM handbook is the June 2014 edition despite its current download-directory date. Its foundations and planning sections supply a different governance emphasis. Cockburn’s author account describes a family tailored by scale and criticality. The accessible FDD publisher material describes modeling and feature-based development. The ASD excerpt and contents preserve speculation, collaboration, learning and planning together. Full monographs were not all available; these are bounded method characterizations, not independent effectiveness findings. [N02–N05](S-28e59cbf7ccd733e-LAW-L2-SOURCE_REGISTER.json).

Later developments also require separate relation types. The dated Continuous Delivery material names Manifesto inspiration and prior pipeline work. Debois’s 2008 post explicitly seeks to extend attention beyond development into operations. Sloss’s SRE origin account locates a Google production-team assignment in 2003; 2016 is the book’s publication date. Platform, discovery and product-mode proposals provide additional responses and possible combinations, but resemblance alone does not establish descent. [N16](https://continuousdelivery.com/2010/02/continuous-delivery/), [H16](https://jedi.be/blog/2008/agile-infrastructure-and-operations-how-infragile-are-you/), [N17](https://sre.google/sre-book/introduction/).

## 4. What the empirical evidence supports

The original warning about whole-method causality survives. The inspected Koch, Drazic and Schermuly meta-analysis has substantial design and outcome heterogeneity and expressly rejects a causal interpretation of its predominantly cross-sectional evidence. A positive association between reported Agile project management and an outcome does not identify which technical practice, decision right, organizational selection effect or surrounding capability produced it. [E03](https://bpspsychub.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/joop.12429).

The 2024 scaling comparison also supports a bounded conclusion: framework differences were small in practical magnitude on its measured team and stakeholder outcomes, after its specified controls. The design does not establish that frameworks are equivalent for financial or system-wide performance, that switching frameworks has no effect, or that dependency coordination is unnecessary. The original rejection of a universal scaling framework therefore remains reasonable without a stronger no-effect claim. [E05](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-024-10481-5).

Component evidence needs the same discipline. The TDD and pairing syntheses contain heterogeneous comparators, settings and trade-offs. They support questioning universal prescriptions; they do not refute every use of the practices. The newly accessed CI review was inspected at abstract level only. Its detailed study-level findings remain unverified in this refresh. [E08, E10, E11](S-28e59cbf7ccd733e-LAW-L2-SOURCE_REGISTER.json).

Source roles are made explicit throughout. A practice guide can define what its author means by a method. A standard can identify an applicable recommendation or obligation. A formal treatment can supply conditional identification reasoning. None becomes an independent empirical Agile trial merely by appearing in a source list headed “empirical.” The original report often already says this in prose; the successor carries those limits into individual claim and intake records. Native strength labels remain verbatim, including labels absent from the original short vocabulary table.

## 5. Adverse evidence remains inside the analysis

The criticism ledger preserves all 20 received criticisms and their response history. Newly inspected Meckenstock material supplies a systematic, adverse-focused account of pressure, customer problems, coordination and learning costs. Its search purpose and single-author coding prevent a population prevalence estimate or a calculation of Agile’s overall net benefit. Annosi’s full text remained inaccessible; only the reported abstract-level finding is used. The review includes that study, so the two are not counted as independent datasets. [N13: institutional version of record](https://extern.fis.uni-bamberg.de/files/54f9d3a9-1a72-4f44-bb76-99144980ca42/fisba105428.pdf); [N06](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/caim.12172).

Adversarial work also challenges attractive negative conclusions. McAvoy and Butler’s participant-observation cases did not support their initial expectation that Agile collective ownership would produce social loafing. Their small, involved-observer design does not prove a universal benefit either. The appropriate response is to examine actual control, incentives and interaction, not to assume that autonomy always helps or always harms. [N07](https://academic-publishing.org/index.php/ejise/article/download/289/252).

The original practitioner critics remain valuable testimony. Fowler’s technical-health criticism, Thomas’s objection to commercialization and Jeffries’s concern about imposed process identify mechanisms worth investigating. Their broad rhetoric and preferred remedies do not receive a higher evidence grade because the authors helped form the movement. Conversely, adverse implementations are not discarded by defining them as “not real Agile.” [C01, C03, C04](S-28e59cbf7ccd733e-LAW-L2-SOURCE_REGISTER.json); [CRITICISM.json](S-875e658a1ec5b9e3-LAW-L2-CRITICISM.json).

The inspected 2025 review acknowledges pilot-heavy and context-limited evidence, yet some of its concluding upfront-order language is categorical. That language is not promoted to a universal constraint here. It also identifies differing requirement granularity, organizational context and confidentiality limits. Those constraints prevent treating the reported numerical gains or certification claims as independently verified general results. The full 2026 publication was inaccessible, so preprint/publication equivalence is unconfirmed. ## 7. Composition, simpler configurations and novelty

The unit is not a checklist of 28 controls to install. [COMPOSITION.json](S-e9630d806934728b-LAW-L2-COMPOSITION.json) provides ten guarded relation records and six configurations: a small reversible change, an XP-style evolving product, Scrum-managed delivery with engineering controls, flow-oriented service work, assurance-constrained adaptive engineering, and a multi-team product/platform system. Every configuration permits justified omissions and identifies a failure mode and cost.

The cost example is deliberately constructed. If normalized cost per item is `100/q + q` for integer batch size `q ≥ 1`, batch sizes one, ten and twenty cost 101, 20 and 25 illustrative units respectively. The assumptions specify fixed setup plus growing waiting/risk cost. This defeats an unrestricted batch-of-one optimum; it is not a fitted model or a prediction for a real project.

Three composition-induced candidates were examined. A portfolio of feedback clocks is already covered by feedback selection and guarded relations. Authority-qualified adaptation refines existing autonomy/assurance guards. A proxy-normalization hazard is a measurement-validity problem, with new evidence rather than a new property identity. Parent-removal and subsumption tests are recorded. No distinct new property identity is admitted at this scope; there is no required novelty yield.

Cross-lineage synthesis is not performed in this lane. The other selected native units and independently examined directional interfaces are required before a material transfer or a three-way property can be qualified. No target system was inspected.
