# Lean requalification: conditional mechanisms, corrected evidence and bounded transfer

## Finding and scope

The original Lean study contains a useful conditional core. Its distinction between Toyota's historical production system, later Lean abstractions, actual deployments and tool conformity should be preserved. Its eight negative, superseded or ceremonial forms also survive this examination: rejecting zero inventory everywhere does not reject conditional replenishment; rejecting maximum utilization as a universal objective does not reject productive capacity use; and rejecting immutable procedures does not reject a useful baseline.

The material corrections concern the warrant for particular claims. Several bibliographic identities were wrong. A supply-chain URL pointed to an unrelated paper. A NUMMI source summary omitted the adverse ergonomic episode central to that study. A critical healthcare review used a pay-for-performance study as direct Lean evidence and labelled a percentage-point difference as a relative risk. Some property histories also implied direct descent or a field-wide evolution that the inspected sources do not establish. These are specific findings, not evidence that all neighboring claims are wrong. Their exact old/new values and affected rows are in [FIELD_CHANGES.json](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3e322d49c45d58af) and [ANCESTRY.json](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-bc51f6db1b4c31da).

## 1. What the genealogy establishes

### TPS was an institutional and technical development

Toyota's corporate history connects textile automatic stoppage to later jidoka and distinguishes Kiichiro's prewar JIT intentions from their interrupted and later realization. The same archive documents equipment reliability, production control, supervisory training, supplier arrangements and labor conflict. Those accounts establish Toyota's retrospective record; they do not independently demonstrate the human or economic effect of every practice. The 1977 Sugimori and colleagues paper supplies a participant exposition of an interacting system, including connecting stock, planning, adjustment and worker involvement. It is not a neutral population study of worker experience. [S001–S006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S013–S015](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

The sources do not support an uncomplicated heroic-inventor narrative. Kato's account describes selective use and replacement of supervisory-training materials, including Shingo's production-analysis teaching. That supports a material training contribution, without establishing that Shingo invented kanban, jidoka or the entire TPS. A precise chronology also remains contested: Toyota's education table lists Job Relations in 1951 and Job Instruction in 1952, whereas Kato recalls JI in December 1951, JM in June 1952 and JR in March 1953. The successor retains the conflict. [S008](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

National introduction is a separate event: the corporate footnote dates it to 1950, while Kato tentatively recalls an earlier date. The five-page interview does not explicitly date the beginning of Shingo's teaching to 1955. The timeline preserves the documented teaching/curriculum contribution without that unsupported year precision. [S008](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Documented influence must be stated at the mechanism actually evidenced. Toyota's supermarket account names a 1954 industry account of Lockheed's method; it does not establish the familiar personal grocery-store visit as the original trigger. Toyota's quality chronology explicitly records a Ford-inspired suggestion system in 1951. That narrow documentary import cannot substitute for a full account of every Ford-to-Toyota production-flow relationship. Statistical-quality training and TQC institutionalization support quality ancestry, not attribution of JIT or jidoka to Deming. [S003](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S010–S012](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Labor history belongs inside this account. The 1950 dispute, 1962 compact and 2001 Toyota Way are different events. Their sequence does not by itself prove a single causal progression toward universally humane work. A statement about participation or trust is a normative or institutional record; realized workload, autonomy, bargaining power and health require other evidence. [S013–S015](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S026](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S031](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

### Lean production and Lean Thinking changed the object of explanation

Krafcik's 1988 study helps explain why an external comparative category became attractive. Its analysis involved volume automobile plants and particular productivity and quality measures. The source's plant selection, standardized labor comparison and limited owner-reported quality measure constrain the conclusion. Nonrandom cross-plant differences and a management index partly entangled with operating characteristics do not identify a universal best organizational system. The original packet already recognized criticism of universal superiority; this successor makes the measurement and access ceilings explicit. [S016](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S019](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S024](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Lean Thinking's value, value-stream, flow, pull and perfection formulation is a later enterprise abstraction. The current Lean Enterprise Institute explanation explicitly attributes that formulation and the later Purpose/Process/People framing. It is a distinct accessed work, R008, rather than full access to the 1996 or revised monograph. The broader abstraction can help ask new questions, but it underdetermines concrete choices about buffer size, labor institutions, assurance, irreversibility and competing beneficiaries. [S018](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S023](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [R008](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

### Descendants and adaptations are plural

Product-development interpretations focus on coupled design constraints, integration and preserving feasible alternatives. That differs from reducing lots of interchangeable manufactured items. Poppendieck's 2003 introduction explicitly translates selected principles into software while distinguishing contextual practices. Its original seven terms must not be silently replaced by later formulations. Anderson's later account explicitly describes WIP-limited evolutionary change and says the 2010 book's five core properties were subsequently expanded. Current practice lists are therefore not automatically the original edition. [S050–S053](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [R007](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Construction offers a further documented adaptation. Ballard's 2000 thesis explicitly describes TPS/Lean and Koskela influences on a shift toward work-flow reliability. Its assignment model distinguishes what is desired from what can actually be undertaken and committed. The author was also a developer, participant and data collector; an interrupted design case and unanswered downstream-benefit questions constrain the empirical reading. Koskela's 1992 report was identified through Stanford's catalogue but not obtained in full. [R010](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [R011](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Lean Startup is another practitioner translation. The publisher identifies Lean-manufacturing inspiration, while the current official methodology emphasizes product/business-model learning and pivot decisions. That establishes a stated adaptation, not a causal success guarantee. Its use of scientific language or Five Whys does not remove the same concerns about weak causal stories, proxy measures and limited feedback samples. A separate comprehensive startup-effectiveness review was not performed here, and no such branch-wide effect is claimed. [R012](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [R013](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S046–S048](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Modern software telemetry, types, feature flags, circuit breakers, error budgets and recovery engineering can address related functions. The inspected evidence does not establish that each directly descends from a Toyota mechanism. The selected property histories now use translation, convergence or analogy where that is the available warrant. The typed [genealogy](S-f7d8088094b8f0cc-LAW-L1-GENEALOGY.json) contains both supported edges and investigated candidates that were not promoted to a transmission claim.

## 2. Flow, batches and buffers: what survives the counterexamples

An explicit WIP bound can control active commitments. It does not automatically reduce all waiting or improve worker wellbeing. Hopp and Spearman's tactical definition treats pull as an explicit WIP limit and distinguishes that question from make-to-order versus forecast-driven production. Sugimori's earlier account also combines short-term replenishment with advance planning and stock. Neither source supports the caricature of reacting blindly to the latest request with zero inventory. [S006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S032](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

The hidden-queue example C01 makes the boundary problem concrete. Five jobs arrive together; one server takes one time unit per job. If all five enter the visible workflow, average time from entry to completion is three. If admission is delayed until service begins, the visible cycle time becomes one, but average pre-entry waiting becomes two. Completion times and total mean lead time remain unchanged. The bound can still be useful for a different reason; the local metric alone cannot establish a system improvement. This is a finite deterministic construction, not a field replication or a proof of a general queueing law. [C01](S-65315ac8ce6c7c9e-LAW-L1-CASE_RESULTS.json), [EL-002](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd), [EL-004](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd).

C02 supplies the reverse control. A deterministic server receiving one unit exactly when it is free can run at full utilization without a queue. The same utilization with paired arrivals produces waiting. This defeats both a universal full-utilization objective and a universal claim that any full utilization is necessarily harmful. Arrival variation, service variation, blocking, failure recovery and the chosen boundary matter. C03 similarly gives a batch-size trade-off: for twelve units with setup time five and processing time one, batches of one take seventy-two total time units, while batches of six take twenty-two; the smaller batch provides earlier first-batch feedback. With zero setup the completion-time penalty disappears. [C02–C03](S-65315ac8ce6c7c9e-LAW-L1-CASE_RESULTS.json), [EL-005](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd), [EL-009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd).

The buffer correction is substantive. In the inspected operations-science account, physical variability is absorbed by inventory, capacity or time. Information can improve decisions, reveal a shortage or enable a better response; it is not automatically an interchangeable physical reserve. An alternate design or supplier matters only if it can actually supply the needed capability within the relevant conditions. This distinction preserves the usefulness of information without pretending a map manufactures missing parts. [S032](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [EL-008](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd).

The supply-chain evidence also resists a single slogan. Toyota's replacement RESCUE source reports information on around 6,800 items, not sites. Fujimoto's correctly identified 2011 paper proposes virtual dualization; it is not the unrelated 2019 RIETI paper at the original URL, and the inherited claim about more than 10,000 mapped sites is suspended. Ye, Suleiman and Huo's observational survey has differing supplier, customer and internal disruption paths; those cannot be collapsed into a universal net JIT effect. Choi and colleagues present a contingent conceptual response, not a causal experiment. [R002](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [R009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S039](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S040](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

The selected configuration therefore combines release, capacity and buffer choices with defined service classes and consequences. It may preserve stock, use spare capacity, accept delay or qualify alternatives. It also evaluates who bears the burden. In C05 a buyer saves 100 cost units while a supplier incurs 140 and customers 30: the local improvement raises total cost by 70. A different allocation can preserve a buyer saving and reduce total cost. These illustrative arithmetic cases do not turn worker rights or safety constraints into freely tradable quantities. [K01 and K04](S-785ece8aee402aea-LAW-L1-COMPOSITION.json), [C05](S-65315ac8ce6c7c9e-LAW-L1-CASE_RESULTS.json).

## 3. Quality, standard work and the social conditions of learning

Detection, acknowledgement, containment, restoration and prevention are different achievements. A detector can report accurately while no responder has time or authority to act. A stopped line does not establish the cause. A qualified alternative can restore a service while the affected process remains constrained; permanent diagnosis and countermeasure testing may proceed in parallel. The successor retains the original useful distinctions but makes the model an activity configuration rather than a mandatory serial lifecycle. [S005](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S046](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [K02](S-785ece8aee402aea-LAW-L1-COMPOSITION.json).

C04 shows why identical signals need not entail identical global stops. With explicit hypothetical detector assumptions, the same positive signal can justify bounded containment for a high-consequence defect and a cheaper check for a benign one. The calculation does not authorize an action: authority, safe-state constraints, uncertain losses and unmodelled consequences remain separate premises. A reliable local interlock may be simpler than an elaborate escalation system. Conversely, a display without acknowledgement is not an adequate substitute for response. [C04](S-65315ac8ce6c7c9e-LAW-L1-CASE_RESULTS.json), [EL-011–EL-015](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd).

NUMMI illustrates why this cannot be treated as an exclusively technical control problem. The source S031 concerns ergonomic deficiencies during the 1993 model introduction and responses involving workers, management and regulators. Its central adverse episode was missing from the original favorable-only summary. The related R001 manuscript belongs to the same research programme, so it strengthens access to the account without becoming an independent replication. Involvement and productive performance did not erase divergent interests. [S031](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [R001](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

A useful standard can make a method teachable and comparable. The current selection does not turn that into immutable compliance or mandatory paperwork. Explicit version, exception, review and retirement arrangements are analytical safeguards whose complexity depends on consequence and change rate; they are not all established as universal historical TPS practices. C07 preserves a stable applicable baseline, admits a properly authorized exception, escalates an uncovered scope gap and reopens a method when relevant conditions change. No rewrite is required merely because a method is old. [S008–S009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S026](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [C07](S-65315ac8ce6c7c9e-LAW-L1-CASE_RESULTS.json), [K03](S-785ece8aee402aea-LAW-L1-COMPOSITION.json).

The same restraint applies to improvement formats. Toyota's chronology records removal of companywide suggestion-count targets in 1982, but that does not make every activity count useless. Counts can describe workload without proving improvement. The PDSA review S047 examined reporting and fidelity; it did not test whether fuller reported fidelity caused better outcomes. Peerally and colleagues criticize linear causal narratives, weak controls and authority gaps. Reed and Card defend tailored experimental learning while rejecting oversimplification. These sources support a demand for appropriate reasoning and evidence, not a theorem that one template is always best. [S011](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S046–S048](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Worker evidence is heterogeneous, and that heterogeneity must remain visible. Parker's longitudinal study used a nonequivalent comparison and self-reported outcomes; its adverse findings cannot be dismissed by declaring the implementation inauthentic. Huo, Boxall and Cheung's two-wave study is a different source, not a Parker coauthored replication. R003 offers a favorable retrospective managerial learning case with a different perspective. The later R004 construction survey reports mixed strain/safety associations; its proposed compensatory-vigilance explanation is not an observed causal mechanism and does not justify work intensification. [S026](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S070](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [R003–R004](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Accordingly, safe voice remains a normative and socio-technical candidate with an evidence burden. C06 separates a channel for reporting from acknowledgement, bounded decision rights and realized protection. Unknown protection remains unknown. It is neither inferred from a policy nor forced into a historical Boolean predicate. The selected responsibility model also avoids assigning an operator a structural problem that only management, a supplier or another authority can change. [EL-021–EL-024](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd), [C06](S-65315ac8ce6c7c9e-LAW-L1-CASE_RESULTS.json).

The same epistemic distinction applies to the timeline, evolution, hybridization and intake summaries: specific work-design and support mechanisms can be investigated causally, while employment security and fair distribution also raise normative and institutional questions. Their importance does not establish that the entire package is a universally necessary cause of successful improvement. [S026](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S031](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [S070](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

## 4. Outcome claims must match exposure, comparator and measure

The healthcare source audit is a useful test of even-handed criticism. S060's largest mortality example is the Premier pay-for-performance comparison by Jha and colleagues, rather than a study directly identifying Lean exposure. The primary estimate is a 0.08 percentage-point mortality difference with an interval spanning zero; the review presents it as a relative risk. That mismatch prevents using this particular study as direct evidence of a Lean mortality null. It does not invalidate every other finding in the review. [S060](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3), [R005](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

R006 is an actual Lean-programme comparison in Ontario emergency departments. Its before-after improvements were substantially attenuated against concurrent controls, with heterogeneous results by outcome and wave. Voluntary selection, other system-wide initiatives and some Lean activity among controls limit attribution. The study did not evaluate frontline-staff outcomes. Its lesson for this corpus is to specify the exposure and comparator, rather than converting either favorable before-after changes or an attenuated comparison into a universal verdict. [R006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

Manufacturing meta-analysis, healthcare reviews and software studies also differ in populations and outcomes. They cannot be pooled narratively as interchangeable independent confirmations of an entire management package. Reviews may reuse the same primary studies. A source describing current DORA metrics is programme guidance, not itself the underlying dataset or a Lean-specific causal trial. The current guide's service/application context and SPACE's multidimensional perspective constrain individual-productivity inferences. [S058–S062](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be71e69b76928a3).

This supports a narrower but useful selection: a process measure can inform an outcome hypothesis, while the outcome remains to be demonstrated. The beneficiary boundary, time horizon, worker and supplier effects, co-interventions and durability belong in that account. A well-conducted inquiry can rationally end in no change, retirement or a cheaper existing mechanism. [EL-001](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd), [EL-025](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd), [EL-032](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-e477c2d583d23afd), [K07–K08](S-785ece8aee402aea-LAW-L1-COMPOSITION.json).

## 5. Composition adds constraints and alternatives

The eight configurations in [COMPOSITION.json](S-785ece8aee402aea-LAW-L1-COMPOSITION.json) examine actual interfaces: release control with capacity and buffers; abnormality detection with authority and recovery; baselines with learning and voice; supplier capability with risk allocation; design options with cross-functional constraints; software flow with qualification and recovery; local measures with beneficiary outcomes; and the choice of a simpler adequate control.

Their reverse directions are evaluated separately. Capacity analysis can change a WIP limit without proving demand value. Response overload can justify changing an alert threshold without proving the underlying abnormality safe. An outcome constraint can veto a local efficiency measure without proving the preferred intervention causes benefit. A constraint from another discipline can eliminate a design option without validating every surviving alternative. These are bounded dependencies, not whole-property equivalences.

Software exposure supplies a further distinction. With enforced deployment scope, independent instances and no cross-instance propagation, sending the same faulty change to one instance instead of one hundred reduces the directly exposed population even when both qualification and recovery are absent. That partial effect says nothing by itself about acceptable loss, duration, authorization or net benefit. C13 reproduces the reviewer's RV01 witness on that exact measure. C14 then adds scoped qualification and functioning recovery: a known fault is rejected, an escaped fault is contained and restored within stipulated model limits, and failed restoration shows why a one-instance bound does not control duration. Neither case establishes real-world adequacy. [K06](S-785ece8aee402aea-LAW-L1-COMPOSITION.json), [C13–C14](S-65315ac8ce6c7c9e-LAW-L1-CASE_RESULTS.json).

Two candidate induced criteria were examined. C08's feasible-design intersection needs both discipline constraints in its witness, but is already contained in EL-029/EL-030. C12 demonstrates that controls with overlapping benefits can be jointly uneconomic even when each is individually useful; that is already addressed by improvement economics and whole-system evaluation. No new property identity is admitted. That result follows from subsumption and removal checks, not a target of zero novelty. The finite examples do not establish empirical superiority of the composite configuration.

Cheap paths are substantive. An already bounded one-at-a-time workflow may not need a board. A stable low-risk method may need only a shared instruction and a change trigger. A small reversible correction may use direct inspection and a known revert path. The relevant failure still has to be covered; merely calling work small does not discharge its consequences. [C11–C12](S-65315ac8ce6c7c9e-LAW-L1-CASE_RESULTS.json), [K08](S-785ece8aee402aea-LAW-L1-COMPOSITION.json).
