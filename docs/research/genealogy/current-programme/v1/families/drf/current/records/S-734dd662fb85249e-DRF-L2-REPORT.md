## 2. GENEALOGY — transmission, shared mathematics and analogy

At least nine strands are needed to avoid a false single staircase. Life-data statistics asks how lifetimes or failures vary under exposure. Military/electronics programmes organize requirements, prediction, testing and feedback. Mechanical maintenance asks whether an intervention restores condition or avoids a consequence. Repairable-systems statistics models recurring events and, in some formulations, the effect of repair. Failure analysis connects modes and interactions to an undesired event. Fault-tolerant computing structures detection, alternative execution and recovery. Software reliability relates fault activation to use. Diagnostics and PHM infer condition or impending loss from signals. Service reliability translates function, interruption and repair into continuing consumer operations. They share problems and sometimes mathematics, but their bearers and evidence are not interchangeable.

The strongest fresh transmission evidence is specific. Randell's 1975 recovery-block account cites the earlier 1974 work and contrasts its recovery semantics with Davies's prior approach; it also proposes extensions and identifies restrictions needed for interacting processes. NASA's 2008 guide documents later uptake of Nowlan–Heap's maintenance-centered reasoning. The FTA survey explicitly links its exposition to earlier fault-tree work and the handbook; its own formal definitions expose the assumptions of one representation. These are directed documentary edges. None proves that all modern service practice descended from a single one of those documents. [[F043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F023](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F045](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

The less certain links remain typed accordingly. The same conditional-probability identity can appear in physical redundancy and software diversity without proving direct historical borrowing. A retry loop resembles a maintenance intervention in that both change a failure trajectory, but code does not physically wear and a restart is not automatically an as-good-as-new replacement. Recovery-oriented computing and site reliability practice share an interest in restoration and operational cost; direct transmission to a particular later SRE practice is unresolved unless the actual source supplies it. The acronym SRE is itself ambiguous between software reliability engineering and site reliability engineering. Similar initials are not ancestry.

A criticism can redirect a strand without destroying it. Knight–Leveson's dependent failures challenge an independence assumption in multiversion claims; they do not prove that no diverse pair can ever rescue a failure. Reliability-growth criticism limits forecasting and causal inference without eliminating conditional planning or assessment. The FMECA standard's own foreword shows an internal criticism of late, isolated analysis, so tailoring is not solely a later invention. The genealogy register distinguishes documentary uptake, documented criticism, shared mathematical ancestry, domain translation, convergence/analogy and unresolved connection; it never uses an untyped arrow as proof of causation. ## 3. RELIABILITY_ENGINEERING_VS_MTBF_FMEA_SRE_CARICATURE

Three caricatures obscure the discipline. The first equates reliability with a mean time between failures. The second equates it with completed analysis forms. The third treats contemporary service branding as the mature destination of all earlier engineering. Each mistakes a measure or implementation for the property that matters. A mean may answer a useful question, a worksheet may organize a valuable challenge, and an SLO may coordinate a legitimate decision. Their adequacy depends on the required function, assumptions, observations and consumer.

The opposite caricature is equally unhelpful: because a measure is imperfect, all metrics are meaningless; because a model is conditional, it has no value; because a process can become bureaucracy, all structured analysis should be removed. The valid middle is specific. A model can establish exactly what follows from its premises. A direct test can settle a local predicate. A historical programme can have both useful engineering content and poor implementations. The current record therefore retains valid bounded alternatives alongside every rejected universal inference in P041–P055.

For example, the 1980 FMECA text already connected its work to design decisions and expressly allowed a reasoned no-action disposition. Google's example error-budget policy is visibly a policy with exceptions and decision roles. Neither document makes its form an empirical theorem. The scholarly task is to preserve the mechanism and identify the context in which it earns its cost, not to replace old ceremony with a more elaborate current ceremony. ## 5. MISSION_FAILURE_MODEL

Let a claim concern item or service X, consumer C, environment/profile E and a mission interval or demand D. A success predicate S(X,C,E,D) might be binary, phased, multistate or partly qualitative. Defining S is not yet measuring reliability. Within a probability model, reliability can then be an expression such as P(S holds through the mission). In a real system, the burden is to establish that the observed event and population correspond to the function the consumer requires. A formally precise predicate for the wrong function is not a useful engineering answer.

A local function can be legitimate. An internal restore can re-establish consistent state without re-establishing the consumer's entire transaction. That does not make the local claim false; it makes the conjunction explicit. [C04](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) sets internal byte/format/application conditions true and credential/counterparty conditions false. The internal result is true and the wider result false. This distinction prevents both overclaim and unwarranted rejection. It also directs the next useful question: which remaining premise blocks the wider function?

Failure states should be as rich as the decision needs. Complete loss, delayed output, wrong output, intermittent loss, latent impairment and accepted degradation may produce different actions. A binary indicator remains adequate when the actual function is binary and directly observed. More categories can create false precision or maintenance burden. The test is whether a distinction changes a warranted interpretation or response, not whether the state machine appears sophisticated.

Operational profiles add another dimension. If conditional failure probabilities are p_i and use weights are w_i, then p = Σw_i p_i under the stated partition. This identity does not say how weights should be chosen ethically or contractually, or whether common uses deserve all testing resources. A rare but consequential failure may need a separate constraint or bounding case. [C09](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) demonstrates that a million-request aggregate can meet its objective while every request in a small group fails. The arithmetic exposes the limitation; it does not decide whose loss is acceptable. Primary source definitions and later practice support these distinctions, but no paper supplies the missing authority for a new target. [[F006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F046](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F024](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

## 6. RELIABILITY_AVAILABILITY_MAINTAINABILITY_MODEL

For a nonrepairable item, a common reliability function is R(t)=P(T>t), with a stated convention for equality and the failure event. Point availability is P(the required function is available at time t). Observed interval availability is the fraction of a declared interval in the up state. Maintainability concerns the ability, or a conditional distribution of time, to restore the function under stated maintenance conditions. Supportability concerns the resources that make such maintenance feasible. These quantities can move in different directions. Fast repair can increase availability while leaving mission interruption frequency unacceptable; excellent survival can coexist with an extremely long outage when failure finally occurs. [[F006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F007](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

The familiar long-run availability ratio has a broader valid domain than a two-state exponential model. Consider regenerative cycles (U_i,D_i), where U is up time and D is down time, cycles are identically distributed and independent across cycles, and 0<E[U]+E[D]<∞. At cycle boundaries, the fraction of time up is ΣU_i / Σ(U_i+D_i). Applying the strong law separately to numerator and denominator gives E[U]/(E[U]+E[D]); the ordinary finite-mean regenerative extension makes the unfinished-cycle contribution negligible in the long run. For completeness, write C_i=U_i+D_i. Finite E[C] gives C_n/n→0 almost surely by the tail-sum bound and Borel–Cantelli, while the strong law gives S_n/n→E[C]>0. Thus the cycle count N(t)/t→1/E[C], and the unfinished contribution, bounded by C_{N(t)+1}/t, tends to zero. These statements concern a long-run time fraction; they do not by themselves prove convergence of point availability. Dependence within a cycle need not invalidate the ratio. This is the author's explicit conditional derivation, not an assertion that any field process satisfies regeneration.

Definitions of MTBF must be checked before inserting them into that formula. If MTBF means operating up time between failures and MTTR means the relevant down time, their ratio form may match it. If “between failures” means a full calendar cycle including downtime, adding repair time again is wrong. If MTTR covers only hands-on repair while diagnosis, access, spares or validation dominate, the operational denominator is incomplete. These are semantic and observation problems before they are estimation problems. Overlapping activities also need an interval union or a critical-path account: a spare delivered while diagnosis proceeds should not automatically be counted twice.

Exponential failure/repair assumptions produce a convenient continuous-time Markov model, but they are not prerequisites for every finite mean. Conversely, a nonrenewal system with imperfect repair, changing workload, shared repair resources or aging can fail the regenerative argument. Infinite means or persistent nonstationarity can also defeat a simple limiting ratio. A correct formula with unsupported input correspondence is a strong model and a weak field claim, not a contradiction. Lindqvist's freshly read repairable-systems passages explicitly exclude repair durations from their scope; they are not misused here as a direct proof of operational availability. [[F013](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

[C01](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) supplies a cheap discriminator. In a 1,000-hour interval, 100 disjoint 0.1-hour interruptions and one 10-hour interruption both yield 99% observed availability. Their frequency and longest interruption differ. No event probability distribution or consumer tolerance was inferred. A mission that cannot tolerate any interruption, or one that can tolerate many short interruptions but not a long one, needs a different receiving criterion. A small set of well-defined measures is preferable to either one prestigious scalar or an indiscriminate dashboard.

## 7. FAILURE_PROPAGATION_MODEL

FMEA/FMECA examines possible modes and their local, next-level and end effects; a fault tree begins with a chosen undesired event and represents combinations that lead to it. Event-tree or state-transition reasoning can be useful when sequence and response matter. These are different views of a system, not automatic rivals. A bottom-up list may omit interactions; a top-down tree may choose the wrong top event or omit a common cause. Quantitative evaluation adds another burden: the joint event law must justify the calculation. [[F046](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F045](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

A simple Boolean example clarifies the levels of evidence. Suppose top failure is A OR (B AND C). Within that model, {A} and {B,C} are minimal cut sets. Enumerating them does not establish that the real system has no other failure route. Nor does it justify multiplying P(B)P(C) unless the relevant events are independent or another valid model supplies that joint probability. Repeating the same physical event in two branches does not make it two independent trials. The reviewed FTA exposition explicitly makes its independent-basic-event assumption and discusses repeated-event dependence; an omitted external common cause still requires engineering investigation. [[F045](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Ordinal criticality scores create a different problem. If severity categories are merely ordered, multiplying them by occurrence and detection ranks assumes arithmetic meaning that the ordinal scale may not have. Relabelling an order-preserving severity scale can change products and rankings. That is an analytical warning about the operation, not a claim that every severity prioritization is useless. A declared decision rule, transparent consequence categories and a sensitivity check can remain useful without pretending the product is a measured risk probability.

Propagation analysis earns its cost when it informs a concrete choice: remove a failure route, detect it, contain it, restore a bounded function, or accept the residual with appropriate authority. The outcome can also be no change because an existing control is adequate or the proposed analysis adds no useful discrimination. A short path review may suffice for a simple reversible operation. More formal analysis is warranted when interactions, consequences or binding requirements demand it. The historical FMECA source already treated decisions, timeliness and shared analysis effort as central, so current anti-ceremony reasoning is partly a recovery of that purpose. [[F046](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

## 8. LIFE_AND_REPAIRABLE_SYSTEM_MODEL

A lifetime distribution describes the time to a defined failure in a population. Its survival, density and hazard are related but distinct. Where differentiability holds, h(t)=f(t)/R(t). A decreasing survival curve does not imply an increasing hazard. The Weibull family illustrates how one model can express decreasing, constant or increasing hazard through its shape parameter; that flexibility is not evidence that it fits a particular mechanism. Mixtures, covariates and selection can further change apparent patterns. NIST's accessible model-choice discussion treats physical understanding and empirical fit as separate considerations. [[F001](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F002](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F012](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Repairable systems introduce recurrence. A renewal model uses successive life-like intervals under a renewal assumption. Minimal repair can leave the effective age unchanged and motivate a different recurrent-event process. An NHPP specifies Poisson increments with an intensity that can change over time; its independent-increment property is more than a curve-fitting convenience. Software activation, intermittent states and service incidents may need other models. The word “rate” cannot erase the distinction between first-life hazard, rate of occurrence of failures, failures per demand and a reported-incident count. [[F013](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F037](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Censoring is not the same as a zero lifetime or a record to discard. Under independent censoring in the exponential illustration, two failures after 5 and 10 hours plus a third item's 12-hour censored observation provide 27 total hours, giving rate estimate 2/27. Discarding the censored exposure yields 2/15 and changes the inference. Informative withdrawal—such as removing visibly degraded units—requires a different observation model. Left truncation, unreported failures and conditional warranty returns can also alter the sampled population. [C06](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) demonstrates only the stated exponential calculation; it does not solve those observation problems.

The limits of model fit deserve emphasis. A visually plausible distribution does not identify a physical cause, and a well-estimated parameter can be precisely wrong for another population. [C13](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) makes the mean/process distinction exact: with one U uniform on [0,1), define N(t)=floor(√t+U). For x=n+r, floor(x+U) is n or n+1 with probabilities 1−r and r, so E[N(t)]=√t. Yet N(4)=2 with variance zero, whereas an NHPP with mean two has variance two. Thus even an exact power-law mean does not establish an NHPP. An approximately straight Duane plot supplies less, although it may remain a useful model diagnostic. This qualifies an informal equivalence in the NIST page without discarding its conditional modeling purpose. [[F039](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a); [C13](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475)]

Uncertainty should therefore be partitioned. Parameter uncertainty concerns an unknown value inside a chosen model; model discrepancy concerns whether the model represents the process; transfer uncertainty concerns a new population/configuration; observation uncertainty concerns what was counted. More samples can reduce one while leaving the others untouched. A conservative bound or direct engineering check can sometimes be more useful than another fitted parameter.

## 9. REDUNDANCY_COMMON_CAUSE_MODEL

Let A and B be success events for two paths in an ideal OR architecture, with no new interactions or control failures. Then P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B∩¬A). The increment over A alone is nonnegative, and is strictly positive exactly when B succeeds on a positive-probability set where A fails. Independence is unnecessary for that identity. It is needed to reduce the joint failure term to P(¬A)P(¬B) in the familiar product formula. Physical separation, different code and different teams can change mechanisms, but they do not logically establish statistical independence. [[F003](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F010](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a); author's derivation]

[C02](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) stipulates joint probabilities: both up .89, only A up .01, only B up .01 and both down .09. Each path has success .90; the pair has .91. Positive dependence is strong, yet the .01 rescue increment is real in the model. The independence shortcut predicts .99 and is wrong for that joint law. Perfectly coincident failures would yield no increment. If an added serial controller independently succeeds only .95 of the time, the stipulated combined architecture succeeds .95×.91=.8645, worse than the original .90. The example distinguishes dependence reducing a benefit from new mechanisms reversing it.

Real redundant systems need more than an OR diagram. Standby paths can have dormant faults, inadequate capacity, inaccessible state or a failed transfer mechanism. Voting systems depend on the fault/adjudication model. A shared power source, requirements error, release pipeline, control plane or maintenance action can dominate nominal isolation. Common-cause analysis should describe both what is shared and what evidence supports separation. Treating all copies as one domain may be a prudent conservative decision where uncertainty and consequence demand it, but it is not an observed fact merely because independence was not proven. Bounds and unresolved alternatives must remain visible.

The Knight–Leveson experiment is consequential because it tests a particular independence expectation in multiversion software. Its 27 versions and large common input set belong to one application experiment, not 27 independent replications across domains. The reference implementation and test design also have limitations. Its dependent failures refute a naive independence claim in that setting, not all useful diversity. Randell's alternate-block approach, von Neumann's formal constructions and contemporary redundant infrastructure answer different questions under different assumptions. [[F010](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F014](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

The decision is therefore comparative. A second channel might dominate, or one simpler component with competent repair might be better. A manual spare can be adequate when restoration time is tolerable. Additional isolation can save one failure domain while introducing state synchronization, operational complexity, energy and diagnosis costs. Neither replica count nor a blanket anti-redundancy reaction is a scientific conclusion. The current P009 clarification preserves that conditional choice and the real benefit possible under dependence.

## 10. DIAGNOSTIC_COVERAGE_MODEL

Detection asks whether a target fault/failure was signalled. Isolation asks how narrowly its origin or affected region was identified. Diagnosis concerns a supported explanation or ambiguity set. Containment and restoration concern subsequent effects. These quantities have different denominators and different success criteria. A detector can supply useful information with no complete diagnosis; a restoration mechanism can work without identifying the original cause. Dependability taxonomy and the primary recovery-block account support that separation. [[F009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

In [C03](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475), a stipulated population has 100 fault opportunities and 9,900 healthy opportunities. The detector signals 60 faults and produces 99 false alarms. Detection coverage is .60, false-alarm rate .01 and positive predictive value 60/159, about .377. Exact isolation occurs for 20, restoration for 15, and containment without restoration for another 30. Isolation conditional on detection is 20/60; restoration conditional on detection is 15/60. None is the other merely because a profile uses the word coverage. The counts and subset relations are assumptions of the example, not field estimates.

Action changes the evaluation. With invented benefit 100 per restored fault and cost 20 per unnecessary automatic action, the selected automatic policy yields 1,500−1,980=−480. A stipulated perfectly confirming inspection costing one per alarm yields 1,500−159=1,341. These illustrative net values omit other costs and the potential value of containment; confirmation accuracy and capacity are explicit premises. The point is that signal value, automatic action value and full restoration are separable. A useful partial signal need not be rejected because an unsafe automatic policy is harmful. [[C03](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475); [F021](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

The native P012 profile is already substantially aligned with this view: it preserves ambiguity groups and permits a shared remedy when exact cause does not change the response. Its shared `RECOVERY_COVERAGE` sentence should be read and corrected as a recovery/containment numerator, with its denominator named separately. It must not exclude unrecovered true detections from detection coverage. That is a targeted clarification, not evidence that the entire diagnostic record confused detection and restoration.

Detectability and identifiability also depend on observations. Different causes may produce the same signal; more instruments may or may not resolve the ambiguity. A direct binary functional test is adequate when the function really is binary and the test observes it completely. [C14](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) shows the other side: checking order and unchanged sum is weaker than checking that output is a permutation of input. The primary historical example openly trades test rigor against cost; the constructed wrong result [0,3,3] for [1,2,3] makes the gap explicit. Adequacy depends on the asserted requirement and accepted residual, not on whether the check is simple or elaborate. [[F043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a); [C14](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475)]


## 11. GRACEFUL_DEGRADATION_MODEL

Degradation is a relation between a reduced output and a declared acceptable function. It is not a positive adjective attached to any system that continues responding. A service can preserve search with bounded staleness, decline an optional feature or prioritize a critical mission phase. The same fallback may be unacceptable when correctness requires current authority or complete state. [C11](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) makes the difference explicit: a 120-second-old search result meets an assumed 300-second limit, while a payment result without the required authoritative balance fails its stipulated function. No actual financial service or safety certification was evaluated.

A degradation argument therefore names what survives, what is sacrificed, for whom and for how long. It includes transition and reentry, because moving between modes can itself fail. It also considers observability: a reduced service can hide accumulating latent loss while preserving a headline availability ratio. Aggregate success cannot settle the acceptability of harm shifted to a small group. The proposed constraint must come from the appropriate consumer and authority, not from the analyst's choice of a convenient metric. [[F024](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Stopping is another contextual transition. For a reversible batch job, stopping before emitting an invalid result can be the simplest adequate response. For a function whose interruption is itself the major loss, a blanket fail-stop rule can be wrong. A reliability-only calculation cannot settle separate safety, security or contractual obligations. “Fail-safe” needs a specified safe state and consequence model; it is not a universal label for turning something off. P014 and P015 therefore remain conditional rather than becoming a requirement for sophisticated continuous operation or for universal shutdown.

The cheapest adequate degradation can be a clear restriction, a queue limit or a manual procedure. More automation is useful only when it protects a concrete function and its own failure paths are acceptable. Degraded capacity, stale state, privileged fallbacks and recovery load remain residual premises. A useful partial outcome does not need to prove all normal functions restored, but it must not silently change the function it claims to preserve.

## 12. RECOVERY_RESTORATION_MODEL

Recovery is not one operation. A restart changes execution state; a retry repeats work; a rollback restores an earlier version or state; a checkpoint permits resumption from a saved point; a backup restore reconstructs data; reconciliation repairs relationships among states; reconstitution supplies a whole usable environment. Each primitive has a different guard. A command's successful exit can be meaningful for its own operation without proving the receiving application or consumer function. [[F009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Randell's primary treatment is particularly useful because it separates recovery from online causal diagnosis and makes its state boundary explicit. An alternate is attempted after a failed acceptance test and restoration of the relevant prior state; external interactions create further constraints. The paper discusses restricted communication and failure of the recovery support itself, and its performance claims remain partly prospective. This is a historical proposal with carefully stated architecture, not evidence that arbitrary modern rollback can undo external actions. Its wider human/environment analogies are not adopted here as measured transfer. [[F043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

The current recovery predicate is therefore layered. Internal bytes may be restored, their format valid and application invariants satisfied. Credentials, external services or authorization may still be absent. Some external effects may be irreversible. These facts should be stated separately, as [C04](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) does. A bounded return to service can be defensible under a declared residual uncertainty; it need not await complete identification and removal of every possible latent cause. Conversely, a local result must not be promoted to global completion merely because the process returned zero.

Backup claims add source accessibility, integrity, compatibility and configuration. The relevant question is whether a path can recover from the particular loss while preserving the required data/function, not whether an inventory contains a backup file. Existing valid demonstrations, isolated exercises and deterministic rebuild checks can be adequate at different scopes. Refresh is needed when a material premise changes, evidence becomes inadequate or an applicable obligation requires it. A fixed universal rehearsal frequency adds no scientific validity by itself. Current NIST recovery material is read at its stated publication scope; this study neither conducted a restoration nor issued an operational playbook for a target. [[F008](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Retries show why a local recovery benefit can conflict with a system effect. Under [C10](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475)'s stipulated fluid model, 90 incoming jobs per second fit within capacity 100, but one retry for 20% of them produces 108 attempts and cannot sustain a stable constant-rate queue. Removing amplification is a legitimate candidate response. It is not a theorem that no retry can ever help during an overloaded episode: a bounded later attempt may succeed after conditions change. The actual policy needs capacity, deadline, idempotence/deduplication, repeat side effects and consumer-loss evidence. AWS's operator account illustrates complicated recovery/backlog and health-response interactions at one event, without establishing a universal retry budget. [[F017](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Recovery checks themselves can be weaker than the full specification. [C14](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475)'s order-and-sum check accepts a nonpermutation. One can either use a stronger check when the full permutation property matters or honestly state the weaker accepted predicate and residual. This preserves cheap checking without laundering it into a broader proof. It also explains why independent observation can be valuable where a shared defect could affect both producer and oracle, while avoiding a universal demand for a second organization or elaborate validation stack.

## 13. MAINTAINABILITY_REPAIRABILITY_MODEL

Maintainability starts with the item but does not end there. Access, modularity, isolation, testability, replaceable boundaries, documented interfaces and preserved configuration can reduce the work needed to restore a function. Tools, spares, skills, authority and service windows determine whether the work can actually be carried out. The current IEC maintainability publication's accessible scope confirms continuing attention to this lifecycle problem; its paid clauses were not examined. NASA's maintenance guidance and the historical FMECA definitions also connect diagnosis, repair tasks and support rather than treating hands-on replacement time as the whole problem. [[F007](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F023](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F046](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

A useful assessment distinguishes at least three levels: a design rationale, a demonstrated task under specified conditions and an actual support capability over sites/times. A diagram or procedure can support the first without proving the second or third. A five-minute module swap means little to operational availability if diagnosis takes hours, a spare arrives days later or a credential is unavailable. Some delays overlap, so a careful time account uses actual intervals and critical dependencies. The relevant distribution may also have a long tail that an average conceals.

Modularity is conditional. A replaceable unit can simplify repair and make an ambiguity set actionable, but extra connectors, interfaces, versions and configuration states can introduce failures. A sealed inexpensive item may rationally be replaced or discarded. Software may be rebuilt from known inputs instead of repaired in place. The choice depends on function, consequence, waste/resource costs, support and applicable constraints. A generic preference for repairability or for disposability is unsupported by this study.

Maintenance is itself an operating mode. Removing a working part, applying a common configuration update or performing a fragile manual sequence can introduce faults. Gray's historical report discusses maintenance and operating causes within selected reporting and attribution limitations; it does not justify blaming operators as a universal residual category. Current FAA/EASA materials establish active institutional attention at portal/summary scope, not measured effects for a new workforce. This study makes no claim about human fatigue or competence from a staffing number, and none about machine-agent performance from human evidence. [[F026](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F035](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F036](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

A proportionate response can remove an unnecessary task, improve physical/software access, provide a simple error-resistant fixture, preserve necessary state or verify the actual function after intervention. Additional checks also cost time and can create workload and failure opportunities. The criterion is the total restoration consequence, including induced faults and support burden, not the volume of maintenance activity.

## 14. MAINTENANCE_POLICY_MODEL

Run-to-failure, age replacement, scheduled inspection, condition-based intervention, prediction and opportunistic maintenance are alternatives, not a universal ranking. Their effects depend on the failure mechanism, observability, consequence and ability to intervene. The historical RCM line is important for connecting maintenance to required function and consequence; the original 1978 report was not successfully accessed here, so the fresh documentary link is NASA's later uptake. Aircraft-specific proportions reported in that tradition are not promoted to universal constants. [[F023](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

[C05](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) provides an explicit conditional policy comparison. Assume independent renewal after replacement, zero replacement duration, no induced failures, failure cost 100 and planned replacement cost 10. For age limit a and lifetime survival R(t), the average renewal cost rate is [100(1−R(a))+10 R(a)] / ∫₀ᵃR(t)dt. Under an exponential rate .01 and a=30, replacement costs about 1.2858 per time unit, compared with run-to-failure cost rate 1. Under a Weibull shape 2, scale 100 and the same costs/age, the selected age policy costs about .6093, compared with run-to-failure about 1.1284. The example demonstrates a mechanism-dependent reversal, not an optimized universal interval.

These model assumptions do real work. Perfect renewal differs from imperfect repair; planned downtime and new-part defects can change the comparison; a constrained mission may forbid run-to-failure regardless of nominal expected cost. A physical degradation mechanism can support an age policy, but a fitted curve alone does not establish it. A precautionary limit under uncertainty can be legitimate when its rationale and authority are explicit. The study therefore avoids demanding complete causal knowledge before any defensible maintenance decision while rejecting automatic age folklore.

Condition-based maintenance requires information that can usefully change an intervention. A precise remaining-useful-life estimate is not enough if there is no viable action window, spare or maintenance opportunity. False-positive actions can damage good equipment or displace more valuable work. Forecasts trained on selected run-to-failure data may not transport to a fleet where interventions censor future failures. Interventions also change the later data-generating process, so feedback can invalidate a static predictive evaluation. The inspected PHM review and current RUL abstract identify challenges at their actual scopes; neither supplies a universal deployed-utility effect. [[F021](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F032](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

The full comparison includes acquisition, sensing, data cleaning, labels, training, integration, calibration, drift, spares, labor, downtime, missed failures and unnecessary action. Some consequences are constraints rather than monetizable terms. A prospective field comparison can be strong evidence, but it is not required for every conditional analytical result or bounded local decision. P052 rejects universal superiority while preserving narrower support from appropriately qualified evidence. An admitted unknown, a cheap inspection or a retained simple policy can be the scientifically warranted outcome.

## 15. RELIABILITY_GROWTH_FRACAS_MODEL

Test–analyse–fix and FRACAS aim to turn failure evidence into a better engineering state. Observation, hypothesis, change, deployment, verification and comparable subsequent exposure are distinct stages. A closed ticket records an administrative disposition; it does not prove that a failure mechanism is removed. A growing test-hour count may be informative, but only relative to what was exercised and what changed. DLA owner records confirm the historical FRACAS and growth-document scopes/statuses; their entire bodies were not freshly read. The NRC report supplies inspected technical discussion of planning, assessment and extrapolation limits. [[F030](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F031](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F037](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

A local deterministic defect can have a much simpler loop. Reproduce a specific invalid behavior, change the relevant logic, verify the intended behavior and establish that the changed configuration is actually in use. That can settle a bounded correction without a standing board, a fitted growth curve or proof of every possible cause. The stronger claim that population recurrence fell requires comparable opportunities, uncertainty and alternatives to the causal explanation. Both levels are useful if named correctly.

Growth models encode a failure and fixing process. A power-law trend can be a useful planning or assessment model under its assumptions, while prediction becomes fragile when environment, test selection, reporting, repair policy or the rate of fixes changes. [C13](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) shows that the mean function alone does not identify an NHPP; [C08](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) shows that decreasing counts can hide an increasing exposure-normalized rate. A programme that stops implementing fixes should not simply extrapolate a prior active-growth regime indefinitely. The NASA critique abstract points to that transition issue but is not credited with a fully examined replacement model. [[F037](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F039](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F040](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Verification after a fix and observation under subsequent exposure answer different questions. A targeted test can show that the particular reproducer changed as intended; a field trend may reveal recurrence under broader conditions but be confounded. Stronger causal conclusions may require controlled comparisons, natural variation with justified assumptions or a detailed mechanism argument. Rare failures can leave irreducible uncertainty. That uncertainty is not permission to claim efficacy, but neither does it force indefinite open-ended monitoring when a bounded accepted result is sufficient.

No-change and retirement are valid dispositions. A report may confirm that an existing control already covers the mechanism, show that a proposed change would add more risk, or identify that the alleged failure was a measurement artifact. The historical FMECA provision for documented no action is relevant here. A feedback process becomes ceremony when its activity counts, closure rates or required number of corrective actions replace the actual engineering consequence. [[F046](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

## 16. RELIABILITY_EVIDENCE_MODEL

Evidence should be matched to the claim, not ranked by apparent sophistication. A finite exhaustive check, a statistical demonstration, a life test, an accelerated experiment, a stress screen, a field observation and an operator postmortem establish different things. A direct test of a binary function can be entirely adequate for a local decision. It does not automatically earn a probabilistic reliability label. A large statistical sample can be irrelevant if it exercises the wrong population or misses the consequential state.

Under an HPP with constant rate λ and exposure T, P(N(T)=0)=exp(−λT). Inverting the zero-event tail yields a one-sided 1−α upper confidence limit λ_U=−ln(α)/T. For n independent representative Bernoulli demands with common failure probability p, P(no failures)=(1−p)^n gives p_U=1−α^(1/n). [C06](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) evaluates both with α=.05 and T=n=100: λ_U≈.02996 per hour and p_U≈.02951 per demand. The HPP MTBF lower bound under that model is about 33.38 hours. The numbers are conditional calculations, not observations of a real product. [[F005](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a); [C06](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475)]

The confidence statement is not a posterior probability that the unknown fixed parameter lies in a particular realized interval. Its coverage belongs to the procedure under the assumed sampling model. Dependence, incomplete ascertainment, informative censoring or a changed operational profile can invalidate that coverage. A Bayesian credible interval would require a likelihood and prior and has a different interpretation; merely changing vocabulary cannot remove model or transfer uncertainty. This study does not fabricate posterior estimates or priors for absent data.

Accelerated testing needs a justified mapping. The model must address how stress changes the relevant mechanism and how modes combine. In [C07](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475), two independent competing constant hazards are .001 each at use conditions, with acceleration factors 10 and 2. The accelerated total is .012; dividing it by 10 produces .0012 instead of the actual stipulated use total .002. Even unchanged mechanisms do not justify assigning one mode's factor to the mixture. A newly activated high-stress mechanism adds another problem. [[F004](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a); [C07](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475)]

HALT, HASS, screening, burn-in and environmental testing should be described by their actual purpose and protocol. Discovering a design weakness outside use conditions can be valuable without estimating a use-condition lifetime. Screening may remove selected weak units without proving the remaining population's reliability. The inspected NRC testing chapter distinguishes design exploration from quantitative assessment; it does not justify turning any test with the word “accelerated” into a lifetime demonstration. Paid current standard clauses were not examined and are not silently imported as requirements. [[F041](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F016](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Evidence also expires selectively. A changed design may invalidate a failure mode or stress relation while leaving other physical knowledge intact. A change to measurement can invalidate a trend without changing the underlying device. A currentness check therefore asks which premise changed and what discriminator is needed. Hashes bind bytes, not external truth. Repeating a test after every harmless edit can be wasteful, while reusing evidence after a consequential configuration or profile change can be unjustified. The current record preserves both sides.

## 17. SOFTWARE_SERVICE_RELIABILITY_MODEL

Software failure is typically an activation of an inadequacy under particular input, state, scheduling or environment, rather than physical wear of unchanged code. Yet deployed software depends on physical infrastructure, changing data, credentials and people. A deterministic correctness statement conditions on its formal semantics; real services can include concurrency and nondeterminism. The current study therefore does not equate a repeated superficial input with an identical complete state, nor does it transfer hardware aging laws to code by analogy. [[F038](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Operational profiles help express a probability of failure under use. They also create a transfer burden: a release, new customer cohort or changed dependency can alter activation frequencies. Defect density or test coverage may be useful engineering indicators but is not identical to that probability. A formal proof can be strong for a specified program/model while relying on assumptions about libraries, hardware and requirements. A growth model may fit past failures without forecasting a future configuration. These are distinct kinds of support, not reasons to reject software reliability inquiry altogether.

Service practice makes the consumer boundary especially visible. A request can be fast but incorrect; a local component can be alive while a multi-step journey fails; nominal uptime can exclude a maintenance interval that mattered to the consumer. SLIs and SLOs can organize a worthwhile trade-off when the measure, population and tolerance are meaningful. Google's implementation guidance and example policy supply practitioner mechanisms and authority roles. They do not demonstrate universal effectiveness or require every nonservice task to adopt an error budget. [[F024](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F025](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

The budget calculation and the action policy are separate. Arithmetic can show that a service used a stated fraction of a permitted error total. It cannot decide whether the objective was legitimate, whether a security emergency should take priority, or whether an action is authorized. The inspected example policy has exceptions and escalation, which reinforces this distinction. [C09](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) shows the additional measurement problem: an aggregate budget can pass while a minority receives no successful service.

Contemporary incidents supply discriminating mechanisms. The CrowdStrike final account describes a content-processing mismatch and a testing condition that masked a latent failure; it explicitly distinguishes its Rapid Response Content from AI. It is later than the preliminary report and is the version used for the new mechanism account. Planned mitigations in the supplier's account are not independently verified by this author. The AWS report describes a sequence across DNS/control mechanisms, dependencies, backlog and health responses; it is one operator narrative, not a prevalence estimate. These cases motivate configuration/path-specific evidence and cautious attribution rather than a universal condemnation of automation. [[F019](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F017](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

## 18. DEPENDENCY_CORRELATION_MODEL

A service is often a conjunction of functions supplied by others. If all required dependencies must work, their marginal availability values do not determine the probability of joint success without dependence information. A parallel diagram can also conceal a common series dependency. Normal-operation and recovery paths can differ: restoring data may require an identity service or control plane that the original service did not need for every request. The failure boundary must follow the actual claimed function, not the organizational chart. [[F045](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F046](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

The AWS account illustrates the importance of sequence and capacity. A partial restoration in one subsystem does not instantly drain backlogs or restore downstream functions. A health response can remove capacity when measurements or state are misleading. A static dependency list does not encode those dynamics. Equally, the existence of one shared-cloud event does not prove that every region or provider always fails together. Correlation is evidence about joint behavior under observed conditions; a common cause is a mechanism hypothesis that needs its own support. [[F017](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Fallbacks need compatible semantics and surviving capacity. A cache may preserve one read function but violate freshness or authorization requirements for another. A second provider can remove one shared exposure while introducing synchronization, switching, skills and billing/tool dependencies. A spare part can be available in inventory but unusable under the required configuration. The comparison must include these paths and costs, not simply count nominally different boxes.

For a simple system, tracing one critical consumer path and its recovery path can be enough. A universal service map can become an expensive inventory with little causal content. The relevant criterion is whether the representation exposes a dependency that changes the protection claim or decision. Where evidence is insufficient, a conservative bound or accepted uncertainty is preferable to an invented independence coefficient. P034 retains the useful mapping mechanism and rejects inventory size as maturity.

## 19. METRIC_PROXY_MODEL

A metric has a numerator, denominator, population, unit, window and observation process. It also has a consumer and incentives. MTBF, MTTF, MTTR, mean downtime, availability, incident counts, severity totals, error-budget consumption and test hours can all be meaningful, but none automatically identifies the underlying engineering objective. A metric may improve because the system improves, because exposure changes, because detection worsens or because the definition is edited.

[C08](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) is deliberately simple: failures fall from 10 to 5 while exposure falls from 1,000 to 100, so the observed rate rises from .01 to .05. Statistical uncertainty is not computed because no sampling design was supplied. The example is sufficient to refute the implication from lower count to lower rate. [C01](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) refutes the implication from equal availability to equal interruption pattern. [C09](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) refutes the implication from aggregate target attainment to acceptable performance for every group. These are logical distinctions, not empirical estimates.

Proxy validity is often conditional rather than binary. A component health bit can be adequate for the component's own narrow predicate and inadequate for the consumer journey. A repair-time measure can guide a specific tooling decision while omitting logistics needed for an operational-availability claim. Improved detection can increase incident counts. Reclassification, excluded maintenance, moving windows, clock changes and incomplete customer telemetry can affect apparent performance. This study does not infer deliberate gaming from a discrepancy, but it treats such mechanisms as alternative explanations that an audit consumer should examine. The minimum adequate measurement is often small: a clear functional result, exposure count and relevant downtime distribution. Separate safety, security, privacy and contractual constraints cannot be traded away merely by changing a weight in an aggregate score.

## 22. EVOLUTION_UNDER_CRITICISM

Evolution here means a documented change or a current analytical refinement, not an inevitable ascent. Life modeling evolves by distinguishing population mechanisms, observation processes and model uncertainty. Failure analysis evolves through tailoring, dynamic/dependent representations and closer connection to design decisions; some of those purposes were present in historical standards already. Redundancy evolves through better attention to common causes, shared specifications and transition mechanisms, while valid simple independent models remain available. Diagnosis evolves toward action-sufficient uncertainty rather than compulsory causal certainty.

The strongest directional changes are explicit. P008 informs P009 by identifying a joint-dependence premise that changes a numerical protection claim. In [C02](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475), replacing a fabricated independent joint law with the stipulated dependent law changes .99 to .91. The reverse inquiry is different: observations from a redundant system can provide cofailure evidence for common-cause investigation, but cofailure alone does not identify the common mechanism. Both directions are meaningful under different guards; neither is a universal implication.

Detection can enable containment when a supported action exists for the remaining ambiguity. [C12](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475) enumerates that guarded relation and retains the missing-action counterworld. Restoration can, in the reverse epistemic direction, help later diagnosis when before/after evidence is preserved and competing hypotheses predict different results. A successful restart alone does not prove a cause. Randell's separation of recovery from later logged analysis is a documentary example of the distinction; it is not a universal recovery-diagnosis theorem. [[F043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a)]

Mission semantics can define what a demonstration must count, while a demonstration can reveal that an operationalized failure definition omits a consequential state. The first direction is constitutive of an estimand; the second is evidence for revising a definition or its measurement. The evidence cannot decide the consumer's values. Likewise, maintainability/support resources can make a restoration feasible, while a restoration exercise can reveal a missing tool or credential. These are scoped material effects, not a claim that every property must enable every other property.

The current register therefore uses preservation, refinement, narrowing, replacement of a false inference, rejection and unresolved status separately. ## 23. INTERNAL_TENSIONS

Reliability work contains real tensions that cannot be eliminated by adding a universal checklist. Prevention can cost more than tolerated interruption and repair. Redundancy can protect one fault set while increasing common-control complexity. Precise diagnosis can improve a repair choice but consume the useful response window. Stronger acceptance tests can catch more incorrect results but add time, resource and oracle dependencies. More observations can support investigation but burden operation and privacy. The appropriate balance depends on the bearer, consequence, authority and evidence.

The tensions also occur between valid properties. Replication can improve immediate availability while state consistency or recovery gets harder. A load-shedding policy can stabilize a service while concentrating loss on selected consumers. A quick rollback can restore local function while leaving irreversible external effects. Keeping old controls can preserve institutional memory but accumulate stale assumptions and false assurance; removing them can reduce burden while losing protection that is still needed. No aggregate reliability score automatically resolves these conflicts.

Native T-09 specifically compares **live spare service capacity/headroom** with cost and efficiency. Surviving capacity must be considered alongside demand bursts, correlated failover demand, retry/restart work and backlog replay; a reserve lost with the same dependency supplies no usable protection. Headroom can support useful service and backlog drainage, while idle resources consume energy and money and add maintenance or complexity. The relevant choice includes degradation priorities and the consumers harmed by rejection or delay. Existing C036 and the AWS operator account preserve this capacity mechanism; scoped SLO guidance makes the consumer criterion explicit. Neither supplies a universal reserve percentage, a field optimum or authority for a separately forbidden loss. Manual shedding, staged recovery, accepted delay or justified no change can be adequate. Replacement-parts inventory addresses a different restoration-waiting problem, retained in C020; it cannot substitute for this T-09 response. [[F017](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a), [F024](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d260ba8d3056010a); [C10](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475), [C11](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-3a4918109a517475)]

Composition requires asking what each part contributes. In the detection-plus-safe-action construction, removing detection removes the particular automatic containment route; removing the safe action leaves an alarm with no proven containment. The combination realizes a form of the existing containment property, rather than creating a new general property identity. If a separate manual restoration is possible, restoration does not imply detection by that mechanism. In a profile-plus-demonstration construction, removing the profile removes the intended population interpretation; removing observations removes the demonstration. The result is the already recognized scoped evidence property, not a novel property merely because two records are linked.

Overlapping controls must not be counted as independent evidence or additive protection without support. Two dashboards reading the same failed sensor may add no coverage. A repeated easy injection may add little information to a current deterministic check. A backup and a replica sharing a corrupting update can share the very loss mode they were meant to protect. The total-cost account includes implementation, configuration drift, maintenance, diagnosis, evidence upkeep and removal. The composition register records these removal tests and admits zero new irreducible general-property identities from the examined combinations.

## 24. HYBRIDISATION_PRESSURES

Hybrid practice is often useful because a real mission crosses traditional boundaries. A maintenance decision can combine a physical degradation model, a diagnostic signal, spare logistics and a consumer outage constraint. A service recovery plan can combine software state reasoning, fault containment, capacity limits and manual support. A demonstration can combine a formal result, a targeted test and field observations. The scientific burden is to connect each contribution to the receiving criterion without erasing its original scope.

Four workable abstract configurations illustrate the alternatives. For a low-consequence reproducible local artifact, a direct functional check, transparent failure and rebuild can be sufficient; an SLO, prediction model and redundant control plane may add no useful protection. For a mission tolerant of a bounded interruption, one simpler system with accessible replacement and competent support can be a viable choice. For a continuity-sensitive mission, redundant paths with supported joint-failure/transfer/capacity assumptions may be warranted. For a stateful service, bounded degradation and staged restoration can protect useful outcomes while some external reconciliation remains incomplete. These are conditional configurations, not rankings of actual deployed systems.

Their costs differ. The first spends little on ongoing observation but accepts explicit loss/rebuild time. The second spends on support and restoration capability. The third pays for redundancy, isolation, synchronization, proof tests and maintenance. The fourth pays for state, degradation semantics, capacity and recovery coordination. Which is preferable depends on the protected mission and uncertainty. Without field cost and consequence data, the report does not fabricate a total monetary score or universal optimum.

Domain translation must be disciplined. Hardware wear-out can motivate age replacement; software fault activation does not become physical aging by metaphor. Human maintenance findings do not quantify agent reliability. Security recovery can supply relevant mechanisms while its own authority and threat assumptions remain distinct. The primary source's speculative human/environment extension is an analogy until evidence supports that transfer. Hybridization earns a claim through a material mechanism and guarded correspondence, not through collecting the names of traditions.

## 25. STRONGEST_SURVIVING_PROPERTIES

The most robust contributions are conditional disciplines rather than universal implementation packages. Define the required function and failure event.
