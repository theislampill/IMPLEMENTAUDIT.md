A distributed property describes a failure, order, authority, effect or operational contract. A reliability property asks whether a specified function is delivered over its relevant conditions and duration, with explicit dependence, observation, restoration and decision assumptions. A formal property supplies a model, specification, inference, correspondence or proof boundary. Their interaction is substantive only when the output of one changes a premise, criterion, warrant, choice or limit actually used by another.

## 2. Materiality is a guarded correspondence, not a complete proof obligation

A material direction has a particular producer and an actual receiving use. The two must concern the same relevant bearer, function, operation, observation or decision. A direction may constrain the interpretation of an existing claim, expose a missing premise, establish a useful partial input, or justify choosing among alternatives. It need not prove the receiving claim in full, change the native property text, establish a complete implementation or demonstrate a positive net benefit. Requiring all those things would incorrectly reject useful, source-supported partial contributions.

That permissiveness is bounded. Two ideas being useful in the same project is insufficient. An arbitrary encoding into a mathematical object does not establish a Formal contribution. A plausible future AI application is not an actual AI task in the selected source. A protocol's safety condition is not a probability law. An example with a valid local result is not automatically evidence of a wider mission result. Each direction therefore retains its guard, residual premises, strongest adverse case and reentry condition. Its reverse needs its own receiving use; it is not inferred by symmetry.

This method explains both the three clock corrections and the retained unknowns discussed below. It also makes direct evidence, a single adequate mechanism, an existing authorization rule, a cheaper local operation, planned outage and legitimate non-adoption proper alternatives. The study does not reward extra architecture for its own sake.

## 3. Why the three clock directions are material

D04 distinguishes causal/logical order from physical time and arbitrary total order. Its physical-clock branch already includes the source's qualitative architecture: GPS and atomic references with different failure modes, masters that cross-check and evict, and daemons that poll varied masters, reject outliers and synchronize. A006 uses this actual source-owned interface. It does not invent a clock application for a generic reliability maxim. Most implementation detail remains deferred, but that does not erase the architecture the source does describe.

**R009→D04: accepted rescue.** The relevant success event is delivery of the same accepted physical-time function and interval. A second reference that exists but is rejected, unavailable at the relevant time, or defeated by a shared selection path is not automatically a rescue. The useful partial input is the distinction between baseline success A and eligible alternative success B that can rescue outside A. A nonempty possible B\A establishes a logical possibility, not a positive probability or a measured gain. The reverse D04→R009 fixes the timing and uncertainty meaning of that rescue function.

**R040→D04: targeted diversity.** GPS and atomic references provide an actual contrast in technological and physical failure causes. That supports a bounded common-cause analysis. It does not make their errors statistically independent, guarantee that agreeing references are correct, or remove common inputs, environment, maintenance and selection failures. The reverse uses the clock's temporal criterion to decide which distinctions matter to the required function. Counting different technologies without that correspondence would be inadequate.

**R051→D04: the incremental architecture comparison.** The source supports an analytic removal/readdition comparison while holding the accepted clock function fixed. It does not establish the history of the deployed design or the adequacy of the restricted comparator under all faults. Only changed polling, maintenance, acceptance and failure paths belong to the incremental burden. A selector already shared by the compared designs cannot be charged as a newly introduced serial component.

The event distinctions make the guard precise. Ideal success A∪B is monotone under any dependence. A failover guard gives A∪(B∩G): it can destroy rescue while preserving baseline A. A newly common guard gives G∩(A∪B): it can destroy baseline success, but only if that is the real changed architecture. The study does not attribute that latter structure to Spanner merely to manufacture a redundancy penalty. It establishes no measured rescue probability, clock algorithm, numerical error bound, actual net benefit or actual harm. D04→R051 independently supplies the mission and time meaning needed for a fair before/after comparison.

The retained R008 joint-error, R010 containment, R011 observation and R012 action-sufficient ambiguity inputs remain relevant. A cached invalid result or bypassed rejection can defeat the intended protection. Detection is not repair, and a safe action may be justified across several possible causes without a unique root-cause diagnosis. A valid bounded-clock lease does not require a universal additional fence. R044→D04 stays unresolved because an actual handbook estimand/technology/exposure transfer is absent; a numerical drift term alone does not supply it. R055→D04 stays unresolved because the selected clock task is not an AI task. Those conclusions do not erase their separately warranted incoming criteria.

The bounded clock joint compares D04, R009, R040 and R051 and performs removal reasoning. Direct contracts and the corresponding source predicates subsume its useful roles. It is neither a ninth configuration nor a new Formal parent created by writing set notation.

## 6. Eight configurations: what their joint reasoning adds

### C1 — Fixed-intent local operation and present closure

D02, D27, R016, R038, F010 and F031 distinguish present completion Q, uncertainty about execution E and authority A. A successful write to the wrong field does not satisfy Q. A direct normal-return contract or a current authoritative observation can establish the required postcondition; adequate repair or an honest UNKNOWN can be the right outcome. There is no requirement to reconstruct every historical event. The joint value is to keep the operation's result, its repair and the right to act attached to the same contract. Removing those distinctions permits an acknowledgment, an unrelated successful predicate or an unauthorized repair to stand in for completion. Six parent removals, three lineage removals, seven original relations and five current supplements are retained.

### C2 — Replicated service, membership and mission probability

D03, D10, D12, R001, R008, F004 and F012 join failure dependence, authorized membership and a mission event. An ideal dependent comparison can improve success from .90 to .91; an actual newly serial component with reliability .95 can change .91 to .8645. These are different architectures. For three individual failure marginals of .1, majority success can range from .85 to 1 under admissible dependence; perfect cofailure is not universally the worst majority case. A membership theorem supplies a protocol premise, not the missing joint probability law. Fixed membership, a single authorized owner and an accepted outage remain alternatives. The R055→D12 supplement is withheld because C2 provides no actual AI membership task. Seven parent removals, eight original relations and seven current supplements are retained.

### C3 — Asynchronous workflow and external effects

D18, D19, D21, D24, D25, R004, R016, F010 and F014 keep intent, effect, acknowledgment and restoration separate. A sequence “set 1; set 2; retry set 1” defeats a claim based only on unary idempotence: the retry can undo a legitimate intervening operation. Compensation is a domain action with its own success conditions, not a transactional eraser. Durable workflow state must correspond to the external effect and to compatible replay semantics. Local atomic operations, a direct end-to-end contract and human resolution are legitimate substitutes. Replay, retention and migration costs remain part of the comparison. Nine parent removals, eight original relations and six supplements are retained; the R051/D21 comparison is not the A006 clock increment.

### C4 — Recovery, diagnosis and shared capacity

D29, D33, D41, R033, R036 and F004 bind recovery and observation work to the same consequential capacity budget. With backlog three, capacity ten and base demand nine, added sustained work two gives demand eleven; added work four gives thirteen. Added sustained work one consumes all capacity and leaves no draining margin. A one-time burst can still drain later, so a burst and sustained load cannot be conflated. Moving pending work upstream from one to six moves the bound instead of proving an end-to-end reduction. A timer is not a model of actual work. Monitoring can itself share the fault and overload path. Static admission, rejection, a narrower service and omitting an unnecessary observer remain alternatives. Six parent removals, eight relations and seven supplements are retained.

### C5 — State, schema and worker migration

D28, D39, R029, F014 and F034 bind saved state to eligible code and worker configurations. A v1 value n/100 requires v2 m/1000 with m=10n; preserving raw bytes preserves the wrong meaning. In the retained 31-history finite control, the correct mapping succeeds throughout while raw copying succeeds in three and fails in 28. This does not establish unbounded arithmetic, machine overflow behavior or timing correctness. Eligible W1/v1 and W2/v2 combinations do not authorize every cross-product. A direct mapping, compatible incumbent or planned cutover can replace a more elaborate migration mechanism. Five parent removals, six relations and seven supplements are retained.

### C6 — Physical replacement and software state

D36, R019, R020, R021, R022, R027, F010 and F011 separate the renewal model from actual maintenance. The age-replacement cost ratio [Cf(1−S(T))+CpS(T)]/∫₀ᵀS applies under its perfect, instantaneous whole-renewal assumptions. Under exponential life and a positive preventive cost, running to failure can be preferable. A software reset is not physical renewal, and a changed stress can change the failure mode. Logistics, support, downtime and induced failure are not zero simply because their values are unknown. The R055→D36 supplement is withheld in this physical configuration even though that global direction is material for T1's different task. Eight parent removals, eight relations and eight supplements remain.

### C7 — Demonstration under incomplete ascertainment

D42, D44, R011, R026, F015 and F045 relate the event, exposure and observation process to the inference. Under the stipulated thinning model the observed rate is qλ. Zero observed events with unknown q does not determine λ; a justified lower bound on q may support a bounded inference. A process with the same mean need not be a nonhomogeneous Poisson process: floor(√t+U) has the illustrated mean behavior while at t=4 the count is two with zero variance. Exposure, censoring and population selection therefore remain part of the claim. Direct finite proof or adequate direct observation can be preferable to statistical demonstration. Added observation can also consume C4's capacity. Six parent removals, seven relations and eight supplements are retained.

### C8 — Urgent response and control retirement

D41, D46, R013, R038, R039, F031 and F041 connect signal interpretation, action loss, authority and the continued consumer for a control. TP=.04, FP=.06, FN=.01 and TN=.89 give prevalence .05, sensitivity .8 and positive predictive value .4. Under the specified loss model, no action costs 5, direct alarm action costs 1+.06H, and perfect zero-delay confirmation costs 1+.1C. That comparator assumes the confirmation it describes; it is not a universal free-confirmation policy. Accuracy does not supply authority, and removing a foreground consumer does not establish that hidden readers vanished. Keeping a useful signal while replacing a harmful response, or making no change, can be right. R055/D41 and R055→D46 supplements are withheld because this ordinary alarm is not an AI task. The configuration has seven individual parents, nine original relations and five current supplements.
