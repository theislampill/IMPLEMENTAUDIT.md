# Distributed Systems Engineering: a source-bounded requalification

## 1. Scope and central finding

Distributed systems engineering concerns the consequences of separate execution and partial failure. Its strongest contributions are conditional constructions and distinctions: a history model that says what a read means; an agreement protocol that separates safety from progress; an identity contract that makes a retry interpretable; a recovery protocol that preserves a consistent state; and a capacity policy that protects useful work. These contributions do not require every application to adopt every distributed mechanism. Their value depends on the consumer's objective, the actual failure model and the complete cost of satisfying it.

## 3. Plural genealogy and dated development — DS-G

The field has no single founding school. Early network software made communication and host coordination concrete engineering problems. Actor and CSP work explored forms of concurrent computation with different communication semantics. Database work developed recovery, isolation and commitment. Logical-time and agreement research made order, failure and progress precise. Later systems combine these strands under new resource, workload and institutional constraints. Similar vocabulary does not prove one strand descended from another.

RFC 1 is a contemporary network-software artifact, not proof of a direct transmission chain to the later end-to-end argument. The inspected Actor paper is an AI-oriented formalism, not a modern crash-tolerance guarantee. CSP's rendezvous model differs from asynchronous message passing and does not silently supply fairness or a deployed failure model. These distinctions prevent a cloud-native origin story from absorbing earlier work and prevent a historical label from importing guarantees it did not state. [S101](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S001](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S002](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S043](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

## 4. Distributed systems and the cloud-native caricature

Distribution is not synonymous with microservices, containers, cloud deployment or a particular orchestration platform. A single server with independent external storage or a remote effect can have a distributed failure boundary. A large program in one atomic local boundary can avoid some such problems. Conversely, separating processes can buy geography, independent scaling, lifecycle autonomy or real containment. Those benefits must be evaluated at the same boundary as their new communication and recovery obligations.

The received negative rows P47–P51 and P54 remain valuable when read precisely. “Microservices automatically improve resilience” is rejected, but independent deployment can still be useful. “A queue absorbs overload” is rejected as an unbounded capacity claim, but a queue can retain a finite burst. An exactly-once processing contract need not cover an arbitrary external business action, yet its enclosed state transition can be correct. A lock name is insufficient against a delayed writer, but a valid integrated locking or bounded-time protocol is not dismissed. Tool criticism should expose the missing property, not deny the value of amortized expertise and reusable abstractions. [S093](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S066](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S049](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S052](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S035](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

This framing also disciplines evaluation. A topology diagram is not a failure-domain argument. A successful normal-path demonstration is not fault coverage. A benchmark can establish a measured cost under its workload without establishing that the design minimizes lifetime cost for another organization. A pure model can be valid without any deployment, while applying that model to a deployment requires correspondence evidence. The distinction protects both theory and engineering from claims they did not make.

## 5. Failure, timing and uncertainty — DS01

Failure models determine which executions a guarantee must survive. Crash-stop removes a process permanently; crash-recovery permits return with specified retained state. Omission, delay and partition describe communication behavior, while corruption changes values or storage. Arbitrary behavior permits actions beyond a crash model, including equivocation. A common cause can affect several components at once. These categories can be combined in a concrete model, but their names cannot be substituted for each other.

FLP's result concerns deterministic asynchronous consensus with its defined process and reliable-delivery assumptions and a possible failure. It demonstrates a nondeciding execution, not merely an inconveniently long finite delay. Practical protocols may obtain useful progress through eventual bounds, suitable failure detectors or a different randomized termination objective. None of this removes the need to preserve safety when a timing guess is wrong. The inspected partial-synchrony and failure-detector work changes the model and exposes the necessary progress premise rather than making a timeout into proof of death. [S006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S007](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S008](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The RPC uncertainty example makes this concrete. A caller can observe the same send followed by timeout when the request never executed and when it committed but its reply was lost. C01 constructs both histories. A durable operation record can distinguish them. A compensating action may instead settle a business consequence while leaving the historical execution uncertain. Normal successful return is positive evidence under the RPC contract; the lesson is not that every response is forever untrustworthy. [S044](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [CASE_RESULTS](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-74adf14463ab73d1).

Redundancy is similarly conditional. Counting four replicas says little without their identities, protocol and tolerated faulty set. C12 shows the elementary intersection fact for two three-member certificates among four replicas, then changes the model by allowing two correlated equivocators. Authentication does not prevent an authorized faulty participant from signing conflicting values. This finite argument is not a complete PBFT proof. PBFT's inspected model also explicitly assumes independent failures, which remains a source premise rather than something the cardinality example establishes. [S096](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The engineering consequence is a correspondence question: which real storage, restart, clock, configuration and control-plane behaviors fit the chosen model? CORDS and the selected incident accounts show why ordinary error and common-mode paths deserve attention, but they do not provide a population rate for every current system. Model discipline remains useful even when the answer is that a simpler local failure envelope is sufficient. [S091](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S075](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S076](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

## 6. Time, causality, order and currentness — DS02

Order is meaningful only in relation to the question being answered. Happens-before captures defined causal dependencies. A sequencer can impose a total order on concurrent events. A physical clock estimates time under uncertainty. Commit order and observation time are still other relations. Lamport's scalar-clock implication is one-way: causal precedence constrains timestamps, while a timestamp comparison does not by itself establish causality. A total order can nevertheless be an entirely valid and economical interface. [S003](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Physical time is not excluded from correct protocols. The inspected lease design uses bounded clock drift and conservative server behavior for write-through cache consistency. Spanner combines clock uncertainty and waiting with replication and transaction mechanisms. Neither example licenses an unbounded clock assumption, and neither means that a generation token is universally necessary whenever time contributes to correctness. C02 isolates the distinction with nine illustrative rate pairs under an explicit margin and a changed clock premise that permits overlap. It also demonstrates that a high-water token rejects an old request only after the resource has installed a higher token. [S035](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S036](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S094](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S095](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Currentness is likewise a chosen contract. A session guarantee can require a suitably updated replica and report failure if none is available. Read-your-writes does not prohibit a later overwrite. Causal exposure can prevent a reference being visible before its dependency while still allowing concurrent conflicts and sacrificing immediate global freshness. COPS's fault discussion assumes detectable fail-stop components; a lost data center can lose locally acknowledged writes not yet exported, whereas a surviving partitioned center retains delayed data. Metadata and replication queues can grow while collection waits for the required global condition. [S029](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S031](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

These are valid tradeoffs, not defects produced merely by choosing weaker consistency. A stale cache can be adequate for an advisory display. A local or sticky session may be sufficient for a user interaction. A stronger read may require waiting or refusal. The consumer needs evidence adequate to the contract, which may be supplied by a service guarantee rather than a separate certificate on every response. P04–P06 retain these distinctions while removing universal representation and mechanism demands.

## 7. Replication, consistency and application invariants — DS03

Replication can increase availability, preserve data or reduce access cost, but the state and operation model determine what it preserves. Weighted voting assumes more than intersecting numbers: the underlying transactional file model and version interpretation matter. Epidemic dissemination can leave uninformed sites when rumor propagation stops, while anti-entropy supplies a different eventual-spread argument. Deletion metadata has its own retention and resurrection problem. No convergence label removes these obligations. [S015](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S016](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The central semantic distinction is between agreement on state and validity of that state for an application. Dynamo's cart design accepts particular concurrent outcomes to preserve chosen availability and shopping behavior; preserving additions can also allow deleted items to return. CRDT constructions establish convergence under their state or operation delivery premises. They do not thereby preserve every balance, uniqueness or cross-object invariant. C04 compares a valid grow-only union with two individually admissible withdrawals whose merged outcome violates a nonnegative-balance requirement. Escrow or serialization changes the admitted operation set; it is not a blanket verdict against convergence. [S037](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S030](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [N003](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S038](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Consistency names must also retain their definitions. Linearizability is an object-history condition; it does not make an arbitrary sequence of operations a serializable business transaction. SQL isolation names have historically masked different anomalies. HAT's taxonomy shows that useful transactional guarantees can coexist with its defined availability, while conflict prevention or recency can require stronger coordination. Sticky availability is a distinct restriction on which state the client can reach. The taxonomy does not turn every use of “atomicity” into the same commit protocol. [S021](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S022](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S039](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Repair must preserve this meaning. A checksum can reveal a difference without identifying the correct copy. A valid version can later corrupt. A rejoining replica can reintroduce old metadata or resurrect state after its deletion evidence expired. Recovery traffic can consume the capacity needed by foreground service. P07–P09 therefore retain explicit semantic and recovery scope, while P15–P16 compare coordination, reservations, ownership and compatible merge operations instead of assuming one replication architecture is generally mature.

## 8. Consensus, broadcast, membership and leadership — DS04

Agreement, validity and termination answer different questions. A protocol may prevent two conflicting decisions while making no progress during a partition. A selected value can satisfy protocol validity and still violate an application rule that the protocol never checks. A leader can be the current protocol leader while a former leader remains capable of reaching an external resource. These separations are the scientific core of P10–P13; none requires treating consensus as a universal application architecture. [S006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S011](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S012](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Viewstamped Replication, Paxos and Raft describe related but distinct approaches to replicated service state, ordered decisions and leadership. The inspected VR model uses fail-stop behavior and eventual recovery of communication; its treatment of lost replicas does not make arbitrary simultaneous storage loss recoverable. Paxos constrains a later proposal by accepted prior values and durable participant state. Raft exposes terms, replicated logs and membership transitions, with additional conditions for client retries and reads. A short slogan about majority voting omits these state and identity premises. [S010](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S011](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S012](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The majority is also a majority of a particular configuration. C03 enumerates quorum intersection within a three-node membership, then changes the membership so that two locally sufficient groups need not share a node. The counterexample concerns an invalid transfer between configurations, not failure of the fixed-membership result. The original P12 already allowed a safe equivalent to an overlapping transition; it therefore receives a qualified retention, not an invented defect. Flexible Paxos further shows why all quorums need not intersect all other quorums: the relevant intersection is between specified phases. Its inspected model does not supply a general dynamic-membership solution. [N002](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Group communication gives another route to a service's ordering needs. The inspected CBCAST/ABCAST report distinguishes causal and total order under its communication and membership model. This is an actual conceptual interface between dissemination, views and application state, not proof that every application needs a globally ordered broadcast stream. A single writer, a local transaction, partitioned ownership or operations with a compatible merge rule can avoid that requirement. Conversely, an application already using a correctly scoped serial transaction may obtain its required order more cheaply from that transaction than from a new avoidance mechanism. [S013](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S038](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S039](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Byzantine agreement extends the admitted behavior of faulty participants. The inspected sources cover arbitrary incorrect behavior, including accidental faults; deliberate hostility is not a necessary trigger. Authentication, identities, fault cardinality, message scheduling and the independence premise expressly stated in PBFT remain relevant. C12 keeps the cardinality argument separate from a probabilistic story about independent outages. Four replicas with a one-fault bound do not establish that shared software, configuration or administration will respect that bound. More replicas can add cost without separating the failure cause. This is why P52's inherited agent-related profile fields were replaced with a genuine BFT model while its historical text and values remain in the exact transformation record. [S005](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S096](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

## 9. Delivery, semantic identity and duplicate effects — DS05

Delivery evidence is relative to an operation contract. Receipt by a broker, durable enqueue, handler completion and an externally visible business effect are different possible contracts. It is nevertheless incorrect to treat every acknowledgment as inherently weaker than the consumer's objective. The RPC paper's normal-return semantics give a positive counterexample within the declared procedure boundary. P21 therefore retains the demand to identify what an acknowledgment means and drops the universal inference that a second receipt is always necessary. [S044](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Retries become safe when the retried operation has an appropriate identity and repeated invocation has a suitable effect. A request identifier that changes on every attempt cannot identify the original intent. Reusing an identifier for a different intent is equally unsafe. The inspected AWS discussion distinguishes repeated requests from distinct later intentions and couples idempotency state with the effect it protects. The KIP-98 design couples producer identities and sequence state with transactional log operations and consumed offsets. These are useful, bounded mechanisms. Neither a trace identifier nor a broker's internal duplicate suppression automatically makes a remote payment or notification exactly once. [S069](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S049](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The useful positive case is just as important as the excluded case. Beldi and Durable Functions describe mechanisms that atomically connect internal state with execution or message records under explicit storage and execution assumptions. Where the desired result lies inside that boundary, the guarantee can discharge the consumer objective. Calling every such guarantee merely an illusion would erase a real distinction between atomic coupling and two independent writes. Where the effect lies outside the boundary, a further operation contract, shared transaction or reconciliation path may be needed. [S050](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S052](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Retention sets a second boundary. In C13, a journal recognizes an intent while its record exists, then cannot distinguish a sufficiently late replay after that record expires. An explicit rejection of old requests preserves safety at the cost of refusing completion. The appropriate horizon depends on the actual retry, replay, restore and client behavior; indefinite retention is not the default answer. A bounded protocol that makes an old attempt impossible, or rejects it explicitly, can reduce the journal burden.

Queue timeouts and dead-letter routes are not completion certificates. The inspected SQS contract permits duplicate delivery even during a visibility interval. Expiration does not stop a worker that still has access to another service. A dead-letter queue isolates work for later handling, and can change business ordering; it does not supply the missing business result. C14 contrasts a quarantined account-creation message with a later charge that still requires that account. The mechanism has succeeded at isolation while the workflow remains incomplete. [N009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [N010](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The economical decision is to identify the actual boundary, admitted histories and consumer objective first. Pure recomputation, one fixed assignment intent without an intervening conflicting mutation, suitable commuting idempotent operations, a correctly scoped version-conditional write, or an existing atomic identity/result or provider boundary can avoid a new distributed deduplication service. Repeating an assignment is not harmless merely because applying that assignment twice in succession has the same result. If B is meant to supersede A, the history A:set(1), B:set(2), retry(A):set(1) wrongly restores 1 despite unchanged A identity and parameters, valid authority and atomic individual writes. Algebraically, f(f(s)) = f(s) does not imply f(g(f(s))) = g(f(s)). The cheap path needs harmless repetition over every admitted interleaving at the required observation/effect boundary.

C15 adapts the exposed adversarial counterexample and retains fixed-intent, commuting, pure-computation and version/identity positive controls. Atomic recognition of the already completed A preserves B in the displayed trace. It does not order a first arrival of A after B: a broader latest-intent objective requires a separate ordering or version policy. A version precondition must be enforced atomically at the effect boundary and match the actual version semantics; a generic version field is not sufficient. These distinctions correct K01 and P19 while clarifying the related P17/P18/P20/P49 alternatives. They mandate no universal journal, fence, deduplication service or second observer. [S069](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S050](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [exposed finite C15](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-f99303a20e4def33).

## 10. Atomic commit, isolation, sagas and compensation — DS06

Transactions combine a logical contract with recovery machinery. Gray's historical account locates transaction ideas in database implementation and recovery work, including logging and restart. Skeen's nonblocking-commit result has an explicit communication and failure model; it is not an unconditional guarantee for arbitrary partition behavior. These sources support the distinction between a transaction's admitted histories and a protocol's ability to complete under particular failures. They do not support treating two-phase commit, serializability and every form of business atomicity as synonyms. [S017](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S018](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S022](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Compensation changes the recovery objective. The original sagas paper allows component transactions and semantic compensating actions, with intermediate effects visible. Its discussion of funds in transit shows that application representation and readers may need redesign. A compensator is not necessarily an inverse that restores every observer's history. Refunding a charge can repair a balance while leaving a notification, a contractual event or a consumed resource in the world. The availability and failure of the compensator are additional premises. [S019](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

C07 makes both sides explicit. A local transfer protects a balance invariant atomically. A reservation followed by release has a useful compensating path. A sent notification cannot be made unread by recording a refund, and a failed compensator leaves pending work. These are differences in the promised outcome, not a universal ranking of transactions over sagas. A business that accepts visible intermediate states and has cheap, dependable recovery may prefer decomposition; a local atomic transaction may be simpler even where a compensation could theoretically be written.

P23's original requirement that atomic coordination be chosen only for a non-compensatable invariant was too strong. Compensatability does not settle latency, contention, operational effort, customer consequences or the required time at which an invariant must hold. A correct choice compares the actual protocols and operating obligations. P24's core semantic warning survives: compensation must be evaluated against the business outcome rather than treated as automatic rollback.

The inherited S055 case sharpens the distinction without dismissing an entire research system. The predecessor executed a finite concurrent-compensation model with 21 states and nine counterexamples to its chosen nonnegative-state requirement. That actual result is preserved and was not replayed during continuation. The inspected paper leaves concurrent backout and external effects outside implemented scope. The case therefore challenges a particular compensator/invariant interpretation and an overbroad transfer, not every internal DBOS transaction or every reported experiment. [S055](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Transaction and compensation mechanisms can also be composed. A local transaction may protect each step's own state; a higher-level saga may repair a multi-party business process. Removing the local atomicity can introduce a new partial-write failure that the higher-level compensation was never designed to recognize. Conversely, an enclosing transaction can subsume a separate compensator for effects entirely inside its boundary. These are the parent-removal questions recorded in the composition analysis, rather than a demand for both mechanisms everywhere.

## 11. Durable workflows and distributed work state — DS07

A durable workflow separates the survival of work state from the execution of external work. The inspected Durable Functions model connects internal state and messages through atomic conditional commit. Its proofs exclude external calls and critical sections from the relevant formal scope. Preserving that exclusion also preserves the positive theorem: reconstructed internal history can be correct even when an outside action needs a different completion argument. Beldi provides another construction under its storage primitives and execution premises. [S052](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S050](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Checkpoint placement can express useful application knowledge. ExoFlow's inspected design allows annotations and checkpoint decisions around operations, including irreversible effects. That does not establish that any annotation correctly describes the outside world, but it is a real way to avoid imposing the same recovery cost on every task. The inspected 2024 Durable Speculative Execution preprint provides a related exploration of dependency and commit barriers. The final 2026 paper could not be inspected through the available publisher route; results from the earlier version are attributed to that version, with no claim of final-version equivalence. [S054](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S056](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Vendor documentation describes another evidence class: the declared behavior of a service, not an independently replicated experiment. The accessed Azure, AWS and Temporal materials distinguish activities, orchestration histories, service modes and code compatibility. Their guarantees must be read at that granularity. A service's Standard and Express modes cannot be merged into one delivery promise; an activity retry contract cannot be substituted for a workflow history contract. [S057](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S058](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S059](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S060](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

P28 contained a consequential universal quantifier: every worker build had to replay every retained history. The native report elsewhere admitted pinning, and the inspected Temporal versioning material confirms a useful compatible-routing alternative. The required condition is that every eligible path receiving a history can interpret it according to the promised semantics, or that an explicit migration makes that true. C06 keeps an old compatible worker available for old history and demonstrates why a new incompatible command sequence cannot simply be substituted. Pinning transfers burden to routing, old-worker retention and eventual retirement; it does not remove that burden. [N001](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Cancellation also needs a contract. A cancellation request can be cooperative; a terminated orchestration need not perform application cleanup; a timed-out activity can remain able to affect an external resource. These are reasons to distinguish requested, observed and enforced cessation. They do not require universal synchronous cancellation of every task. A harmless obsolete computation can finish and discard its result. A task capable of a consequential late effect needs a suitable authority, operation or reconciliation boundary. [N005](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [N009](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

P25–P28 consequently preserve durable internal execution, allow scoped completion evidence and require compatibility at eligible paths. C06's restored internal value alongside a separately advanced external effect demonstrates why restoration alone does not decide the outside result. The simpler adequate design may be an explicit local state machine with a durable transaction, rather than a workflow platform. The choice depends on the actual history, latency, ownership and recovery requirements.

## 12. Queues, admission, retries and overload — DS08

A queue can absorb a burst when work arrives temporarily faster than it is served. It cannot make indefinitely greater arrival work fit into smaller sustained capacity. It can also hide delay, accumulate expired work and separate the party imposing load from the party seeing the cost. P48 retains the useful burst case while rejecting the queue-as-capacity argument. The required policy can be very simple: a bounded queue, a fixed admission limit, early rejection or a caller that already limits demand. A universal requirement for a continuously estimated deadline-success probability is not supported by these distinctions.

Retries have two effects that must be analyzed separately: another chance to complete useful work, and additional load or duplicate effects. C05 includes a transient safe read helped by retry, a duplicated effect without atomic identity, and three retrying layers that multiply a request into 27 leaf attempts. The last is an authored arithmetic example, not a measured production amplification rate. A single bounded retry point or admission rule can be adequate even if every origin does not receive every downstream capacity signal. [S066](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S067](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Tail-latency techniques are similarly conditional. Redundant requests can reduce a tail when spare capacity and cancellation semantics make the additional work tolerable. They can worsen overload when they compete for the same scarce resource. The source's workload and scheduling conditions matter more than adopting the named technique. Metastability research adds a feedback concern: failure recovery, retries, cache loss or accumulated work can lower effective service capacity and keep the system in a degraded state after the initial trigger has ended. [S061](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S062](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The inspected incident analysis of metastable failures uses a bounded set of 22 incidents, largely public reports with one different access source. That population supports recurring mechanisms and hypotheses; it does not estimate the prevalence of all production outages. The GitHub recovery incident provides a concrete account of prolonged recovery and reconciliation, but cannot be attached as independent proof to every overload property. [S063](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S073](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

DAGOR illustrates an institutional choice inside a technical control. Its local queue-wait signal and business priorities decide which work is admitted and which users or operations bear delay. A downstream response time is not interchangeable with local queue time. The author and revised-paper routes inspected here have recorded version limitations; no uninspected erratum or performance figure is used to prove a universal policy. Rajomon remains abstract-only in this study and supplies a frontier question, not a quantitative winner. [S064](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S065](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

P29–P34 are therefore evaluated by combined offered work, useful completion, rejection consequences, recovery margin and the cost of the controller itself. A shared global budget is one implementation, not a scientific necessity. Independent fixed bounds can jointly meet an objective; poorly coordinated limits can also oscillate or reject useful work. Bulkheads help only when their isolation matches the resource whose exhaustion matters. A separate process name does not isolate a shared disk, connection pool, identity provider or operator action.

## 13. Snapshots, checkpointing, logging and recovery — DS09

The Chandy–Lamport result concerns a consistent global state under its communication assumptions. Such a state can correspond to a cut in a valid permutation of concurrent events without having existed as a simultaneous physical state in the observed execution. P35's inherited problem and audit wording demanded actual coexistence, while other native passages already used the correct coherent-cut formulation. C11 distinguishes those claims: a cut absent from one event ordering exists in another ordering with the same independent events, but becomes invalid after a new send–receive dependency is added. [S004](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

This correction does not make arbitrary collections of local backups consistent. Channel state, causal dependencies, ordering and the algorithm's model still matter. Nor does a snapshot establish the truth of every unstable property at every time. The original stable-property argument has directional limits that should not be erased by calling a snapshot a perfect picture. A cut can answer one analysis question while omitting data needed by a different consumer.

Asynchronous Barrier Snapshotting adapts the snapshot problem to streaming dataflow. In the inspected version, communication and blocking premises permit barriers and operator state to delimit recovery, with additional handling for cycles. That is a documented recipient-side adaptation of snapshot ideas, not evidence that all distributed checkpoint systems have the same storage or output semantics. The study's bounded synthetic experiment cannot establish the performance of a different topology, backend or current deployment. [S048](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Rollback-recovery work distinguishes logging, checkpoint dependencies and the output-commit problem. A correct internal reconstruction can coexist with an external effect that has already escaped. C06 and P36 preserve the internal guarantee and make the external residual explicit. A restored internal counter is not automatically a second safe instruction to an external service, but an enclosing atomic operation or suitable provider identity can make recovery complete at the needed boundary. [S071](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S052](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Incident evidence adds practical premises without turning those incidents into universal frequency claims. The inspected GitLab account includes restoration-path, tool-version and monitoring problems; the GitHub account distinguishes database recovery from later reconciliation and user-visible delivery. A backup that exists may be unreadable, stale or too slow to restore within the required window. Conversely, a successful local restore rehearsal is useful evidence for the exercised scenario even when it does not prove a complete regional failover. [S074](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S073](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

P35–P38 thus ask what state is recoverable, who may write after recovery, which effects need reconciliation, and whether recovery work can complete under the remaining resource budget. A local restore need not implement an entire distributed fencing architecture when there is no competing writer. A failover that leaves a prior writer active does need a valid exclusion mechanism. Rehearsal claims stay bounded to actual failures, configurations, observations and completion criteria.

## 14. Ownership, fencing and concurrent mutation — DS10

The most consequential correction is to universal generation fencing. A lease protocol with bounded clock behavior and a resource or server that delays conflicting permission can establish exclusion under its declared timing model. The inspected leases paper supplies that positive case. Its write-through cache setting and timing premises should not be silently transferred to an arbitrary paused client making a later external write. Conversely, an argument that assumes unbounded pauses and unbounded clock behavior cannot refute every protocol that expressly assumes usable bounds. [S035](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S095](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

A high-water token check has its own often omitted boundary. A resource can reject an older token after it has observed a later one. Merely allocating a later token elsewhere does not ensure that every old request is already rejected at this resource. C02 examines both arrival orders. Correct use therefore needs the token to be installed or checked at the protected operation boundary, or another protocol step that establishes the necessary precedence. Chubby's sequencing, caching and lock-delay discussion is a source-specific design account, not a promise that all users automatically achieve these properties. [S094](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The stale-writer problem is separate from duplicate intent. C10 admits two copies of a current authorized request unless an identity mechanism suppresses the second effect. In the reverse direction, deduplication accepts a first occurrence from an obsolete writer unless authority is checked. Neither mechanism proves the other's whole predicate. Their composition is material only when both hazards occur at the shared protected resource. A trace identifier used by both systems creates no such relation.

P05, P13 and P50 now retain multiple valid mechanisms: resource-checked generations under the necessary installation ordering; a valid bounded-time protocol; an atomic local exclusion boundary; or ownership rules that remove the competing-writer case. The analysis asks which failures a design actually admits and what cost is justified. It does not replace the universal slogan “all leases are enough” with “all leases require the same fencing format.”

## 15. Protocol, schema and configuration evolution — DS11

Compatibility is a relation between particular producers, consumers, stored state and transitions. A wire format can remain parseable while the meaning of a field changes. A new writer can produce data that an old binary reads incorrectly after rollback. A feature flag can route some traffic through an old path that still uses an obsolete assumption. The inspected Protocol Buffers guidance and rollback discussion make these reader/writer and sequencing questions concrete. They do not establish compatibility for an arbitrary schema merely because its syntax validates. [S078](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S079](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

An evolution plan should identify eligible mixed-version paths and the point at which old interpretation can no longer recur. This point can depend on stored records, message retention, pinned histories, rollback procedures and rarely used clients. It need not require every machine to hold the newest binary if routing and compatibility establish the intended contract. P39–P40 therefore distinguish completion of a defined migration from a universal all-nodes-at-once rule.

Configuration faults show why membership counts alone do not define independent operation. The inspected Meta and Cloudflare incident accounts concern shared control/configuration paths that affected many otherwise separate components. These are concrete cases of common exposure, not estimates of how often every configuration system fails. A separate control plane can still depend on the data path it must repair; an out-of-band route can share the disabled dependency. [S075](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S076](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The evolution–replay relation is bidirectional but asymmetric. A code change constrains which workers may interpret an old history. Retained histories constrain when old worker code can be retired. Neither direction establishes that an external effect remains valid or that every possible history was tested. P28, P39 and P46 together expose a lifecycle burden: retaining compatibility indefinitely costs storage, workers, operational knowledge and security maintenance; retiring it requires a justified horizon or explicit conversion. [N001](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S060](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

A simple adequate path may be a planned stop, export and restart, a local transaction or a deliberately unsupported old version with an explicit rejection. These can be more predictable than continuous mixed-version operation where the business permits downtime. Where continuity is required, the additional compatibility machinery needs a correspondingly precise contract.

## 16. Observability, diagnosis and reconstruction — DS12

Observability is evidence for a decision. A sampled trace can reveal a recurring latency path without reconstructing every request. An aggregate can support a capacity decision while being unable to establish the completion of one customer's irreversible operation. A causal identifier can connect events without establishing which participant was authorized to act. P41–P43 therefore separate usefulness, completeness, currentness and authority instead of demanding a complete trace for every action.

The inspected Dapper material supports sampling and production tracing as a practical design, at the indexed sections actually read. X-Trace extends causal reconstruction across administrative domains but permits partial visibility. Hindsight trades collection and buffering costs against later demand for detailed traces; eviction and the tested workload remain limits. W3C Trace Context standardizes propagation semantics and includes privacy and sampling considerations. These are different kinds of source: design, evaluated system and protocol specification. None is an omniscient observer. [S080](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S081](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S083](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S082](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

C08 gives a positive continuation case. Under an explicitly hypothetical independent sampling model, zero failures in 100 trials yields a specified one-sided bound below the authored five-percent decision threshold. The same observation cannot certify the next individual unsampled operation, and biased sampling or stale measurements invalidate that calculation. The example is arithmetic under assumptions, not a production reliability estimate or a recommended universal threshold.

Diagnosis also depends on the oracle. Aletheia's inspected evaluation reports true positives, false positives and false negatives against its stated ground-truth approximation. Query interpretation and implicit associations limit what the observations prove. Elle's inspected PVLDB paper derives isolation anomalies from recoverable observations under its interpretation conditions; absent a detected anomaly is not universal correctness, and unsupported predicates remain outside scope. The native Elle DOI was not silently equated with the actual accessed paper. [S092](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S087](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Stronger observation carries costs: storage, cardinality, delay, sensitive-data exposure, interference and dependence on the telemetry path itself. The cheapest useful evidence may be an existing atomic operation result or a local state check. A high-consequence cross-service uncertainty may justify reconstruction, independent observation or reconciliation. An observer that fails during the incident leaves a missing-evidence condition, not automatic evidence that the observed service succeeded or failed.

## 17. Fault experiments and the populations they actually examine — DS13

A fault experiment connects a hypothesis, a selected system and configuration, an injected fault, an observation and an oracle. Its result is useful at that conjunction. A process-kill test does not exercise every storage corruption. A single-region drill does not establish independence from a shared identity service. A test that never reaches the faulty path does not demonstrate tolerance of that path. P44 retains these distinctions without demanding an impossible exhaustive experiment over all failures.

The inspected CORDS study exercises selected single-block, single-node faults in eight systems under specified replication and workload conditions. LazyFS examines a different persistence fault surface: its seven-system study reports 20 bugs with known, ambiguous and new classifications, and excludes some metadata behavior. These are concrete demonstrations that persistence assumptions deserve scrutiny. They are not independent random samples of all contemporary databases or a direct estimate of production incident rates. [S091](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S088](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Rose adds a current fault-injection design and reports 20 bugs in eight selected systems. Two Redpanda observations share a cause; exclusion of unsuitable, unbuildable or expensive-oracle systems matters to the denominator. Control paths, threads and memory-mapped behavior impose blind spots. Its cheaper level can find a useful subset without establishing equivalent coverage to the most extensive mode. These distinctions support a tiered experiment choice, not a mandate to run the most expensive experiment on every system. [S089](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The inspected methods in S090 distinguish 198 analyzed tickets from 73 reproduced failures. S086's repository and false-positive populations depend on filtering, selected tools and reporting conditions. Neither number can be substituted for a random industry denominator. S092's diagnostic evaluation has explicit false positives and false negatives. These examples reinforce a recurring lesson: a discovered bug, a reproduced failure, an eligible repository and an independent study are different units. [S090](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S086](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S092](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

AgentChaos provides a recent and separately scoped frontier. Its configurations, benchmark tasks, model choices, triggering conditions and repeated seeds are described in the actual preprint reading. Trigger-conditioned outcomes and low starting success rates constrain interpretation. Repeated seeds of the same implementation do not become independent implementation replications. The inspected arXiv version is not silently promoted to a later conference artifact. [S098](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The 14 original successor cases in this study remain unchanged authored discriminators, exposed before execution. The separate r2 case C15 adapts the exposed reviewer assignment counterexample and adds explicit first-arrival and objective controls; the reviewer models and root reproduction are prior exposed evidence, not held-out tests or independent new studies. They are finite enumerations or calculations with visible premises, not held-out data, industrial benchmarks or empirical validation of this commission. The inherited compensation result is a separate predecessor execution. Together they test specific distinctions: uncertainty versus repair, current authority versus duplicate identity, a valid cut versus actual coexistence, and a useful control versus its cumulative cost. A successful case gives no frequency estimate. A failing helper is diagnosed as a helper failure unless evidence connects it to a scientific proposition.

## 18. Coordination avoidance and application semantics — DS14

Avoiding coordination is meaningful only relative to a coordination requirement. An application can reduce cross-node agreement by using local ownership, partitioned operations, compatible monotone accumulation, reservations or an altered business promise. These choices differ. Some move coordination to allocation or reclamation; some weaken a recency requirement; some preserve an invariant because the permitted operations cannot violate it. None establishes that every distributed operation is independent.

The inspected CALM review characterizes a relationship between monotonicity and confluence in the relevant computational model. Confluence is not linearizability. Communication can occur without the coordination that the result excludes, and a whole application does not inherit a component's property merely because it uses a CRDT. The review is not a substitute for a complete inspection of every original formal proof. Its examples of added/deleted sets and completion decisions illustrate why closure, absence and reclamation can reintroduce a coordination question. [N006](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Invariant confluence asks a different question: whether independently valid states remain valid under the defined merge for the admitted transactions and invariant. Strong eventual consistency supplies a convergence contract under its delivery or state-merge premises. Commutativity can help one construction without establishing either every invariant or the required observation order. P16 now keeps these result families distinct. C04's balance example shows how a valid convergent mechanism can still fail a different application rule. [S038](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S030](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [N003](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Session guarantees and causal systems offer useful middle choices. Read-your-writes, monotonic reads, writes-follow-reads and monotonic writes are not interchangeable. In the inspected session paper, successful use still depends on reachable replicas satisfying the session constraints; a later overwrite can matter to what a client sees. COPS supplies a concrete causal design with local storage assumptions, garbage-collection conditions and a limit when an unexported local write is lost with its datacenter. These are actual alternatives to blanket strong consistency, with actual availability and retention costs. [S029](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S031](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

CAP rhetoric often obscures these choices. The inspected Gilbert–Lynch result concerns its specified atomic register and availability definition under partitions. The later inspected Gilbert–Lynch perspective revisits the definitions and engineering interpretation. It is a different work from Brewer's S026, which remains externally uninspected here. Changing a consistency or availability requirement can select a different design point; it does not refute a theorem whose premises no longer describe that point. [S025](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [N004](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

The practical criterion is the cheapest mechanism that meets the actual invariant, observation and completion contract over the admitted failures. A strong transaction already present at the correct boundary may cost less than new application-specific coordination avoidance. A monotone accumulation with no harmful intermediate observation may need far less coordination than a global log. Neither direction is a universal maturity ladder.

## 19. Architecture, deployment domains and the current frontier — DS15

Distribution can serve scale, latency, fault containment, administrative separation and independent evolution. It also creates interfaces where delay, partial failure, identity and interpretation must be reconciled. The relationship is not one-way: more independently deployed services can improve one constraint and worsen another. Fowler's inspected account is practitioner argument and experience, not a controlled comparative study; its useful role is to articulate tradeoffs and a credible simpler alternative, not to prove a universal monolith-first law. [S093](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Locality is therefore a scientific nontrigger, not a nostalgic preference. If one transaction encloses the whole required effect, it removes a particular remote coordination problem. It does not remove every network dependency elsewhere in the product. If a service boundary is required by geography, ownership or independent failure containment, pretending it is local does not eliminate that boundary. P14, P45–P47 and P54 compare the actual interface and cost rather than counting services or approving a fashionable topology.

The edge frontier demonstrates why deployment labels are insufficient. The inspected N008 architecture describes a majority-backed log and storage design while explicitly excluding partitions from the relevant model; logging a deployment decision is not observing that every deployment action completed. Its publication is March 2026 despite a 2025 DOI string. N007's accessible publisher abstract identifies a further edge-oriented logging proposal, but the body was inaccessible and its guarantees and performance are not admitted. These sources motivate questions about intermittent connectivity, resource constraints and placement without supplying a general verdict on edge reliability. [N008](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [N007](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

Agentic systems add uncertainty about computation and task interpretation to familiar distributed boundaries. The inspected SagaLLM publication is from 2025, correcting the inherited 2026 date. Its qualitative cases and limited model/task population do not establish that all agent work should use sagas, that a validator is independent of the actor it checks, or that compensation restores arbitrary external effects. The source itself leaves important formal and implementation work open. AgentChaos and the inspected survey ask related but distinct questions about faults and architecture. Shared vocabulary such as actors, agents, retries or compensation is not proof of direct transmission from every older formalism. [S097](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S098](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S099](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

This study's currentness claim is bounded to the search and access record through 10 November 2026. It does not rank every current database, workflow service, edge system or agent framework. Received-only geo-distributed and architecture citations are identified as such, with no unseen results borrowed for the synthesis. The inspected alternatives are sufficient to adjudicate the retained universal claims; they do not complete all possible future comparative evaluation.

## 20. Criticism, evolution and ceremony stripping — DS16

The original corpus contains substantial sound analysis. It distinguishes failure models, causal order, consistency, duplicate effects, recovery, capacity and architecture costs; preserves rejected forms; and repeatedly gives local alternatives. Those contributions remain. The requalification does not require every row to change, nor every caution to become a defect. P12's existing safe-transition alternative, P24's compensation warning and P35's already-correct narrative passages are examples where fidelity requires preserving the sound material while correcting the narrower conflicting text.

The demonstrated revisions have different causes. A bounded-clock lease refutes a universal necessity claim, not a concern about stale writers. A valid consistent cut refutes an actual-coexistence requirement, not causal consistency. Compatible routing refutes an every-build/every-history quantifier, not replay discipline. A normal RPC return refutes an all-acknowledgments-are-insufficient statement, not uncertainty after an exception. The P52 profile repair restores the relevant subject matter and distinguishes arbitrary faults from only malicious participants. Source identity and date corrections improve citation fidelity without creating a new theorem.

Other revisions are analytical qualifications rather than discovered universal counterexamples. Deadline control, feedback, retry budgets and transaction choice admit several useful policies. The source bodies and constructed cases support narrowing their universal implementation language. The ten native evidence partitions are retained as distinct questions; historical ratings do not prove current deployment transfer, independent replication or a field's overall effectiveness. The criticism register records these differences so that a source-access limit is not confused with a false statement.

Ceremony is machinery without a justified contribution to the intended outcome. A term number not checked by a resource cannot protect that resource; a trace identifier alone cannot authorize a write; a dead-letter queue cannot finish a business process; an unused backup cannot establish recovery time. Yet an abstraction can amortize difficult work across many consumers. Requiring each team to rebuild a transaction log, retry identity scheme or workflow history may cost more than a sound shared implementation. P51 therefore retains criticism of empty ceremony while recognizing the positive value of a well-scoped abstraction.

Criticism itself is subject to scope. Kleppmann's lock analysis distinguishes efficiency and correctness and exposes delayed-client hazards, but its timing assumptions do not invalidate a correctly bounded-time cache protocol. HLC's historical rhetoric does not erase physical-clock analysis in Lamport or partial synchrony research. A new paper's claim of novelty or superiority is a research claim to inspect, not a reason to rewrite the field's ancestry. [S095](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S033](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S003](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989), [S007](../SOURCE_MAP.md#s-1e32122ff3a9d989).

## 21. Cost, institutions and accountable decisions — DS17

The cost of a control includes more than its normal-path latency. It can require retained history, workers for old versions, additional replicas, clock discipline, identity distribution, tracing storage, recovery capacity, operator knowledge and a party authorized to resolve exceptions. A compensation scheme needs someone who owns the business consequence. A priority policy needs someone accountable for which work is rejected. A shared service can reduce duplicated work while concentrating a common-mode dependency.

C09 makes the role of cost explicit using hypothetical values. With authored control costs and residual-risk assumptions, an extra control is warranted in the high-consequence case and not in the lower-consequence case. When two controls share a failure cause, their presumed combined benefit falls. The same already-selected adequate bundle remains unchanged under the no-change control. These numerical examples are transparent decision illustrations, not estimated probabilities, prices or an optimizer for real organizations.

A qualitative evaluation can be more honest than a fabricated universal formula. The analyst can ask whether the additional mechanism changes a credible failure consequence, whether its premises are independently supported, and whether a simpler existing boundary already supplies the result. For a local durable update, a second remote coordinator may add cost without reducing the relevant risk. For an irreversible cross-party action, stronger completion evidence or reconciliation may be worth substantial cost. The decision remains conditional on the consumer objective, not the label of the mechanism.

Institutional boundaries shape those premises. Two services run by one operator may share credentials and configuration. Two nominally independent providers can share a network, authority or human procedure. A business process that crosses firms may lack an atomic commit interface and may be unable to compel a compensator to act. A privacy rule may limit how much state an observer can retain. These are correspondence questions that the neutral study can expose but cannot answer for an unexamined organization.

Retirement is part of the mechanism. Removing an old worker, deduplication record, tombstone, schema reader or signing key is safe only relative to the paths that can still use it and the consequences of rejecting them. Indefinite retention also carries cost and exposure. P20, P28, P39 and P46 collectively require a justified horizon or conversion/rejection rule; they do not require retaining every historical artifact forever.

## 22. Relations and conditional configurations — DS18

The relation register evaluates actual shared interfaces and both directions separately. ## 26. Author self-critique and review boundary

The main risk in a study of this breadth is flattening differences into a universal checklist. The reconstruction counters that risk by preserving each native identity and separating current qualifications, source access, model validity, deployment correspondence and consumer decisions. It can still be wrong at a particular inference or relation; the exact records permit reviewers to challenge that inference without trusting the author's summary.

A second risk is overcorrecting universal warnings and losing their valid cases. The bounded-clock lease correction does not make an arbitrary stale external writer safe. Positive internal exactly-once semantics do not absorb unmodeled effects. Pinned histories still need compatible eligible workers. Sampled evidence still needs a valid sampling and decision model. The positive, adverse and no-change cases are intended to expose these two-sided errors, not to prove the whole field through small scripts.

A third risk is source dependence and selective access. The study retains version boundaries, abstract-only exclusions, scoped readings, incident selection and shared result families. It does not turn 112 citation records into 112 independent studies. The current source corrections and access failures remain part of the subject. Claims of scientific acceptance await the controller's two sequential, distinct substantive reviews of the same final science. Packaging verification, author self-critique and a successful generator do not supply either review.
