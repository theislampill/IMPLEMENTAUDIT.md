## 3. Claim formation, invariants and progress

A useful application inference distinguishes at least three propositions: evidence supports P under assumptions A and semantics M; A and M represent the relevant implementation/environment; and P answers the intended requirement. They can have different truth values. A theorem from inconsistent assumptions cannot supply an application needing realizable premises. An intentionally narrow lemma can nevertheless be exactly the intended result.

For a transition system, induction requires initiation, consecution and an implication to the desired property. If Init implies I, every T-step from an I-state preserves I, and I implies P, the argument establishes P for reachable states. The reverse is invalid: P can hold on all reachable states yet fail consecution at an unreachable P-state. Native P006 already made that distinction; the revision supplies a concrete discriminator without inventing a historical defect.

Safety and liveness also retain different quantifiers. Remaining forever in a safe waiting state can violate eventual progress. Weak and strong fairness constrain different enablement patterns; neither supplies a numerical response deadline. The finite lasso constructions preserve these differences and permit arbitrarily long finite delays where the eventuality does not bound them.

Property class matters for refinement. A system whose output possibilities are the same for each secret can lose that relational information-flow property when a refinement selects output equal to the secret. The behavior set shrinks, but this particular hyperproperty fails. A refinement selecting a fixed output preserves it. The result challenges an unsupported property transfer, not refinement in general. [Report §§5–6](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d5e5aa993d91de56), [cases C02–C04,C15](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be00f7164600b45).

## 4. Composition requires compatible semantics and frames

A producer guarantee must actually discharge the receiver's assumption. A guarantee x≥0 does not establish x>0; strengthening it to x≥1 can repair that interface. The weaker local fact remains valid while the attempted composition fails. A system goal can guide an interface specification in the reverse direction, but it does not prove that the component meets it.

Frames protect supposedly untouched state only under the relevant ownership and interference premises. Aliasing can change a value that a naive local proof treats as separate. Likewise, two implications A⇒B and B⇒A do not establish either when both can be false. A sound circular assume-guarantee rule remains possible with the appropriate temporal, induction or closure conditions; simultaneous induction from a base differs from an unsupported circle.

These are material partial contributions. A local contract can remove one uncertainty without establishing progress, deployment correspondence or all stakeholder requirements. Requiring the whole system's proof before admitting any useful local contribution would be an underclaim. Treating the useful local contribution as the whole proof would be an overclaim. [Report §7](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d5e5aa993d91de56), [composition R03](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-c226a3bf7db4e6db), [cases C07–C08](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be00f7164600b45).

## 5. Abstraction and search preserve directional limits

A sound over-approximation includes the concrete behaviors and may add others. Proving universal safety over that abstraction can transfer to the concrete system, but an abstract counterexample can be spurious. A sound under-approximation can supply a concrete witness; absence of a witness there does not establish universal safety. More detail can remove false alarms while increasing state space and maintenance cost. The adequate model is the one that discriminates the consequential claim at justified cost.

Completed finite model checking is complete at its exact domain. Bounded exploration of a larger system is a different result, and interrupted search is inconclusive. C06 distinguishes a counterexample first reachable beyond a chosen bound, budget interruption, and completed exploration of a finite cyclic graph. A cycle need not prevent exhaustive state exploration; a chosen depth does not make the original system finite.

The accepted source repair also corrects the old public Alloy citation and qualifies version-dependent claims. Current inspected Alloy 6 documentation distinguishes bounded temporal checking from complete checking over a finite universe. That does not establish every infinite path is ultimately periodic or validate every implementation. The historical source, current documentation and actual checked object keep distinct roles. ## 6. A solver or proof result belongs to its statement

A SAT witness, UNSAT certificate and UNKNOWN response are different evidence. A satisfying assignment can be checked against its formula; a certificate can support a separate checking path with its own parser, rules and semantics. Both can leave the translation from intended problem to formula unverified. Two solvers sharing a mistaken encoding do not provide independent assurance of that translation. The inspected SMT-LIB 2.7 revision does not guarantee that an assignment returned after UNKNOWN satisfies the asserted formula.

Proof search and proof checking similarly have different trust boundaries. Axioms, admitted statements, evaluation, external oracles and parsers can affect the actual guarantee. A smaller kernel can reduce a trusted boundary without establishing universal correctness by size. A valid checked proof of a fixed intended statement remains valuable regardless of whether a human or AI found it.

Type and static-analysis guarantees must be named. A correctly typed integer result can implement the wrong function, while richer types may express substantial functional correctness. A sound analyzer for a restricted language and error class retains that result even when it says nothing about an unsupported program. A documented escape, an implementation soundness bug and a spurious alarm are distinct. An eager verification-condition argument also cannot silently migrate to lazy semantics; the accepted LiquidHaskell discussion retains its recorded access and implementation limits.

Specification weakness is likewise separable from proof invalidity. An impossible antecedent, absent temporal trigger and too-weak postcondition are different reasons a result can miss the intended objective. A deliberate narrow lemma is not deceptive merely because it is narrow. Shared omission in forward and backward generated translations defeats a simple round-trip assurance argument, but does not invalidate checked proofs of correctly fixed statements. [Report §§10–12,16,20](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d5e5aa993d91de56), [cases C02,C09,C13,C19](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be00f7164600b45).

## 7. Concurrency, monitors and translation boundaries

Linearizability requires a suitable sequential account respecting the real-time order of nonoverlapping operations. It is not merely any serial permutation and supplies no automatic progress. The accepted finite complete-history checker rejects a read returning 0 after a completed write of 1 in its declared model. Pending invocations require the fuller definition; the study discusses them without claiming the bounded checker implements it.

Memory semantics can add executions. The finite store-buffer construction produces a fourth result pair absent under the chosen sequentially consistent model. It is a discriminator, not a complete x86, ARM, C or Rust semantics. The relaxed-memory RustBelt follow-up changes assumptions and contributes its own repair; that does not retrospectively falsify a theorem under the earlier sequentially consistent model.

A passive runtime monitor can establish useful observed evidence without the authority to intervene. Missing events, delayed verdicts and enforcement failures remain separate. A continuation-based monitorability condition need not guarantee a decisive verdict on every actual run. Finite satisfaction can still be established for an appropriate co-safety observation; monitoring is not restricted to failure detection.

Compiler preservation supplies another specific correspondence edge. A target behavior included in the allowed source behaviors satisfies that relation, while a different artifact may not be the object of the theorem. Parsing, assembling, linking, hardware, undefined behavior and timing retain their own scope. Translation validation checks an individual result under a supported subset; a weakened experimental postcondition does not establish the original stronger one. A checksum establishes identity, not semantic preservation. [Report §§13–15](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-d5e5aa993d91de56), [cases C10–C11,C16–C17](SOURCE_MAP.md#s-0be00f7164600b45).

## 8. Evidence after change, cost and the current source frontier

Theorem truth, proof-script executability and present application relevance can diverge. An API change can break a script without changing the theorem. A changed environment can invalidate an application premise while leaving the proof intact. Weakening a statement can produce a green proof that no longer answers the old question. Reuse therefore follows affected propositions, assumptions, tools and correspondence boundaries, rather than requiring indiscriminate requalification after every harmless edit.

A direct proof, assertion, finite truth table or existing control can already resolve a decision. The accepted four-row truth table for the two-input XOR rule is complete for its small domain; it does not scale automatically to arbitrary systems. Cost includes expertise, annotations, compute, integration, observation overhead and maintenance. Selected industry accounts, nonrandom surveys and vendor-referred interviews can reveal mechanisms and barriers without establishing a universal causal return on adoption.

Source dependence remains explicit. Multiple CompCert or seL4 accounts often share one project and proof stack. A paper, preprint, data card and benchmark representation need not be independent studies or mathematical problems. External artifacts were not installed or reproduced by this documentary author, and inherited source accesses are not new browsing.
