## 4. The engineering problem and the plural history

AOSE addresses software in which intentional responsibilities, bounded discretion, persistent goals, interacting participants and changing organisations are useful subjects of engineering. It asks how requirements, responsibilities, interactions and implementations relate. That question is wider than any one BDI language, message standard or contemporary LLM framework. A strategic actor in a requirements model need not become a runtime agent. An engineering agent can be implemented on ordinary services and objects; a computational actor is not automatically the same abstraction. [Sources CS001, CS005–CS007, CS018–CS026](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [claims C012–C019](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

The received genealogy is plural. Contract Net contributes a distributed allocation precedent; practical reasoning and intention accounts inform BDI semantics; intentional programming supplies another foundation. Actor/dependency requirements work develops a different engineering viewpoint. Gaia imports conventional analysis/design concerns, including FUSION, while representing roles and interactions. Fresh Gaia passages support that import and its bounded original domain, including the fact that requirements capture is outside that method's analysis/design scope. These observations do not make every AOSE method descend from one programming paper. [Genealogy G01–G06 and GE01–GE07](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [NEW-A01](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Tropos carries actor/goal/dependency analysis through development, with security and later adaptive extensions. MaSE and O-MaSE address systematic and customisable development; Prometheus develops agent design and related consistency/testing work. MAS-CommonKADS keeps knowledge, task and inference views; DESIRE treats compositional specification. PASSI, Agile PASSI, INGENIAS and Agent OPEN offer differing routes through modelling, code, process tailoring and method fragments. These are not interchangeable spellings of one lifecycle, and peer comparison is not evidence of direct descent. [Sources CS012–CS013, CS024–CS025, CS029–CS041, CS043, CS048, CS053–CS058, CS066 and CS069](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

ADELFE and ASPECS are particularly useful counterexamples to a universal method. ADELFE's local-cooperation treatment addresses adaptive systems under its conditions and expressly leaves room for ordinary objects. Its quoted existence-form theorem does not by itself prove that every local repair policy will achieve the required global outcome. ASPECS combines method ideas for holonic organisation; the source-specific PASSI/RIO relationship is retained, while arbitrary method composition remains unjustified. The fresh ASPECS publisher visit supports identity and abstract scope, not a new full-body reading of its detailed method. [Claims C026–C028](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [NEW-A09 and NEW-A13](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Public commitment and information-protocol approaches respond to an important limitation of private mental-state communication semantics: independent parties need assessable public meaning. Singh's 1998 criticism does not prohibit internal BDI perspectives. It instead prevents an unobservable private state from automatically settling public compliance. Normative organisation, multidimensional agent/environment/organisation programming and later operations work introduce other explicit interfaces. Their adoption does not make norms hard access controls, nor does it make design support a complete operational lifecycle. [Sources CS014, CS028, CS061–CS062, CS074, CS076, CS078–CS080, CS098 and CS121](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

The frontier includes temporal, normative and explanatory BDI work and symbolic/learned hybrids, alongside modern framework studies and security benchmarks. Explicit hybrids can establish documentary continuity at the named interface. Similar labels cannot establish that every LLM framework inherits AOSE's earlier concepts or assurances. The official Tropos+ abstract supplies a new stated continuation, but describes a research agenda rather than a completed method. The official AGENT workshop record establishes community context. Neither is evidence of broad effectiveness. [Genealogy G28–G32](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [NEW-A11–NEW-A12](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

## 5. Requirements, authority and interaction

An intentional model is an instrument of inquiry. It can expose dependencies, conflicts and affected actors, yet remain wrong about who matters or what would count as success. This is why affected-stakeholder validation, requirement-to-acceptance correspondence and implementation verification remain distinct. A weighted goal score can hide a hard constraint; a completed diagram can omit a non-user. In executed cases AC01–AC02, an explicitly omitted neighbour defeats the finite completeness claim, while an unknown affected population produces insufficient evidence rather than a fabricated completeness result. The example is a logical discriminator, not an observed incidence estimate. [Claims C001–C011](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [executed cases](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Authority is similarly irreducible to intention. A capable actor may lack permission; an authorized actor may lack capacity. A requested or adopted goal does not grant either. The four executed permission cases preserve refusal, infeasibility, unknown support and permitted action as distinct outcomes. Their input permissions are stipulated for a constructed world; no algorithm here determines real legitimacy. A successful effect also cannot retrospectively authorize an action. [Claims C006–C009 and C125](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [cases AC03–AC06](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Role structure, concrete holders and incurred obligations operate at different levels. A role can have many holders, and one agent can hold several roles. Organisation rules can restrict combinations that are locally permissible. During reconfiguration, a replacement capable of a role may nevertheless lack authority to assume an existing promise, creditor consent or compatible protocol state. AC07 refuses retirement with an unresolved non-transferable obligation; AC08 permits the constructed valid transfer; AC09 omits transfer-specific work when no live duty exists. This preserves both safety of continuity and a cheap nontrigger path. [Claims C020–C025 and C105](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Interactions require meaning beyond message syntax. Shared fields can carry incompatible currencies, referents or discharge conditions. A timeout may leave a delivered effect uncertain; replay can duplicate it. Authentication identifies a claimed participant under a trust arrangement, whereas authorization concerns an action. A public commitment can specify an obligation without forcing performance. These distinctions survive whether the implementation uses formal protocols, plain APIs or a direct agreement. The semantic-contract, oracle and replay cases expose the different obligations without mandating a particular notation. [Claims C033–C043 and C109](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [AC14–AC15, AC27–AC30](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

## 6. Correspondence and assurance

The assurance account is a set of bounded warrants. A model checker may establish a proposition for a supported programme model under an encoded environment. A transformation may preserve a specified property between particular metamodels. A test may establish a particular observation under its oracle. None alone establishes that every real environmental state was represented, that physical effects match abstract actions, or that the requirement is legitimate. Conversely, a valid theorem does not become false merely because its application premises are missing. [Claims C058–C061 and C081–C098](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [15 formal-result reassessments](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

The native formal records retain their different model classes: AJPF and supported agent-language semantics, declarative GOAL reasoning, goal-plan path/edge analysis, compositional DESIRE, information-protocol histories, timed BDI, decision-logic verification with environmental abstractions, environment-enabled CAN, ATL coalition strategies and partial design/protocol consistency checks. The author did not rerun their proofs. Selected primary inspection of ADELFE addresses a quoted quantifier and its inference; it is not an audit of the original theorem's proof. ATL synthesis also must not be conflated with unconstrained LLM plan generation just because both produce plans. [Formal records FM001–FM015](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Oracle adequacy is central. A final balance can match the target while a consent condition was violated. An internal success flag can denote plan completion while the intended external goal remains unachieved. A correct tool return can nevertheless be adequate evidence when its contract actually warrants the relevant effect; an additional independent implementation is not universally necessary. In AC27–AC28, the declared criterion includes both balance and consent, so the simpler final-state-only test is exposed as insufficient. [Claims C048, C087 and C098](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Liveness requires particular care. A finite trace that is still pending does not refute eventual discharge. AC18 explicitly returns insufficient evidence for such a prefix. AC19 supplies a finite graph whose admitted infinite pending cycle refutes universal eventuality without an added fairness premise. AC20 removes that cycle through an explicitly bounded-service transition structure and supports eventuality only for that model. Neither result certifies a real scheduler. This is a faithful additional representation of the native safety/liveness distinction. [C023](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [case predicates and executions](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Evidence can also be reused selectively. AC16 changes a tax-model premise and invalidates its dependent warrant while an adequate independently premised receipt survives. AC17 removes the remaining support and yields insufficient evidence, not a false conclusion. A changed premise is therefore neither an excuse to keep all old evidence nor a reason to discard everything. The native C103 already states that distinction. [C103](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [AC16–AC17](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

## 8. Operations and generated planning

Deployment requires actual configuration, interfaces, effect semantics and capable consumers. A log that never reaches someone able to act does not close a consequential feedback loop. That consumer can be one person, a direct conversation or an existing automated mechanism. Likewise, a change can preserve much of the previous evidence when its material premises remain valid. These cheap paths are integral to the native study and survive requalification. [Claims C099–C111](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Long-running plan generation illustrates the boundary between architecture and guarantee. Ciatto and colleagues recommend suspending the requesting intention while others continue; the inspected evaluation is a small four-model, twenty-run grid-world study, with task success and plan-alignment measures that do not coincide. The source does not establish a hard response deadline. AC23 shows that other work can still miss its deadline when the declared scheduler service is late. AC24 allows timely other work while refusing a plan whose authority was revoked before it became ready. Nonblocking design, scheduling, authority and outcome acceptance are separate premises. [NEW-A06](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [C130](SOURCES.md#ama-l3); [AC23–AC24](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).

Human intervention also has a physical and temporal boundary. Approval is not cancellation; a pause signal does not demonstrate that an already dispatched effect has stopped. Moving an effect after approval does not by itself prove crash-safe exactly-once execution. Stable identifiers and explicit uncertain-effect reconciliation can prevent some replay failures, but only under their executor contract. These are inherited operational distinctions, not claims that every application needs an elaborate new supervisory apparatus. [Claims C039, C109–C110](SOURCES.md#ama-l3).
