A narrow positive witness can support an existential claim without certifying a complete interval or every obligation. By contrast, absence throughout a wait needs adequate coverage of that interval. The original checker’s complete-trace requirement remains a fact about that checker; it is not universalized into a requirement for every narrower positive claim. [Current guard and interpretation nuance](SOURCES.md#ama-s), [saved-observation reconciliation](SOURCES.md#ama-s).

A wrong task, actor, version or episode does not qualify the selected memory. Generated success alone stays unverified.
