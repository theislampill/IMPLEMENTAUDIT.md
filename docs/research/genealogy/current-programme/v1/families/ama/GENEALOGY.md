## Autonomous Agents: plural mechanisms and conditional continuity

The historical source follows bounded rationality and resource-aware decision making; symbolic planning and its execution interfaces; behavior-based situated control; practical reasoning, persistent goals and BDI; uncertain-state decision making; adaptive interfaces and mixed initiative; reinforcement learning; and later language-mediated agency and assurance questions. These lines overlap without forming one inevitable sequence. A situated controller need not instantiate a BDI architecture; a BDI interpreter does not obtain an unlimited assurance theorem merely by inheriting a formal vocabulary. [L1 source-grounded reader](AMA-L1.md)

Criticism changes the inheritance: representation has a cost, memoryless reaction has a scope, intentions can become obsolete, feedback can be wrong, and a nominal human supervisor can be unable to act. The relevant predicates concern identifiable observations/actions, legitimate goals/delegation, grounded effects and bounded revision. Exact source editions and inspected passage limits remain attached to their historical records. A later source correction belongs to the accepted current study and is not smuggled into the historical record.

## Multi-Agent Systems: distributed problems and public responsibilities

The historical field joins several problems: task connection, interacting partial plans, cooperative persistence, communication meaning, public commitments, strategic allocation, organization, distributed constraints, argumentation and partner-dependent learning. Contract Net, Partial Global Planning and intentional teamwork have related distributed-AI ancestry, while economic impossibility and social-choice results retain their imported model assumptions. STEAM is a documented selective hybrid. Public-commitment criticism changes what an external observer can reasonably test about opaque participants; it does not invalidate every inspectable internal mental-state model. [L2 source-grounded reader](AMA-L2.md)

Genealogical contact is not algorithmic derivation or independent replication. Related programmes, editions and datasets remain dependent where the original records say so. A contemporary multi-agent framework's vocabulary does not establish that it implements the older mechanism, and an internal coordination result does not automatically establish collective external fulfillment.
