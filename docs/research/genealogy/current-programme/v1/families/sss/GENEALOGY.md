## Architecture: consequential structure, descriptions and revision

Architecture draws on abstraction, decomposition and information hiding; program families and reuse; conceptual integrity; component/connector styles; multiple views and architectural languages; scenario evaluation; decision knowledge; conformance and adaptation. These strands ask different questions. Parnas's modularisation account protects against selected changes rather than prescribing maximum decomposition. Program-family uses relations differ from call relations. Perry and Wolf's elements, form and rationale and the component/connector-style tradition make system-level structure explicit. [Historical Architecture intake and exact source locators](SSS-L1.md).

Description approaches answer stakeholder and communication needs. Kruchten permits unnecessary views to be omitted. A description standard does not certify design quality. Scenario methods and quality tactics require a concern, mechanism and observable response. Architectural mismatch exposes incompatible assumptions in reusable parts. Formal connector work gives bounded model results; conformance work requires a mapping to actual relations. Later adaptation adds valid observation, allowed/effective action and observed consequence. The public historical account therefore supports loops and revision rather than a universal serial method.

## Maintenance and Evolution: purpose, understanding, change and continuation

The maintenance genealogy combines purpose classifications, activity models and population-level evolution with comprehension, reverse engineering, configuration, transformation, regression testing, migration, economics and ecosystem support. Its origins are anchors for particular ideas, not a claim that maintenance began at one inspected paper. The canonical reconciliation retains disagreements and source identities across two frozen studies. [Historical canonical intake and source map](SSS-L2.md).

Theory and practical understanding explain why useful continued execution and the ability to change can diverge. Reverse engineering, refactoring and migration distinguish observation, transformation and actual effects. Testing traditions make suite and oracle premises explicit. Economic and ecosystem work broadens the frame to cost bearers, willing capacity, support and retirement. Contemporary learned/noncode systems expand the artifacts and assumptions that can matter, while preserving the older need to bind a claim to its actual subject and evidence.

This history does not create an obligation to keep growing a program or modernising a still-adequate system. A stable product, bounded repair, restricted servicing or purpose-specific archive can be legitimate. The canonical 105-property selection is distinct from the 98- and 74-row ancestor studies and their separately reconciled bibliographies.

## Product Line Engineering: commonality, variability and product realization

The product-line genealogy includes component reuse, program families, domain analysis, feature modelling, industrial production and formal construction/analysis branches. FODA joins feature distinctions with domain analysis and rationale. Industrial approaches connect assets, products and management; domain and application responsibilities communicate in both directions. Proactive, reactive and extractive approaches offer different adoption routes. [Historical Product Line intake and source map](SSS-L3.md).

Feature-language and constraint analysis exposes model-level inconsistency. Derivation and assurance still need the realised product and actual environment. Testing and test evolution must preserve their coverage and oracle limits. Product-focused approaches and industrial cloning experience challenge universal up-front platform commitments. Dynamic variation creates transition and control questions beyond static family membership. Later interchange, community, learned-assistance and regeneration work remains heterogeneous; source availability or a benchmark does not establish lifecycle superiority.

## Joining traditions without erasing their predicates

| Historical contribution | Related receiving question | Required separation |
|---|---|---|
| Architectural dependency or interface assumption | Which change or product can be affected? | A real dependency needs typed meaning and scope; names or co-occurrence are insufficient |
| Maintenance obligation and preserved behavior | What must continue to hold for supported consumers? | Intended outcome, observation and legitimate authority are distinct |
| Product configuration and realization | Which actual products contain or exercise the changed asset? | Model validity and requested selection do not establish actual derived behavior |
| Conformance or model result | Can a result be reused for this baseline and observer? | Model correctness and realised applicability need separate evidence |
| Support and retirement decision | Can an asset or temporary arrangement end? | Declared closure does not discharge residual consumers or preserved-use promises |

The synthesis is a fourth study of such relations. It is not a fourth constituent lineage or a chronological culmination that makes all previous mechanisms universally necessary. The [public synthesis account](SSS-S.md) retains guarded directions and alternatives. Its accepted current r16 subject is separately identified, and its detailed derived findings remain privately prepared pending disclosure.
