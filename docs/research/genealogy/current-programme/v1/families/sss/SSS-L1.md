## Concrete discriminators and alternatives

Reusable components can bring incompatible assumptions about their environment or interaction. Their individual success does not settle the assembled system. A purportedly isolated service can still share capacity or state. The appropriate inquiry follows the relevant interaction and failure containment; it does not assume isolation from a box boundary. The historical mismatch case and deployment accounts support this distinction at their own scopes. [Historical public intake][intake].

Formal connector analysis has a model-relative guarantee under its stated well-formedness and compatibility premises. Compatibility alone is insufficient. The implementation and environmental correspondence must survive before the result is used to support a realised claim. A model's continued validity need not be denied when the unsupported part is its application. Runtime adaptation adds observation, model, permitted action, actual execution and observed consequence; a functioning loop or improved local score is not the whole obligation.
