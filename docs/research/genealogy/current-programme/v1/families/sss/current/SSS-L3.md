## A warranted product family

Product Line Engineering begins with an intended population of related products and stakeholders. The question is whether commonality, variability and coordinated production or maintenance yield a justified advantage for that population. A collection of branches, a feature tree or a generator is not sufficient evidence of a useful family. The account distinguishes the family, its models, shared assets, derived products and the organisation or ecosystem that sustains them. Each has a different predicate. A satisfiable feature assignment can be wrong for the stakeholder need; a valid model can be incorrectly realised; a correctly derived product can still fail its acceptance criterion. [Accepted report](SOURCES.md#s-801c9d567f3c3843), [native property ledger](SOURCES.md#s-96ca3965ea335545).

The family boundary therefore needs real products or intended products, useful commonality and variation, and an economic or assurance reason to coordinate them. Scope and admission decisions should follow actual consumers and obligations. A small configuration file, selective reuse or deliberate traceable clone-and-own can be adequate. A full platform is not automatically better because it represents more variability. This conditional family warrant prevents the lineage from becoming a universal productisation programme.

## Models, binding and actual derivation

Feature models require an explicit interpretation and a supported language. A SAT result establishes satisfaction of the encoded constraints, not adequacy of those constraints or behavior of a generated artifact. Richer model languages and later binding times can add expressiveness while increasing analysis, configuration and operational burdens. There is no universal one-feature/one-module mapping. A feature can crosscut implementation structure; one asset can support several feature combinations; noncode assets can affect the realised product. [62-property source reader](properties/SSS-L3.md).

Derivation needs the actual configuration, assets, tool versions and baseline identities relevant to the promised result. Reusing a generator label does not establish identical inputs or output. Reproducibility asks whether the stipulated derivation relation holds; correctness asks whether the realised product satisfies its criterion. A product can reproduce the same defect perfectly. Conversely, a changed artifact may be equivalent under the intended observer even when byte identity differs. The demanded equivalence should match the purpose rather than silently switch between bytes, structure and behavior.

Fixed configuration is not fixed runtime input, state or environment. ESPLE-062 preserves that boundary. A valid family model therefore cannot establish universal behavior across environmental conditions it does not represent. Runtime selection introduces observation, plant, control and reachability conditions that static selection does not discharge. Earlier binding, restart, a fixed deployment or a manual controlled action can avoid unnecessary runtime machinery when the actual need allows it.

## Assurance and the limits of coverage

Shared assurance transfers only when its premises cover the actual product, assets, configuration and environment. A family-level result can leave residual product checks. A valid selected assignment is not proof that a generator produced that assignment, nor proof that the product's observable behavior is acceptable. The current qualification keeps these stages separate so that a failure in one stage does not falsely refute a true result at another. [Accepted property requalification](SOURCES.md#s-3d80ff8cac32a89a).

Sampling is a finite resource choice under a declared risk and coverage rationale. Pairwise coverage can miss a consequential three-way combination; the tiny `111` discriminator shows the logical gap. Its force is that local coverage does not guarantee a particular untested joint behavior. It does not require exhaustive testing of every possible product in every setting. A known critical triple can be added, a small space can be exhaustively checked, the supported domain can be restricted, or a different adequate realization can remove the fault. The response should be justified by the consequence and premises, not by a universal coverage slogan.

Formal analyses and actual observation play complementary roles. A theorem within the model can remain true while an implementation or mapping assumption fails. A supervisory result about nonblocking does not establish inevitable realised progress, and neither creates a right to execute a runtime change. The accepted finite controls discriminate the claim boundaries; they are not field demonstrations of product-line effectiveness or comprehensive validation of every formal source.

## Costs, governance and legitimate alternatives

The economic comparison includes setup, ongoing maintenance, coordination, variability management, uncertainty and retirement horizon. No universal number of products guarantees break-even. A short-lived or heterogeneous population can favor selective reuse or clones; recurring related production with stable useful assets can favor a managed family. Existing investment is a sunk cost, not a veto on a better prospective arrangement. Costs and benefits can fall on different product owners, making coordination and scope assumptions material. [Report, economic and organisational qualification](SOURCES.md#s-801c9d567f3c3843).

Model permissions and organisational authority remain separate. An allowed feature combination does not authorise an actor to deploy or alter someone else's product. Independently owned ecosystem participants may accept a common interface without delegating universal release control. A production plan must therefore identify the actual actors, decisions, assets and consumers needed for the promised result. A small negotiated boundary can be sufficient; the research does not require a central platform authority or board in every family.

Automation is a mechanism, not the definition of a product line. AI-generated candidates still need the relevant model, realization and product evidence. ESPLE-048's claim that regeneration is superior over a maintained lifecycle remains unresolved. A promising prototype or successful regeneration does not answer multi-release comprehension, regression, support, cost or governance questions. Conventional generation, maintained assets and bounded assistance remain valid comparators; the unresolved claim does not establish a universal human-only constraint.
