## A justified difference over time

Maintenance and evolution concerns a software system that continues to matter while its needs, environment, understanding or support conditions change. Its first question is what difference is intended and legitimately required. Its second is what the actual operation changes and preserves. Its third is whether the consequences, consumer obligations, cost and capacity remain supportable. These are different inquiries: classification is useful when it guides a decision, but a universal exhaustive taxonomy does not itself answer them. [Accepted report](SOURCES.md#s-6a77d9d67fc95ffc), [selected canonical properties](SOURCES.md#s-ea758478a921e273).

C001's intended difference and legitimate requirement authority remain distinct from C094's authority to execute an action. One actor may legitimately hold both powers; the distinction does not create a mandatory additional approver. A descriptive observation can show what happened but cannot create a right to require or perform it. Likewise, a candidate that changes something useful may exceed the intended task. Correctly identifying the operational effect and the authority boundary prevents “maintenance” from functioning as an unbounded permission label.

The accepted qualification types claims as normative criteria, model guarantees, heuristics, empirical hypotheses, rejected universals or open questions. They are not a homogeneous set of Boolean rules. A criterion needs a legitimate purpose; a theorem needs its premises; a heuristic needs a context and discriminating alternative; an empirical hypothesis needs evidence at the claimed population. The [105 qualification records](SOURCES.md#s-57eaa157d70bb5de) state these roles and retain the original native values rather than silently replacing them with the later interpretation.

## Genealogy and the meaning of change

The canonical genealogy reconciles 17 thematic nodes and 40 adjudicated edges, preserving the two input packets and their field variants. Twenty current accounts reassess the relevant traditions; the genealogy treats chronology, thematic convergence and documented influence separately. The reconciliation is not an assertion that every predecessor statement was identical, or that a null value means the source had no account. [Genealogy qualification](SOURCES.md#s-ac59ae740ce7f390), [native reconciliation](SOURCES.md#s-96cb252cfad032fa).

Lehman's work concerns defined populations and historically revised laws. It does not prove that every useful program must continuously gain features. A fixed, finite computation that remains adequate under its actual conditions is a legitimate no-change case. Naur's theory-building account explains how losing the people and understanding needed to change a program can degrade evolution capacity without making its current execution stop. Claims about restoring that capacity remain conditional and disputed; neither preservation of source nor disappearance of its original authors settles the question alone.

Chikofsky and Cross distinguish reverse engineering, redocumentation, design recovery, restructuring and reengineering by their actual operations and aims. Reverse engineering is observational; redocumentation can express the same abstraction level; design recovery uses additional domain or theoretical knowledge; restructuring preserves the relevant behavior while changing structure; reengineering can change the system. Labels do not override what an operation does. A source-preserving read-only analysis differs from a transformation that happens to preserve a selected external behavior. This is why the r4 observer-role clarification matters: the criterion's observer is a semantic role for distinguishing behavior, not automatically a person exercising rights.

## Preservation, transformation and adequacy

The lineage distinguishes five questions that are often collapsed. C030 states the behavior-preservation rule under a declared observer and model. C031 asks whether the transformation rule applies to the actual input and conditions. C032 concerns the transformation engine's implementation. C033 concerns the particular candidate produced. C034 concerns whether doing the task is worth its cost. A program compiling after a change does not establish all five. A valid transformation theorem can survive a faulty engine; a correct engine can be used outside a rule's premises; a behavior-preserving candidate can still be economically unnecessary. [Canonical predicates and current interpretation](SOURCES.md#s-57eaa157d70bb5de).

A transformation can change operative source while preserving the behavior observed by an explicitly chosen observer. That does not make the operation read-only. It also does not imply preservation under every possible observer. Structural and behavioral dimensions must therefore remain separate. The r4 revisions correct neighbouring accounts and explanation fields without erasing their native raw values, so that an actual structure-changing, behavior-preserving operation can be described accurately.

Regression selection C040 is safe relative to an existing nonobsolete suite and controlled premises about dependencies and test behavior. It does not make the suite a complete specification, cover unknown runtime dependencies, or establish correctness under a changed environment. Retesting the full suite can be cheaper and more robust than maintaining a selector. Conversely, a finite exhaustive test under a correct stipulated oracle can establish the intended finite result. Rejecting the inference from arbitrary sampled successes to universal correctness does not reject testing itself.

## Transitions, recovery and compatibility

C049 concerns recovery of actual effects, whereas C061 concerns reverting source. A source revert cannot recover information destroyed by a noninjective conversion unless the needed information was retained elsewhere. Reopening the old code path is therefore different from restoring the old system state. A feasible inverse, retained backup, containment, forward repair or legitimate acceptance of residual loss can be the appropriate route. The lineage does not prescribe rollback in every case. [Report, state/recovery and compatibility analysis](SOURCES.md#s-6a77d9d67fc95ffc).

Compatibility is indexed to supported observers, versions, time and reachable mixtures. Two correct endpoints can have an unsafe intermediate state. A rolling change must account for the actual combinations that can occur, including schema, application, configuration and shared-resource states. The F1 example's adjacent-schema rules, leases, write fencing and backfill work under particular premises. Quiescence does not by itself establish atomicity. The r2 explanation corrected the scope of a claim without pretending to replace the protocol or demonstrate a more general transition theorem.

The same discipline applies to staged exposure. A candidate/control comparison needs actual isolation, representative observations and a consumer able to intervene within the legitimate bounds. Shared data or capacity can contaminate the comparison while visible acknowledgements remain identical. A cheap offline check, a quiescent transition or deferral can be preferable when a live staging arrangement cannot discriminate the relevant effect. The minimum mechanism follows the actual risk and information need rather than the popularity of a deployment practice.

## Economy, support and end of life

Maintenance economics compares prospective tasks, costs, cost bearers and horizons. A universal zero-debt rule is unwarranted: a short remaining service horizon can make retaining a known liability rational when repair costs exceed the justified benefit and live obligations remain met. Sunk effort does not establish future value. A release label, repository popularity or number of commits does not establish actual support capacity. The relevant condition is willing and capable people, usable knowledge, tools, validation time, coordination and a feasible response route. [Report, economy and capacity](SOURCES.md#s-6a77d9d67fc95ffc).

Deprecation signalling, phaseout, actual shutdown and custody of residual consumers are separate events. An announced sunset is not a guarantee that supported use has ended. Retirement may require transfer of data authority, support or recovery functions even when new feature work has stopped. Purpose-specific preservation distinguishes retaining source, retrieving an artifact, explaining historical behavior and reproducing an execution. Each promise needs the corresponding evidence; indefinite live reconstruction is not universally required.

AI-assisted work adds candidate, tool, benchmark, task-alignment, work-outcome and authority distinctions. A benchmark result can support a bounded capability without showing durable net maintainability or unattended lifecycle stewardship. General durable net maintainability C095 and general unattended maintenance C100 remain unresolved. This does not rule out bounded assistance, deterministic tools or useful human-and-tool arrangements. It rejects promotion of a narrower success into an unestablished whole-lifecycle capability.

## Composition, induced properties and alternatives

The current composition contains 83 typed relations and eleven configurations. Four induced canonical properties, C102–C105, sharpen existing failure modes: evidence/action coherence across an actual handoff; selective invalidation when a relevant premise changes; obligations over reachable consequential joint states; and assessment of a combined control arrangement's protection, consumers, continuation and burden. Three other candidates are absorbed by existing meanings and six obligations remain open. These findings do not make a new permanent installation mandatory. A narrower existing practice or direct adequate check can supply the function. [Composition qualification](SOURCES.md#s-58bab4dd09e91646).

The eleven configurations preserve meaningful alternatives: a stable unchanged system, a small repair, transformation, staged release, concurrent evolution, incremental migration, a rehearsed quiescent transition, phaseout and custody, bounded automation, learned noncode adaptation, and restricted servicing. The choice depends on the task and premises. An older supported baseline can be adequate; a no-change conclusion can be legitimate; a family-wide migration can be unjustified. A known unmet obligation changes that assessment, while a descriptive change in fashion does not.

C105 is particularly important to the synthesis. It evaluates what the combined arrangement actually protects and whom it serves, together with prospective cost and continuation conditions. Removing a duplicated check can be appropriate; removing an independent sensor or coverage condition cannot be justified merely because two checks have the same name or similar output. Protection is established before cheaper feasible alternatives are compared. This is a criterion for a bounded decision, not a global optimiser and not a universal demand to reduce control work.
