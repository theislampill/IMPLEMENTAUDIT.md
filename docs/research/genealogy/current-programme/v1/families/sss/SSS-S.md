## Three kinds of judgment in one change

Architecture asks which consequential structure and relation matters to a concern. Maintenance asks what the change must achieve or preserve and how its effects and continuing obligations will be handled. Product Line Engineering asks which related products justify shared mechanisms, which configurations are allowed and what each selected product actually realises. Shared vocabulary does not make these equivalent predicates. A valid feature assignment need not be a correct product; a sound local architectural model need not justify a maintenance action; a release boundary need not end older supported uses. [Historical lineage accounts](README.md#four-units-and-their-populations).

For a concrete shared change, an architectural dependency may supply part of an impact account, and a product-realization map may identify the affected variants. Maintenance supplies the supported obligation, intended difference and relevant future action. The composite claim needs a common actual artifact or mapped relation, baseline, observer, time and receiving consumer. A partial input can be useful without satisfying the whole receiving predicate. A missing bridge leaves that application unsupported; it does not automatically falsify every local result.

## Directions, reverse evidence and higher-order effects

The existing public record describes guarded directions and legitimate configurations. Direction matters because the output useful to one receiving decision can differ from the output needed in the reverse decision. A product failure can expose a missing family constraint. A consumer's actual inability to use a proposed migration can reveal a missing architectural distinction. These returns require their own observations; they do not arise merely by reversing an earlier arrow. [Historical composition sources](SOURCE_MAP.json).

Pairwise compatibility is also insufficient for every whole-system claim. Individual components or model fragments can be acceptable while their shared assumptions, resource demands or transition states are incompatible. That calls for evidence at the relevant joint scope, not a universal demand for every conceivable combination. A smaller supported domain, direct check, adequate local alternative or omission of an unnecessary mechanism can satisfy the actual need.

## Alternatives, negative results and novelty

No product-family mechanism, a stable configuration, a direct observation, selective reuse, a maintained clone, substitution, narrower support or explicit retirement can be legitimate. The choice depends on the actual obligation, feasible means, costs, consumers and authority. A control arrangement can be too elaborate as well as too weak; omission is justified only when its relevant trigger or protected obligation is absent or adequately served elsewhere.
