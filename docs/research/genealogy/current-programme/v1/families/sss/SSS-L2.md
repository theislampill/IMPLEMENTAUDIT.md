## Maintenance as a continuing obligation

Maintenance concerns useful existing software, its consumers, the environment and the people and resources able to sustain it. It includes preserving behavior, intentionally changing behavior, restructuring, migration, replacement, restricted support and retirement. Noncode artifacts matter when they affect the real obligation. A document can aid maintenance without transferring the actual ability to maintain. The intended outcome, evidence and capable legitimate actor must fit the task. [Historical public intake][intake].

The genealogy contains related lenses rather than one complete taxonomy: purpose and activity classifications, population-level evolution, comprehension and reverse engineering, configuration and refactoring, regression testing, migration, economics and ecosystem support. The canonical reconciliation preserves disagreements and source versions. Its 98- and 74-row ancestor populations are not added to the 105 canonical identities; equal source counts in the ancestors do not imply identical bibliographies. ## The distinctions that make a change assessable

Understanding is not the same as changing. A correct refactoring rule does not prove its implementation correct, its application valid for the actual input, or the resulting candidate correct under an excluded observer. Cleaner structure or a better metric need not improve useful future change. The historical property records retain these distinct bearer, criterion, applicability and economic questions. [Canonical properties][ledger].

Existing behavior is not automatically desired behavior, and passing tests is not a complete specification. Differential agreement, metamorphic relations and mutation evidence have distinct assumptions. Test selection and prioritisation concern cost or detection timing; they do not change what a pass proves. A direct check or the full relevant suite can be cheaper than an elaborate selector. Evidence should answer the intended change question and preserve the relevant exclusions. [Historical public intake, tests and observations][intake].

Recovery follows actual effects. Reverting source may leave data loss, external effects or concurrent changes untouched. Safe coexistence is different from recoverability, and neither alone establishes that a temporary migration is finished. A repair, incremental coexistence, prepared cutover, forward recovery or legitimate scope reduction can be appropriate under different premises. The composition provides branches and feedback, not a compulsory serial pipeline. [Historical composition model][composition].

## Support, economics and meaningful non-change

Useful non-change is legitimate. Evolution hypotheses are not instructions to grow; age is not a defect; modernization has no benefit merely because it is newer. A proposed intervention must justify its outcome, uncertainty, cost and future support burden. Technical debt is a way to reason about prospective burden and value, not a universally calibrated balance that must be reduced to zero. The beneficiary and cost bearer can differ. [Historical public intake, operating idea and economics][intake].

A nominal owner needs willingness, knowledge, access and capacity. A message must reach an appropriate consumer able to act. Deprecation, sunset and archival preservation make different promises; an inactive completed project need not be a failed one. Source retrieval, continued service and historical execution reproduction need different evidence. A popular dependency or a repository label does not establish a feasible support response. [Historical canonical support and preservation properties][ledger].

AI assistance and automated repair produce candidates and evidence, not self-authorising decisions. Net benefit depends on task alignment, quality, verification cost and consequential outcomes. A review step is not automatically sufficient, and the opposite universal claim that human-only work is superior is also unsupported. Different benchmarks, versions, surveys and selected follow-ups must not be pooled as a single representative experiment. Long-term maintainability benefit and general unattended maintenance remain unresolved historical questions. [Historical public intake, automation and unsettled questions][intake].

## Composition, adverse records and limits

The historical canonical composition retains four analytically induced criteria: coherence among change, evidence and action; selective invalidation of changed assumptions; continuity over relevant reachable joint states; and proportionality/retirement of combined controls. Their parents and finite failure witnesses are explicit. Existing adequate mechanisms can discharge them; they are not four mandatory new installations or a global optimiser. The corpus preserves rejected claims, ceremonies, a superseded form, contested and unresolved claims, together with open composition questions. [Historical composition model][composition] · [All properties and ancestry pointers](SSS-L2-HISTORICAL-PROPERTIES.md).

The canonical reconciliation inspected frozen research records. It did not claim to reread every primary work or reproduce the underlying studies. Historical, formal, empirical and analytical evidence retain separate scopes and dependence. Integrity establishes correspondence and custody, not independent truth. The present historical reader does not turn the later accepted r4 corrections into public material, and it does not derive adoption requirements for IMPLEMENTAUDIT.
