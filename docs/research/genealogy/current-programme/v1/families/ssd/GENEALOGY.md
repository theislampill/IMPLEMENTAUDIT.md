## Three distinct traditions

The existing public lineage explanations preserve plural origins and criticism:
Systems Engineering concerns intended use, interfaces and configuration;
Systems Security Engineering concerns protected consequences, authority and
trust; Decision and Operations Engineering concerns defensible action,
resources and uncertainty. Their later synthesis must establish actual guarded
connections. Shared vocabulary and co-use alone do not prove direct ancestry,
influence or a material relation.

Historical original/evolved wording is a source claim at that freeze. The
accepted corrected genealogy is retained privately, including typed relations,
criticisms and access limits. It is not inferred from the shape of this public
family table. See [L1](SSD-L1.md), [L2](SSD-L2.md), [L3](SSD-L3.md) and
[S009](SSD-S.md) for exact subject and review identities.

## Missing history is not failed current science

The three original executed commissions remain unauthenticated in the received
evidence. Synthesis histories include separately identified original/isolated/
owner subjects and some missing-body or descriptor-only limits. Those limits
must remain visible. They neither authenticate an absent original by analogy
nor cancel a separately accepted newer study. Later acceptance belongs to its
exact frozen subject and cannot be projected backwards onto an earlier
GAP_REVISE receipt.

## Public and private continuation

Four disclosure decisions govern four separate scientific units. Their full
scientific text and exact byte scopes are preserved privately. No branch
publication permission is treated as approval of those interpretations. A
future approved public projection must preserve the current selector and
retained scientific limitations, including the S009 derivative-summary failure,
and receive affected documentary review. Until then the authorized historical
and metadata account and the incomplete public current science are assessed
separately in [COMPLETENESS](COMPLETENESS.md).
