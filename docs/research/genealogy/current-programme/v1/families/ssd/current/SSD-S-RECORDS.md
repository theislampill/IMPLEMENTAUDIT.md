## SYN-C01 — Admissibility precedes comparison

**Claim id.** SYN-C01

**Title.** Admissibility precedes comparison

**Proposition.** For a particular operational decision, the live alternatives must meet the actual authority, hard consequence and physical feasibility conditions before a preference or optimization result can recommend one of them.

**Parents.** L1-P001; L1-P003; L1-P009; L1-P019; L1-P020; L2-P04; L2-P06; L2-P17; L2-P49; L3-P001; L3-P003; L3-P005; L3-P008; L3-P018; L3-P016; L2-P26; L3-P062

**Bearer.** A named commitment or exact effect, its affected parties and actual available alternatives.

**Guards.** The mission criterion, constraint status and relevant authority are established at their real scope. Model feasibility and physical feasibility are distinguished. The ranking is interpreted only within its represented values. U/V branches require the same actual input/model/purpose and the explicit native role assessments; a partial relevant contribution is sufficient for materiality but does not establish all other premises. Current native use is bound in R5_NATIVE_ROLE_ASSESSMENT.json and R5_NATIVE_EVIDENCE_CONTRACTS.json. An already-required and actually used native map is a legitimate bearer for a scoped coverage/model-use ceiling; this does not prove identity, current grant or physical completion. Any received F condition remains an actual hard criterion, not validation evidence or a demand for a richer model.

**Cases.** CASE01; CASE08; CASE10; CASE22; CASE32; CASE34; CASE43; CASE49; CASE51
