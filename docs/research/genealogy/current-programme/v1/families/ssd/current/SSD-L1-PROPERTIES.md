## P001 — Ground stakeholder and mission intent in authoritative, current evidence

**Property id.** P001

**Property name.** Ground stakeholder and mission intent in authoritative, current evidence

**Historical origin.** Hall's problem-definition and goal-setting tradition; NASA stakeholder-expectations practice; requirements-engineering work on environment and agreement.

**Lineage class.** DIRECT_SYSTEMS_ENGINEERING_LINEAGE

**Tradition relation.** SYSTEMS_ENGINEERING_NATIVE

**Original form.** Identify customer objectives and translate them into system goals before detailed design.

**Problem it addressed.** Component teams can optimise a technically impressive solution that does not solve the governing mission or current stakeholder problem.

**Failure mode it tries to prevent.** Solution success is asserted against an unauthorised, stale or internally invented notion of value.

**Mechanism.** Record the intended outcomes, sources, decision authority, conflicts, use context, assumptions and current validation evidence; make downstream decisions point back to that governed intent.

**Trigger or context.** Multiple stakeholders; costly or irreversible design; regulated/contractual commitments; uncertain mission; long lifecycle; changed operating context.

**Non trigger or cheap path.** For a small reversible task with one empowered user and immediate feedback, a dated goal statement plus direct confirmation is enough.

**Dependencies or preconditions.** Access to representative stakeholders or mission evidence; explicit system boundary and intended lifecycle; a means to resolve or preserve disagreement.

**Stakeholder or mission preconditions.** The governing need, stakeholder classes, intended use and current context must be identifiable; latent/absent stakeholders are a declared limitation.

**Authority preconditions.** A named person, body, law, contract or mission authority must be able to legitimise or change the governing intent; facilitation is not authority.

**Configuration or identity preconditions.** The intent record, source and applicable system/configuration/time horizon must be versioned.

**Interface or integration preconditions.** External actors, environments and ecosystem dependencies that affect intent must be within scope.

**Evidence or vv preconditions.** Validation evidence must be capable of challenging the stated need and intended-use model.

**Known failure modes.** Solution-biased framing; token stakeholder participation; stale authority; proxy outcomes; hidden conflicts; retrospective rationalisation.

**Important criticisms.** Stakeholder workshops and CONOPS documents can create consensus theatre; stated requests may reflect politics, solution bias or absent users.

**How the property evolved.** Original form: Identify customer objectives and translate them into system goals before detailed design. Evolved under criticism into: A living intent model whose authority, recency, conflicts, measures and affected decisions are explicit; revalidate after material change.

**Evolved form in this source.** A living intent model whose authority, recency, conflicts, measures and affected decisions are explicit; revalidate after material change.
