## 4. Ten connected whole-synthesis findings

**SYN-C01 — admissibility before comparison.** A real permission boundary,
noncompensable consequence or physical constraint can exclude an option before
preferences compare feasible alternatives. A high score does not confer a
grant. This does not require a numerical optimizer where a direct constraint
settles the choice. [Synthesis claims, C01.]

**SYN-C02 — claim-specific currentness.** Exact bytes may remain relevant to one
claim and be stale for another. Configuration identity, authority currentness,
semantic correspondence and physical correspondence are separate. A relevant
delta invalidates only the warrant it changes; an unknown dependency stays
unknown instead of forcing universal reuse or universal repetition. [C02.]

**SYN-C03 — error paths and shared workload are different axes.** Independent
challenge concerns actual error pathways, not headcount; shared expert or rig
capacity concerns joint schedulability, not epistemic independence. Adequate
checks individually can be jointly infeasible. The 276 resource guards retain
known coupling while actual capacity remains unknown where not measured.
A common account label alone does not prove a physical resource pool. [C03;
resource guard selection.]

**SYN-C04 — recovery combines function, trust and executable recourse.**
Restored service, closure of known hostile authority and feasible authorized
fallback concern different premises. Known-set closure can be valid while
broader residual containment is restricted by uncertainty. Recovery does not
become complete eradication, and a bounded stateless rebuild may remain a
legitimate adequate path. [C04.]

**SYN-C05 — adequacy and comparative economy are separate.** A mechanism can
meet the stated consequence without being shown cheapest. Comparison includes
transition and lifecycle burdens over the same horizon, with the adequate
incumbent or no change retained. Low consequence or reversibility alone does
not establish a rank. [C05.]

**SYN-C06 — inquiry is scoped and can be complementary.** Information can have
prospective decision value even when the realized observation leaves action
unchanged. Two jointly informative observations can defeat a greedy one-test
stopping rule. Partial evidence or one direct check can also legitimately end
inquiry when it settles the relevant concern. The argument does not waive a
required research commission or claim universal optimal stopping. [C06.]

**SYN-C07 — feedback changes actions and measured populations.** A deployed
policy, metric or incident response may alter the population used to evaluate
it. Incident learning and missingness retain their actual native meanings.
Detection needs a consumer with real response authority and capacity; more
signals do not by themselves establish better control. [C07.]

**SYN-C08 — retirement is role-specific.** Executable grants, verification
material, decryption capability, data and recovery evidence have different
continuing uses. Removing authority need not destroy verification or recovery.
Indiscriminate deletion, universal rotation and unlimited imagined future
retention are not inferred. [C08.]

**SYN-C09 — model validity and application fit differ.** A theorem, bounded
attestation, simulation identity or fixed-domain test can remain correct while
the wider intended application is unknown. The L3 P065 split is retained.
No quantity of run precision or receipt integrity supplies omitted physical,
input-data, population or authorization correspondence. [C09.]

**SYN-C10 — dependence must be interpreted through the actual consequence.**
A common error source can undermine corroboration, while positive covariance
can reduce the variance of a particular matched difference. Commonality is
not uniformly beneficial or harmful. Qualitative common cause does not create
a measured covariance or probability. The estimator and receiving claim fix
which inference is warranted. [C10.]

These are connected guarded conclusions about the selected relationships,
not ten new primitive properties, empirical effect estimates or a theorem
requiring every task to activate all three lineages. Their complete contrary
cases, costs, residuals, invalidation conditions and current S009 reassessments
are in the synthesis reader and original claim object.
