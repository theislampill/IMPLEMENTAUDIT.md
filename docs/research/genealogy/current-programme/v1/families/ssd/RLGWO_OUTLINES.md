# SSD — four rough RLGWO responsibilities

[SSD family](README.md) · [Genealogy](GENEALOGY.md) · [Source map](SOURCE_MAP.md) · [Completeness](COMPLETENESS.md)

These are four distinct research and integration-preparation outlines inside
the existing **32 conceptual responsibility slots**. They are neither four new
issues nor executor-ready work orders. They authorize no source mutation,
issue reopening, campaign, merge or deployment. Current accepted science and
exact-current native implementation must be reconciled separately; protected
interpretation remains private pending its source-specific approval.

## SSD-L1

**Existing owner:** [R002D / #197](https://github.com/theislampill/IMPLEMENTAUDIT.md/issues/197), OPEN at receiving.
**Responsibility:** retain the distinction between intended use, requirements,
interfaces, configuration and evidence when a future current-source check asks
whether a system-level assertion is actually justified.

**Native consumers:** R001F receiver/work-order contracts; R000E/R0024
currentness and evidence anchors; R0022 planning/capacity; R0023 acceptance;
R0020 convergence where genuinely triggered. The exact public unresolved
P007/P012/P032 routes are preserved in [OWNER_MAP](OWNER_MAP.md).

**Discriminator and cheap path:** inspect current source and focused fixtures
first. A correct rejection of stale evidence, missing derivation or unjustified
late integration supports no change. A direct observation, small rationale or
justified destructive/expensive-test deferral can be adequate.

**Possible residual:** only an actual current-tree accepted-but-invalid case
can justify an owner-local amendment. Unknown deployment or real intended-use
fitness cannot be closed by a documentation or schema test. No complete model,
V-method or new systems lane is implied. **Disclosure dependency:** SSD-L1.

**Native consumers:** SECURITY_NATIVE finding/profile, R000C acceptance,
R000D/R0028 independent challenge, R000E–R0010/R0024 identity/currentness,

observed configuration or recovery evidence belongs to its existing owner.
No live offensive testing, credentials, new security mode, scanner campaign or
universal security certification is proposed. **Disclosure dependency:** SSD-L2.
