## Scientific responsibility at the authorized public scope

The historical account concerns protected consequence, threat assumptions, real authority, enforcement, current state, observability and recovery.
Identity, technical ability, authorization and enforcement are different predicates. A signature, inventory or scan supplies only its bounded claim. Successful restoration does not by itself establish trustworthy recovery.

A narrower grant, one exact bounded check, a direct known-component check or an adequate simple recovery path can be legitimate. No universal security workflow, scanner campaign or certification follows.

These statements explain the linked public historical sources; they are not a
summary of undisclosed corrected findings. See the maintained Page's genealogy,
source distinctions, mechanism discussion and complete property reader.
