## What a finding establishes

A property identity is a traceable research claim, not an unconditional instruction. Its predicate states what would have to be true for that claim to apply. Mechanisms explain why a relationship may hold; assumptions and guards restrict the setting in which it was examined. Evidence, counterexamples and final dispositions determine whether the account supports the claim, rejects it, limits it or leaves it unresolved.

Read those parts together. A useful contribution to another discipline may satisfy only part of the receiving predicate. It need not establish the receiver's complete conclusion. A supported A-to-B relationship also supplies no automatic B-to-A result. The direction, configuration and evidence of the reverse relation must be examined separately. The account retains negative and unresolved records so that absence of support cannot be mistaken for a positive transfer.

The synthesis notation **A × B × C** describes an investigation of composition. It is not a numerical product or a promise that every possible combination works. The synthesis accounts explain independent obligations, shared assumptions, configuration effects, incompatibilities, higher-order relationships, alternatives and novelty judgments. An adequate existing control, a simpler solution or a legitimate no-change conclusion is a scientific outcome. A constituent correction or a newly organized explanation is not automatically a new synthesis property.

The detailed records remain alongside the narrative accounts. Repeated context may be stored once and referenced by record identity; such factoring does not alter a direction, remove a guard or convert an unknown result into a supported one. The unit source notes identify the accepted record set, its transformations and the limits of the available evidence. Readers can inspect individual property and interaction results without obtaining a private conversation archive.
