## Evidence boundaries

Historical provenance, formal validity, empirical comparison, field experience, replication, criticism and transfer are separate. Strong theory can coexist with weak deployment evidence (for example configuration logic or supervisory control). Industrial cloning, BSH tasks and Bosch recovery provide useful but setting-bound observations. Learned regeneration is exploratory and unresolved as a replacement. Model/test mutation and warnings are not full product correctness or measured maintenance labour. See the evidence register in the full ledger for units and dependence.

## Inputs a later audit must obtain

Identify the actual products, shared assumptions, intended variation, supported baselines, asset/tool/configuration identity, relevant environment, acceptance obligations, observed failures, ownership and credible alternative costs. Do not fill missing inputs from a feature tree or a familiar label. A missing applicable evidence item may justify “evidence insufficient”; a missing inapplicable mechanism is not a defect.

### ESPLE-001 — Warranted family boundary

**Disposition / kind:** STRONGLY_RETAINED / CRITERION_OR_INVARIANT. **Examination:** EXAMINED_WITH_EVIDENCE_LIMIT.

**Mature form:** State which products and foreseeable needs belong together; test shared domain assumptions against counterexamples before committing to shared production.

**Controlled failure:** Superficially similar products can require incompatible assumptions, release obligations or ownership arrangements.

**Trigger:** A decision to coordinate assets or future variants across more than one product need. **Non-trigger or cheap path:** For incidental shared utilities, use ordinary component reuse or independent products without introducing a family programme.

**Prerequisites:** Product needs, differences, expected future use and who can sustain shared assets.

**Domain profile:** `ESPLE-DP-P1` above; apply its scope, semantics, binding, asset, identity, assurance, evolution, authority and economics fields to this candidate’s stated trigger, mechanism and postcondition. Property-specific retirement/support boundary: Reopen the boundary when required assumptions diverge; dissolve or split the family when coordinated reuse no longer earns its cost.

**Evidence needed from the audited system:** Actual inputs corresponding to Product needs, differences, expected future use and who can sustain shared assets. Then seek evidence of the postcondition: Included and excluded products have reasons; at least one plausible contrary case has been adjudicated; the chosen boundary supports feasible derivation. Merely naming the technique is insufficient.

**Consumer and authority:** Portfolio and domain decision-makers with product representatives; similarity analysis does not authorise merging teams or obligations.

**Criticism and evidential limit:** Economic and market assumptions can be wrong even when the family model is internally precise. Bosch's mixed dependencies also warn against forcing all products into one homogeneous model. Evidence partitions: Absent as a decisive comparative effectiveness estimate for this precise property; examples and guidance are not converted into causal estimates. Contextual field support in the cited industrial or community observations; limited independence and generality.

**Anti-ceremony boundary:** For incidental shared utilities, use ordinary component reuse or independent products without introducing a family programme. Simplification must still preserve the stated postcondition. **Cost:** Scoping and negotiation consume effort before a reusable asset exists.

**Conflicts/alternatives/constraints:** No separate conflict edge is asserted; the candidate’s own prerequisites still apply. **Criticism records:** `ESPLE-C01`, `ESPLE-C18`, `ESPLE-C20`.

**Neutral audit question:** Which shared assumptions warrant this family, and which plausible product would invalidate them? Is the outcome already provided, inapplicable, adequately addressed by a simpler mechanism, or not established by the available evidence?

### ESPLE-003 — Value-bound admission of variability

**Disposition / kind:** RETAINED_IN_EVOLVED_FORM / CRITERION_OR_INVARIANT. **Examination:** EXAMINED.

**Mature form:** Admit a new variation only with a product or credible future need, an implementation boundary, an assurance consequence and an identified support cost.
