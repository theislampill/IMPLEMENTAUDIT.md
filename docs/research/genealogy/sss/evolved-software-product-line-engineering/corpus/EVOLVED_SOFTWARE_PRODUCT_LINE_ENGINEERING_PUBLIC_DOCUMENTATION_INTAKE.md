## Introduction suitable for public documentation

Software Product Line Engineering studies how related software products can be developed through managed commonality and variability, reusable assets and a defined way of deriving particular products. Its history includes component-reuse proposals, program-family design, domain analysis, feature modelling, industrial production practices and multiple implementation and verification branches. The tradition is not simply a collection of feature flags, a product roadmap or an instruction to share every piece of code. [ESPLE-S001; ESPLE-S003; ESPLE-S005; ESPLE-S008; ESPLE-S009; ESPLE-S011]

“Evolved Software Product Line Engineering” is the analytical name used by this independent study for a criticism-tested synthesis of that established field. It is not an invented historical school or a claim of scholarly consensus. The synthesis retains warranted family scope, explicit meaningful variation, controlled product derivation, evidence with stated boundaries, product acceptance and support-aware evolution. It also preserves the choice of independent products or selective reuse when they are adequate and less costly.

## Evolution under criticism

Practical difficulties changed what can responsibly be claimed. Product-focused and incremental approaches challenge mandatory comprehensive upfront investment. Industrial cloning research prevents a blanket prohibition on clone-and-own. Configuration analysis exposes represented inconsistencies but cannot prove omitted domain constraints or correct code generation. Sampling and family analysis provide different bounded evidence, while evolution and runtime reconfiguration add continuity and transition obligations. These responses can narrow a mechanism, replace a ceremony or preserve alternatives; maturity does not always mean more machinery. [ESPLE-S007; ESPLE-S010; ESPLE-S015; ESPLE-S019; ESPLE-S020; ESPLE-S023; ESPLE-S026; ESPLE-S031; ESPLE-S041]

## Composition in plain language

First decide whether the products really warrant a shared production commitment. Keep the meanings of their shared and different capabilities clear. Use assets and a derivation method that actually produce the intended member. Check the resulting product using evidence whose limits are known. Feed product failures and changed needs back into shared knowledge, and evaluate shared changes against supported products. Reconsider investment and remove obsolete variability when its obligations can be discharged.

These are coupled activities, not a forced serial pipeline. Products can expose missing family knowledge; a shared repair can be interrupted by a failing variant; an old baseline can remain supported while a successor develops. Runtime variation adds a separate requirement to control the path between configurations. The smallest coherent implementation may use ordinary modules, a decision table, a versioned configuration and a reliable manual procedure. The synthesis does not require every available modelling or analysis tool.

## Strongest properties and common caricatures

The strongest retained boundaries are warranted family scope, coupled domain/product responsibilities, meaningful feature semantics, correctly scoped constraint diagnostics, explicit assurance boundaries, product acceptance, shared-change impact, adequate alternatives and transition safety when reconfiguration is dynamic. They are bounded obligations, not claims of universal economic returns.

A feature tree alone does not establish controlled variability. A successful build does not establish product correctness. Pairwise testing does not rule out all higher-order faults. A fixed configuration does not remove ordinary input or environmental dependencies. More possible configurations do not automatically mean more useful products. A common core does not confer authority over independent suppliers or communities.

## Criticisms and evidence limits

Published industrial experience is often selected and observational. A small task experiment does not price the whole adoption programme. Many failing configurations may share one fault. Formal results require the model and implementation assumptions under which they were obtained. Benchmarks do not automatically establish deployment safety. Several canonical or current works were available only through excerpts, abstracts or metadata, and those limits remain explicit.

Recent work on interchange, test evolution, community-driven variability, learned assistance and regeneration is relevant but heterogeneous. The stronger lifecycle claim for regeneration remains unresolved in this study. The announced VARIABILITY 2026 conference occurs after the 6 September cut-off and must not be described here as already completed. ## Claims not to make

Do not claim an invented origin or consensus for the analytical label. Do not claim that a feature model proves implementation correctness, that pairwise testing is exhaustive, or that a formal model certifies its environment. Do not turn isolated industrial successes into universal productivity or break-even claims. Do not equate popularity, standardisation or the number of variants with maturity. Do not describe an announced conference as completed. Do not assert that a host system has adopted or implements any property without an independent audit. Do not present learned regeneration as a proved replacement for maintained family engineering.
