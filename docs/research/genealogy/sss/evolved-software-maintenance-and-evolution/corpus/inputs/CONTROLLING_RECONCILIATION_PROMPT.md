- duplicate vs distinct
- domain-specific vs general
- ceremony vs property

For each disagreement:

1. identify whether the difference comes from:

   - different trigger/guard;

   - overgeneralisation in one run;

2. choose the canonical disposition only after weighing the support;
3. preserve the losing judgement and its rationale in the reconciliation ledger;
4. mark unresolved when the evidence does not warrant resolution.

Do not average dispositions.
