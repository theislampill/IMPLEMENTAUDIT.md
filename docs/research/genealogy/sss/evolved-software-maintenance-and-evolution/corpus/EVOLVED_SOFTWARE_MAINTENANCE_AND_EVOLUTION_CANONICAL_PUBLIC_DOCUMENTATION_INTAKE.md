# Evolved Software Maintenance and Evolution: public-documentation intake

**Revision ESME-CANONICAL-R1-2026-09-06; research cut-off 2026-09-06.** This label names an analytical synthesis of established software-maintenance and software-evolution traditions. It is not presented as an existing historical school, professional standard or universally validated method. The canonical account reconciles two independent frozen studies. It contains 105 examined criteria and hypotheses, with 106 bibliographic/resource records, rather than declaring one original study the winner.

## What the account is about

Maintenance is not simply fixing bugs after development ends. Its subject is useful existing software, the obligations it serves, the people and resources that can sustain it, and the environment in which it operates. A maintenance decision can preserve behaviour, intentionally revise behaviour, restructure an implementation, migrate data, replace a subsystem, reduce supported scope or end a service. Non-code artefacts matter when they affect the real obligation. Preparation and transfer matter when someone must actually be able to maintain the result. [ESME-CS001 (A:ESME-S001); ESME-CS006 (A:ESME-S006); ESME-CS013 (A:ESME-S013); ESME-CS041 (A:ESME-S041)]

The historical literature offers several lenses rather than one complete taxonomy. Purpose classifications describe why work is done; activity and multidimensional classifications describe other aspects. Evolution studies examine software populations and feedback over time. Comprehension, reverse engineering, configuration, refactoring, testing, migration, economics and ecosystem work each make different failures visible. The canonical genealogy preserves documented connections while distinguishing resemblance from demonstrated influence. Its origins are historical anchors, not claims that maintenance began with a particular inspected paper. [ESME-CS003 (A:ESME-S003); ESME-CS004 (A:ESME-S004); ESME-CS005 (A:ESME-S005); ESME-CS008 (A:ESME-S008); ESME-CS014 (A:ESME-S014); ESME-CS019 (A:ESME-S019)]

## The central operating idea

The analytical synthesis is to keep a consequential change aligned with the actual supported obligation, subject, evidence and capable authority. Obtain enough understanding to decide the action, not an exhaustive account of everything. Identify what may be affected and which observations should stay the same or intentionally change. Choose evidence and mechanisms adequate for that question. Follow real effects, not merely the presence of documents, tests or approvals. This formulation is the reconciler's composition; it is not a quotation or empirical whole-system performance claim.

These relationships form loops and alternatives, not a fixed pipeline. Discovery may revise the impact boundary. A concurrent edit or changed model may invalidate only part of the evidence. An irreversible effect can remove a recovery option. A sound local repair may make a migration unnecessary. A migration may use incremental coexistence or a prepared cutover, depending on the actual constraints. A control may be omitted when its trigger is absent or retired when its protected obligation ends.

Useful non-change is a legitimate result. Empirical evolution hypotheses are not commands to grow. Legacy value is not proof that all change is wrong. Modernisation is not valuable simply because it is newer. The canonical account asks what outcome, obligation and uncertainty justify an intervention, including its future support burden. ## Distinctions that should not be lost

Understanding a system is not the same as changing it. A document can help without transferring the ability to maintain. A correct refactoring rule is not proof that its implementation is correct, or that the actual output meets a previously excluded observer. Cleaner structure or a better metric does not guarantee useful future change. [ESME-CS014 (A:ESME-S014); ESME-CS016 (A:ESME-S016); ESME-CS022 (A:ESME-S022); ESME-CS046 (A:ESME-S045); ESME-CS076 (B:ESME-S020); ESME-CS078 (B:ESME-S022)]

Tests and observations need interpretation. Existing behaviour is not automatically desired behaviour. Passing tests is not a complete specification. Differential agreement, metamorphic relations and mutation evidence are useful under their own assumptions, not interchangeable correctness certificates. Selection and prioritisation address cost or detection timing; they do not change what a pass means. A simple direct check or running the full relevant suite can be cheaper than an elaborate analysis. [ESME-CS025 (A:ESME-S025); ESME-CS026 (A:ESME-S026); ESME-CS047 (A:ESME-S046); ESME-CS049 (A:ESME-S048); ESME-CS102 (B:ESME-S063); ESME-CS103 (B:ESME-S064)]

Recovery concerns actual effects. Reverting source does not necessarily undo data loss, external actions or concurrent changes. A safe coexistence state is not proof that recovery remains possible, and neither proves the temporary migration is finished. These three distinctions survive even though two originally “composition-induced” candidates were absorbed into already inherited recovery and retirement criteria. [ESME-CS050 (A:ESME-S049); ESME-CS063 (A:ESME-S062); ESME-CS029 (A:ESME-S029)]

Support is more than a repository signal. A nominal owner needs willingness, knowledge, access and capacity. Communication must reach a relevant consumer able to act. Deprecation, sunset and archival preservation make different promises. A finished inactive project need not be a failed project, and a popular dependency need not have dependable support. [ESME-CS032 (A:ESME-S032); ESME-CS084 (B:ESME-S040); ESME-CS085 (B:ESME-S043); ESME-CS054 (A:ESME-S053); ESME-CS055 (A:ESME-S054); ESME-CS056 (A:ESME-S055)]

## Economics and automation without slogans

Technical debt is a way to discuss future burden and value, not a universally calibrated account balance. Deliberate deferral can be sensible; imposing zero debt can spend more than it protects. Costs may fall on different people from those receiving the benefit. A debt register earns its place through a decision and consumer, not its size. [ESME-CS027 (A:ESME-S027); ESME-CS090 (B:ESME-S048); ESME-CS058 (A:ESME-S057); ESME-CS059 (A:ESME-S058)]

Automated repair and AI assistance produce candidates and evidence, not self-authorising decisions. Their benefit depends on candidate quality, task and resource conditions, verification work and consequential outcomes. A human review step is not automatically adequate, but the contrary claim that human-only work is always superior also fails. Evidence from different benchmarks, versions, selected follow-ups and surveys must not be pooled as though it were one representative experiment. [ESME-CS034 (A:ESME-S034); ESME-CS092 (B:ESME-S052); ESME-CS036 (A:ESME-S036); ESME-CS037 (A:ESME-S037); ESME-CS081 (B:ESME-S034); ESME-CS064 (A:ESME-S063); ESME-CS066 (A:ESME-S065)]

Learned and non-code systems widen the artefacts and assumptions that can matter. Data, configuration, prompts, tools, hidden consumers and changed distributions can make yesterday's evidence inapplicable. A policy file is not the same as its operative effect. This is a bounded extension of maintenance questions, not a claim that all systems need a particular AI or policy architecture. [ESME-CS038 (A:ESME-S038); ESME-CS062 (A:ESME-S061); ESME-CS096 (B:ESME-S056)]

## What is analytical and what remains unsettled

Four cross-mechanism criteria are retained as analytically induced: coherence between change, evidence and action; selective invalidation when assumptions change; continuity across relevant reachable joint states; and feedback-driven proportionality and retirement of combined controls. Their parents and failure witnesses are explicit. Existing adequate mechanisms can already discharge them. No comparative field trial of the entire composition is claimed, and no global optimiser or exhaustive state model is prescribed.

The corpus contains sixteen rejected claims, one superseded form, two ceremonies, two unresolved primary questions and one contested primary claim. Two findings remain specifically unresolved: long-term maintainability benefit from AI assistance and general unattended maintenance reliability. Lifecycle servicing irreversibility remains contested. Six further composition questions preserve uncertain cost, scope and practical feasibility. These are research boundaries, not proof of impossibility and not proven best practices.

All original input rows are preserved. Disagreements were not removed to make the public account sound more decisive. The canonical denominator arose from semantic splits, coalescences, adverse-candidate treatment and analytical-property tests. Source work and edition identity was separately reconciled; equal source counts in the original studies did not imply equal bibliographies.

## Limits and permitted downstream use

This reconciliation inspected the frozen research records. It did not re-read every underlying paper, reproduce studies or turn partial source access into full access. The source table preserves exact work, version, original access level, claim locator and evidence boundary. Formal findings remain model-relative; published cases remain case-specific; historical accounts and related datasets retain their dependence. Integrity verification establishes custody and internal consistency, not independent scholarly truth.

The packet is ready for later Evolved-SSS synthesis as one external lineage input. It exports every property, not only favourable ones. It has not compared sibling corpora, performed SSS or cross-trifecta synthesis, evaluated IMPLEMENTAUDIT, derived adoption requirements or changed any repository. A later reader must make those separate judgments with the relevant target evidence and authority.
