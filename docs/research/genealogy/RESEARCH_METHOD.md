# Retained Research Method

The corpus retains criticism-filtered engineering lineages without turning their historical labels into runtime modes. The method separates five evidence objects:

1. historical genealogy and source provenance;
2. a complete candidate-property population;
3. criticism, contrary evidence, assumptions and domain limits;
4. evolved property forms with triggers, non-triggers and engineering payoffs;
5. frozen machine-readable ledgers plus human-readable reports and intake surfaces.

## Denominator discipline

Every frozen property remains in its lineage denominator, including rejected, superseded, ceremony-stripped, domain-bound and unresolved records. Similar properties across lineages are not silently merged. The master index therefore uses `<LINEAGE_ID>::<SOURCE_PROPERTY_ID>` as its globally unique key while preserving the original property ID.

## Evidence partitions

Historical provenance, standards or guidance, formal results, empirical comparisons, incident evidence, field practice, criticism and open questions are distinct evidence types. A strong result in one partition does not automatically satisfy another. The source ledgers retain their heterogeneous evidence schemas rather than being rewritten into a false universal scale.

## Evolution under criticism

Research synthesis asks which mechanism survives criticism, where it applies, what failure it controls, what assumptions it requires, and when its cost exceeds its payoff. Ceremonies and branded practices survive only when their underlying engineering property remains decision-relevant.

## Freeze and replication

Each lineage freezes its selected sources, complete property population, report, ledgers, intake material and packet identity. Later repository-specific adoption work must cite these frozen identities, preserve non-adopted rows, and create its own target-specific crosswalk outside this neutral corpus.
