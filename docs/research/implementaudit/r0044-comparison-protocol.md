override. Participants may not spawn others. Parent integrates outputs; the
ordinary service alternative is used for deterministic source reconstruction
and validation in both arms. No source-only computation is labelled an

and authority/evidence limits to be preserved. Missing facts or an incorrect
stronger claim make the affected complete alternative incomplete. A direct win,
tie, partial result, failure or inconclusive outcome is retained.

Root records its real documentary receiving decision and integrates usable
findings. Existing `check-handoff-packet.sh --receiver-requires-reproduction`
checks complete actual result packets and labelled incomplete negative controls.
