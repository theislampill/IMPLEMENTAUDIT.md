## Executor reconstructibility

The packet states the central question, start gate, immutable denominator,
per-row historical/current comparison, destination taxonomy, owner and
reachability matrix, RXX genealogy burden, child-skill burden, ordered work,
parallel writer rules, held-outs, STOP conditions, rollback and terminal
receipt. A cold executor can begin at A00 after public readback and compute the
closed READY frontier without relying on this conversation.

The provisional state-derived work-orchestration candidate is intentionally
more detailed than a title but less authoritative than an admitted work order.
Its eight mechanism cells, genealogy, root problem, consumer, authority,
controls, package/reachability, no-regression, no-bloat and terminal acceptance
remain evidence slots. This preserves a serious candidate without converting
it into an outcome.

## Acceptance inheritance and anti-gaming

The RXX schema requires each additional architectural claim to record:

```text
NEW_REQUIREMENT
→ EXISTING_OWNER(S)
→ EXISTING_ACCEPTANCE_INHERITED
→ ADDITIONAL_FAILURE_MODE
→ ADDITIONAL_DISCRIMINATOR
→ ADDITIONAL_ACCEPTANCE_BURDEN
```

The held-out plan includes denominator loss, duplicate keys, historical-basis
erasure, copied dispositions, source/package/behaviour overclaims,
discriminator fiction, RXX quotas, taxonomy-derived children, authority
leakage, title-only DAG matching, artificial split/merge pressure, corpus
leakage, cheap-path regression, stale evidence and omitted JOIN. These controls
are decision-discriminating rather than ceremonial categories.

## Findings and repairs during this review

Two test-harness portability defects were found before this receipt: the new
preparation Bash test and the retained-corpus Bash test invoked `python`
directly even though the repository supports `python`, `python3`, or Windows
`py -3`. Both tests now use the established launcher-selection pattern. The
preparation test's exact-658 negative also now mutates the schema's actual
`ROWS` member, and the hypothesis negative mutates the actual token. These
repairs change no work-order conclusion or retained research content.

No preparation artifact allocates an RXX, predicts the next number, files an
issue, assigns a current property disposition, admits a new child, or declares
the state-derived orchestration hypothesis accepted.
