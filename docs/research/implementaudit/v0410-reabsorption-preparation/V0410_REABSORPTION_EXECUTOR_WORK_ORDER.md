## 1. Central question

For every frozen property, answer:

> What changed in transferability, native ownership, reachability,
> deterministic enforceability, cognitive composability, cost and proof status
> because exact released v0.4.0.0 exists?

The answer may be “nothing”. A more capable architecture changes feasibility;
it does not automatically strengthen research evidence, erase assumptions,
establish activation, or justify another runtime mechanism.

### Prohibited shortcuts

- No disposition by property name, research lineage, RXX title or keyword
  similarity.
- No copied historical result presented as current.
- No source presence presented as package, installation, reachability or
  behavioural activation.
- No feature, RXX, amendment or child-skill quota.
- No new RXX merely because an architecture composition sounds important.
- No forced reuse of an existing RXX when doing so would distort its semantic
  centre.
- No child skill named after a methodology or control loop without an
  independently meaningful cognitive contract.
- No weakening assumptions, domain limits, failed witnesses or anti-ceremony
  boundaries to increase the newly-transferable count.
- No full research corpus in the runtime package.
- No manufactured RED/source delta where released implementation already
  satisfies the admitted contract.

## 17. Rollback, compensation and retirement

- Reconciliation ledgers are append-only evidence; supersede incorrect rows
  with exact reason and identity rather than rewriting historical source.
- Before tracker publication, a rejected candidate is removed from the admitted
  set without consuming an RXX number.
- After tracker publication, correction is additive and independently read back;
  never delete or silently rewrite issue history.
- Source/package changes roll back through their native revert owner while
  preserving failed witnesses and RXX history.
- A new reference/child/checker/state field is retired when repeated evidence
  shows no decision/reasoning payoff, unacceptable false activation, cheaper
  existing ownership, or maintenance cost exceeding consequence.
- Retirement must restore a coherent lower-complexity owner and retain negative
  cases that motivated the original mechanism.

## 18. STOP/Andon conditions

Stop affected mutation/admission work and preserve evidence on:

- public v0.4 identity/readback incomplete;
- corpus/index/baseline hash or denominator mismatch;
- current ledger not exactly 658 unique rows;
- historical non-adoption basis lost;
- unresolved property/RXX references;
- RXX namespace or body/comment drift during allocation preparation;
- source/package/install/public identities conflated;
- candidate disposition decided before evidence;
- duplicate semantic owner or incoherent catch-all amendment;
- child candidate acquiring state, currentness, mutation, PASS or closure
  authority;
- package leakage of genealogy corpus;
- held-out failure or reviewer finding not reconciled;
- material exact baseline change without affected evidence invalidation.

Continue unaffected read-only lanes when dependencies permit.
