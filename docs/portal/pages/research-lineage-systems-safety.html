Systems Safety

Use this lineage when an action can enter a hazardous state or produce an unacceptable loss even without a component failure.

What this lineage contributes

Systems Safety contributes explicit unacceptable loss, hazardous state, enforceable safety constraints, control and feedback integrity, containment and recovery. It challenges unsafe interactions, timing and transition conditions rather than treating a risk label as the control.

Consequence drives depth: high-consequence continuation requires a current target state, observable feedback, a capable intervention chain and a bounded path to containment or recovery.

What changed

The landed v0.4.0 composition adds deterministic consequence-control selection. It rejects nominal override and method-only risk matrices, chooses bounded degraded operation only when stopping is itself hazardous, and preserves STOP when the control envelope cannot be shown.

Decision-state acceptance also keeps required function, current authority, evidence limits and recovery visible instead of promoting component health to system safety.

What was already native

IMPLEMENTAUDIT already used consequence-aware STOP, rollback, negative evidence, bounded scope and post-effect verification. Independent review and claim ceilings already prevented a local green result from proving broader safety.

What was rejected

No universal risk matrix, mandatory hazard worksheet, safety score, fixed review count or automatic “human override” claim was adopted. Low-consequence reversible action with direct authoritative readback retains the cheap path.

What remains unverified

This route does not claim a complete hazard analysis, a certified safety case, universal safety, loss probability or safe operation in an external environment. Hazard completeness and live intervention capability require domain-specific evidence.

Provenance

The frozen Systems Safety population is reconciled through the R002C composition. Current claims resolve to landed consequence-control, planning and operating-discipline owners. See the Evolved-CSS synthesis for family placement and the one-substrate boundary.
