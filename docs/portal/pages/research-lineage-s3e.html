Research & Engineering Lineage

S³E

State Synthesis Substrate Engineering: Evolved-SSDDRFCSS names one native state-synthesis substrate: a way to assemble only the current facts, authority, evidence, consequence and recovery conditions needed for the next governed decision.

One substrate: Evolved-LAW, Evolved-CSS, Evolved-SSD, and Evolved-DRF provide property genealogy and evidence ceilings. They do not create nine runtimes, selectable methodology modes, or a fixed ceremony.

Why four families converge

The four families did not share one historical origin. They converge because consequential implementation repeatedly fails at the joins between process, understanding, authority, evidence and recovery. Evolved-LAW asks which feedback, option, buffer or control is worth its cost. Evolved-CSS asks whether joint workers can understand the state, whether the evaluator can discriminate it, and what consequence a mistake creates. Evolved-SSD binds the decision to current intent, system identity, authority, alternatives and constraints. Evolved-DRF keeps partial failure, required function, proof assumptions and recovery postconditions explicit.

That convergence produces a substrate rather than a method. Each family supplies questions that may alter the same native decision state. When several traditions protect the same invariant, one native owner records and enforces it. When they disagree, the tension and domain assumption remain visible instead of being averaged into a slogan.

What becomes decision-usable

S³E combines the Evolved-LAW, Evolved-SSD, Evolved-DRF and Evolved-CSS property families through existing native owners. It keeps decision-relevant state, current authority, evaluator fitness, effect evidence, consequence and recovery coherent without asking a user to select a lineage.

For first readers, the substrate can be understood as a compact answer to nine questions. Which outcome or required function matters? Which candidate and environment are current? Who can decide, act, verify and stop? What is known, inferred, stale, missing or disputed? Which check can actually distinguish the protected property? What action or external effect is pending? What loss or failure direction constrains the choice? Which alternatives and dependencies remain live? What would establish recovery, closure, reopening or retirement?

Not every task needs every answer. The direct path remains authoritative when an empowered owner and one deterministic discriminator settle a local, reversible action. More state is synthesised only when consequence, coupling, delayed observation, external authority, recovery difficulty, uncertainty or hard-to-reverse commitment can change the decision.

Where the families constrain one another

  • Speed versus evidence. LAW favours early actionable feedback; statistical and formal properties reject weak discriminators; safety and reliability may demand containment before further measurement. The answer depends on whether another observation can still improve action before consequence grows.
  • Parallel flow versus single-writer truth. Decision and flow properties permit ready independent work; security, distributed and verification properties require one current authority and lawful joins for shared semantic state.
  • Adaptation versus stable constraints. Cognitive and agile adaptation helps under surprise, while safety, security and formal obligations protect invariants. Adaptation is allowed only inside a current, observable and recoverable envelope.
  • Transparency versus attention, privacy and attack surface. State must be legible enough for action, but more detail can overload the consumer, expose sensitive information or make an unfaithful explanation persuasive.
  • Efficiency versus resilience. Lean flow and operational optimisation remove waste; reliability and distributed evidence show that finite reserve, independent recovery and bounded duplication may be valuable. Slack survives only while a named consumer and recalibration rule justify it.
  • Proof strength versus world validity. Deterministic and formal evidence can settle a narrow property decisively; systems, cognitive and safety evidence still ask whether the property, model, actor and environment match the real consequential use.

What v0.4 translated into the native substrate

The landed runtime 0.4.0 composition sharpens conditional state synthesis, custody by the currently authorised governor, action selection, evaluator discipline, identity currentness, consequence control, recovery and closure. It does so through the existing governor and native state/evidence owners, not by invoking a research family at runtime. The published public identity is v0.4.0.0; research genealogy explains the bounded properties behind that implementation but is not implementation proof.

At the architecture level, bounded model-facing cognition can be delegated while consequential authority stays with the governor; independent ready work can proceed while shared semantic writers serialise; state and evidence return to one current decision point before transition. Exact package, source, configuration and evaluator identity keep evidence attached to the candidate it actually checked. A request, local success, package build, installation, hosted run and public readback remain separate evidence layers.

What was already native

Most mapped properties resolved to existing, stronger or composed native controls. The retained research composition records 658/658 property disposition accounting across twelve frozen lineages: 106 LAW properties plus 552 CSS, SSD and DRF properties. That is not 658 source changes. A row can resolve to a pre-existing owner, a sharpened or composed owner, a rejected ceremony, a bounded tension, an out-of-scope domain obligation, an unresolved evidence state or an owner decision.

Durable audit state, explicit ownership, exact identity, bounded phases, independent review, negative evidence, STOP, rollback, receiver readback and proof-specific claim ceilings pre-date the family labels. The synthesis reconciles why those mechanisms belong together and where their limits differ.

What the substrate deliberately did not import

No twelve-lineage runtime, four-family mode, methodology questionnaire, maturity ladder, fixed documentation ceremony, universal score, risk matrix, optimisation oracle, consensus service, reliability programme or formalisation mandate was added. The source traditions do not become lifecycle stages, and their branded artefacts do not become conformance requirements.

Specialised mechanisms remain conditional tools: a designed experiment, hazard analysis, threat model, queue model, fault-injection exercise or formal proof is admitted only when it can discriminate a live consequential decision and its assumptions can be carried. The no-trigger path adds no research record merely because a family has vocabulary for the problem.

Evidence and generalisation limits

The frozen corpus supports genealogy, criticism, mechanisms, domain boundaries and a complete property-disposition census. The accepted crosswalk supports preservation of admitted architecture relations on its exact checked candidate. Neither establishes universal domain activation, causal effectiveness, optimal thresholds, reliability, security, safety, formal correctness or better outcomes across other repositories, agents, hosts or organisations.

Source, generated, built, packaged, installed, deployed and public states remain distinct. The recorded v0.4.0.0 publication gates are complete, but a lineage page does not substitute for those checks or promote untested running-host or marketplace behaviour, external-effect proof or broader provenance. The retained research corpus is source evidence, is excluded from both runtime payloads, and does not activate the separately deferred v0.4.1 work.

Package architecture and version boundary

There is one public/default governor, /implementaudit. Runtime 0.4.0 includes four internal model-facing capabilities for state interpretation, assessment, implementation support and abnormality handling—audit-state, audit-assess, maintainer-only audit-implement, and dual-entry audit-andon. They are bounded capabilities, not public lifecycle stages: none owns authority, currentness, mutation, transition, release or closure, and none routes the user into a methodology family.

The canonical plugin includes two archive-local .claude-plugin manifests and the governor plus its four internal child skills under skills/. The separately generated standalone compatibility projection omits plugin manifests, flattens the governor to archive root, and carries deterministic internal-procedure projections for the children. The canonical plugin and standalone compatibility projections are mechanically checked independently; they are not literal member-for-member copies.

Across both projections, the four child procedures under the governor remain bounded parts of one architecture rather than independent authorities.
