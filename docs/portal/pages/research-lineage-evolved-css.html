Research & Engineering Lineage

Evolved-CSS

Evolved-CSS joins Cognitive Systems Engineering, Statistical Engineering, and Systems Safety so that shared state is understandable to the people and agents using it, discriminating enough to support a decision, and bounded by the consequence of being wrong.

Boundary: Evolved-CSS is property-level genealogy, not a runtime mode, conformance scheme, user selector, or claim of universal effectiveness.

Three traditions, one bounded convergence

The family is a convergence, not a claim that one tradition descended from either of the others. Cognitive Systems Engineering grew through classical human factors, supervisory control, joint cognitive systems, work analysis, distributed cognition and human–automation research. Statistical Engineering brings several separate lines of control, sampling, experimental design, metrology, reliability and robustness. Systems Safety grew through accident prevention, reliability and hazard analysis, lifecycle system safety, functional safety, assurance and control-oriented reformulations. Their histories overlap and borrow from shared systems practice, but similarity of vocabulary is not proof of common ancestry.

They meet on a practical question: what must a decision-maker know before a consequential action is safe to take? Cognitive work supplies the human and coordination conditions; statistical reasoning asks whether the observation or evaluator can distinguish the relevant states; safety reasoning asks which hazardous state or unacceptable loss the decision must prevent. None can answer the whole question alone.

What survives in combination

The combined property is decision-usable, consequence-aware state. State is not useful merely because it is displayed or recorded. It must reach a capable consumer in time, distinguish fact from inference, expose freshness and uncertainty where those can alter action, and connect a command to evidence about the resulting world state. If the observation cannot discriminate the protected condition, or the intervention chain cannot control the consequence, the correct state is unresolved rather than optimistically complete.

Complementary mechanisms and real tensions

  • More visibility can reduce understanding. Extra alerts, explanations and measurements may overload attention, create false authority or obscure the one condition that can still change action.
  • Shared state can improve coordination and create correlated error. A common representation reduces handoff loss, but a stale or captured source can manufacture false consensus. Provenance, update authority and independent challenge therefore matter.
  • Adaptation can preserve work and violate a safety constraint. Flexible allocation is useful only within a visible envelope of current authority, capability, consequence and recovery.
  • Measurement discipline can conflict with urgent containment. A better estimate has value only while waiting for it does not increase loss. Severe, hard-to-reverse consequences can justify conservative control before statistical precision is available.
  • Informational independence can slow common action. Withholding a favoured hypothesis may improve exploration, but shared facts, hazards, scope and authority cannot be withheld from people who must coordinate or recover.

What IMPLEMENTAUDIT translated

IMPLEMENTAUDIT did not install the three traditions as methods. It translated their stable discriminators into existing native controls for durable state, issue and phase reconstruction, receiver-current handoff, evaluator integrity, action selection, consequence control, STOP, recovery and closure. Material work can now require a compact decision-state account: the live consumer, required condition, current authority, evidence and uncertainty limits, pending effect, credible consequence, and recovery or reopen condition.

The translation also keeps measurement and mutation separate. When a candidate can alter the evaluator that judges it, the earlier witness and independently discriminating positive, negative, boundary and adjacent cases remain visible. When an action has a material consequence, acceptance follows the observed effect or restored state, not merely the request, acknowledgement or local command result.

What was already native

Durable state, explicit ownership, independent review, negative controls, STOP markers, rollback, bounded recovery and evidence-specific claim ceilings existed before this family synthesis. Many of the 195 frozen property rows therefore resolve to an existing or already stronger owner, a composition of owners, a bounded tension, a rejected ceremony or a domain-specific residual. The row census is not a count of new features.

When one authoritative deterministic discriminator settles ordinary bounded work, use it and stop. No trigger means no added ceremony, record, or family machinery.

Deliberate non-imports

No cognitive-work worksheet, situation-awareness or workload score, metric dashboard, universal risk matrix, fixed review quota, mandatory experiment, safety-case ceremony or selectable CSS methodology was added. The synthesis also rejects the ideas that a nominal human approval is an effective control, repeated green tests create independent evidence, a risk-matrix category is the risk, or more information is monotonically better.

Domain techniques remain available when their assumptions hold—for example a focused work analysis, designed experiment or hazard method—but their named forms, thresholds and documentation bundles are not general runtime requirements. Components, measurements and safety language cannot promote one another’s evidence layers.

Evidence and generalisation limits

The three frozen reports mix historical sources, formal mechanisms, standards and guidance, empirical studies, reviews and incident evidence. Those evidence types answer different questions. A standard can establish recognised practice; an incident can expose a failure mechanism; a controlled study can support a bounded comparison. None alone proves universal effect size or transfer to another repository, agent, host or operational domain.

This family does not prove better human–agent performance, calibrated evaluator validity, complete hazard discovery, loss probability, package reachability, host behaviour, deployment, or universal safety. Aviation, process, manufacturing, metrology and other domain results transfer only at the supported mechanism and assumption boundary. Each child page states the narrower limits that survive its own frozen source table and property ledger.
