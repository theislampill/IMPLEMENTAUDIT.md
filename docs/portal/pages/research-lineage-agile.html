Agile

Historical and current sources: the original Page narrative and complete property reader below retain the historical public Agile account. Its 28 original rows are not the corrected accepted study merely because current identities have the same count. LAW-L2 source genealogy and qualification records the exact later subject. Detailed current scientific interpretation remains withheld pending source-specific disclosure; family completeness keeps that limit and the unfinished LAW-S separate.

The surviving contribution is a set of adaptive engineering mechanisms, not iteration alone and not mandatory Scrum ceremony.

Genealogy

  1. Iterative/incremental development, test-first work, evolutionary delivery, prototyping, and risk-driven spiral predate the 2001 Manifesto.
  2. Scrum, XP, DSDM, Crystal, FDD, ASD, and neighbouring methods formed a plural field. Scrum supplied empirical management; XP supplied a more explicit technical changeability system.
  3. The Manifesto and principles formed a thin declaration rather than a complete lifecycle or causal study.
  4. Lean software, software Kanban, Continuous Delivery, and DevOps hybridised with or reacted to Agile practice.
  5. SRE, safety, security, product discovery, evolutionary architecture, platform engineering, and systems assurance also converged from partly independent traditions.

Beyond the word iteration

Agile's strongest surviving properties include integrated working evidence, early risk retirement, automated regression, frequent real integration, technical changeability, release-ready state with exposure decoupled, direct problem/environment feedback, authorised process adaptation, sustainable pace and sustainable recovery capacity, and operational feedback after delivery.

A calendar iteration without engineering evidence, adaptation authority, technical quality, or operational learning does not establish those properties.

Rejected caricatures and ceremonies

Agile is not Scrum, and Scrum is not a complete engineering system. Shorter feedback, a two-week sprint, fixed iteration, release trains, continuous deployment, daily status meetings, user-story syntax, story points, velocity, TDD sequence, pair programming, one Product Owner, or a scaling framework are not universal prescriptions.

The retained property must be tested directly: coordination rather than stand-up attendance; learning rather than retrospective occurrence; regression evidence rather than a TDD label; knowledge resilience rather than pair count; and outcome evidence rather than velocity.

Native route

IMPLEMENTAUDIT uses adaptive planning, integrated evidence, phase-by-phase execution, explicit stopping, and feedback loops as native controls. The Evolved-LAW synthesis explains which controls pre-existed and which proportionality distinctions were sharpened. It adds no sprint, backlog, Scrum, XP, SAFe, or Agile runtime mode.
