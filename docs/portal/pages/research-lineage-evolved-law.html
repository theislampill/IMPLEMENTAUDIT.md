Evolved-LAW

Lean × Agile × Waterfall: guarded contributions to a real decision

The accepted A008 synthesis asks how Lean, Agile and evolved staged engineering can contribute to the same actual work, decision or effect. It does not prescribe the union of three catalogues. Lean contributes outcomes, flow, resources, learning and participation; Agile contributes decision-relevant observation, integrated behavior and adaptation; staged engineering contributes consequential commitments, configuration correspondence, assurance and distinct rights. Each contribution must reach a real receiving premise under a stated guard.

Direction matters, including useful partial contributions

A source can supply one needed observation, criterion, capability or right while the receiver's complete claim remains false or unknown. That limited contribution can matter even when the final choice does not change. Its reverse needs a separate argument.

For example, an actual external evidence task assigned to the same workers and time horizon must be counted in a workload comparison. This establishes a completeness condition, not fairness, wellbeing or measured gain. Technical readiness can inform the response to a worker concern without creating the worker's right to be heard. An effective transfer of a technical role identifies the responsible occupant only when a real response duty already attaches to that role, item, issue, scope and time.

Seven configurations, not seven mandatory phases

  • Small reversible work: a competent direct check, real authority and feasible response may suffice; a short edit is not automatically low consequence.
  • Variable demand and constrained flow: count upstream waiting, actual setup, eligible capacity and evidence work; a free WIP slot is not permission for every step.
  • Uncertain product: technical, intended-use and delayed-outcome evidence run on different clocks; choose an informative observation within an actionable window.
  • Strong staged engineering: stable relevant requirements, physical coupling or scarce qualification can justify deliberate sequencing while preserving challenge and warranted revision.
  • Adaptive work with consequential assurance: technical readiness, recipient acceptance, external conditions and operating authority retain distinct meanings.
  • Persistent migration, hardware and long-lead effects: restoring code, state, service and harm are different claims; a nominal alternate may share the failed dependency.
  • Urgent response: delay can make an existing expedited route appropriate without creating permission, staffing, competence or safe recovery.

SORME: a compact public label, not a new mode

Second-Order Reflexive Meta-Engineering (SORME) describes the capability to improve the engineering process itself while preserving evidence about what changed and why. Evolved-LAW is the research lineage for that description. It is not a runtime mode, lifecycle, certification, or claim that three branded methodologies execute inside IMPLEMENTAUDIT.

Property-level synthesis

The useful unit is the protected engineering property. Where several traditions point to the same invariant, one native owner implements it. The result is property-level synthesis and no methodology-specific runtime. Similar vocabulary alone does not prove direct lineage.

  • Bounded buffer or protective slack is protective only while it serves a live engineering function and consumer; otherwise it is waste and should be removed or retired.
  • Faster feedback is useful only when its information is actionable before avoidable commitment.
  • Local utilisation does not outrank system capacity, queue health, or recovery.
  • Options remain open only while expected information value exceeds carrying cost and an exit condition remains live.
  • Control depth rises with consequence, coupling, delayed observability, external authority, recovery difficulty, and hard-to-reverse commitment—not with a methodology label.
