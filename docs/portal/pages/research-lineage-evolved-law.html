Evolved-LAW

Evolved-LAW remains the Lean, Agile, and Waterfall family within the integrated S³E substrate. Its historical title and classifications remain intact; integration does not turn the family into a selectable runtime.

A property-level Lean · Agile · Waterfall synthesis for second-order reflexive meta-engineering—not three methods bolted onto the runtime.

SORME: a compact public label, not a new mode

Second-Order Reflexive Meta-Engineering (SORME) describes the capability to improve the engineering process itself while preserving evidence about what changed and why. Evolved-LAW is the research lineage for that description. It is not a runtime mode, lifecycle, certification, or claim that three branded methodologies execute inside IMPLEMENTAUDIT.

Property-level synthesis

The useful unit is the protected engineering property. Where several traditions point to the same invariant, one native owner implements it. The result is property-level synthesis and no methodology-specific runtime. Similar vocabulary alone does not prove direct lineage.

  • Bounded buffer or protective slack is protective only while it serves a live engineering function and consumer; otherwise it is waste and should be removed or retired.
  • Faster feedback is useful only when its information is actionable before avoidable commitment.
  • Local utilisation does not outrank system capacity, queue health, or recovery.
  • Options remain open only while expected information value exceeds carrying cost and an exit condition remains live.
  • Control depth rises with consequence, coupling, delayed observability, external authority, recovery difficulty, and hard-to-reverse commitment—not with a methodology label.

Work-conserving and authoring truth

Work-conserving means ready independent cells may proceed when their expected information or decision value exceeds dispatch and reconciliation cost. A shared issue, milestone, branch train, eventual package, or public surface is not itself a dependency. Exact write/write, semantic-owner, authority, resource, acceptance, and composed-only dependencies still serialise the affected cells.

R0025 / #186, R0026 / #187, and R0027 / #188 own the frozen evidence populations and crosswalk. R001D, R0020, R0022, R0023, and the other RXX labels identify repository work-order and native-control ownership. They are not a hidden user workflow, a maturity ladder, or evidence that every property changed in this release.

Governed-writer boundary

R0024's destructive-mutation route binds a validated phase step, complete planned effects, transactional evidence, and a governed-writer window. That lock coordinates cooperating IMPLEMENTAUDIT helper writers. It cannot prevent an unrelated process with the same operating-system authority from writing directly, so pre-image, identity, post-state, and interference checks remain necessary. “Governed writer” must not be read as a universal filesystem transaction or host-wide exclusion guarantee.
