The composition claim and its limits

The synthesis asks when a result, constraint or observation produced under one lineage supplies a useful operand to another lineage's actual decision. Architecture can produce a typed dependency or a mapped conformance observation. Maintenance can use that fact to assess impact, action scope, preserved obligations or support. Product Line Engineering can supply a realised product/configuration mapping, a selected sample or a family boundary. The claim is a guarded partial contribution: the recipient still needs its independent purpose, criterion, authority, coverage and other premises. The source need not satisfy the recipient's whole predicate, and the recipient's success need not establish the source's whole predicate. Native production rule, current receiving account.

A directional relation therefore identifies the producer, produced operand, receiving operation, same-object mapping, actual consumption and inference limit. Merely reusing a file, sharing an upstream premise, performing two practices in the same lifecycle, or naming the same task does not establish the relation. The reverse direction requires its own produced decision or observation and a separately demonstrated receiving use. Two established arrows do not create an automatic inverse, an all-to-all clique, or a transitive conclusion. A direct sufficiently bound check can replace the named practice that originally supplied the input.
