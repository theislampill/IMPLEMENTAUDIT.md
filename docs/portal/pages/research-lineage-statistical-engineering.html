Statistical Engineering

Use this lineage to decide whether a measurement, evaluator or experiment can discriminate the claim well enough to guide action.

What this lineage contributes

Statistical Engineering starts with the decision-relevant measurand and measurement-system fitness. It distinguishes accuracy from precision, carries variation and uncertainty into decisions, uses representative populations and negative controls, and makes proxy resistance explicit.

Evaluator agreement, drift and recalibration matter only in relation to intended use. A score or repeated pass is not validity evidence when the instrument, population or decision boundary is wrong.

What changed

The landed v0.4.0 composition strengthens evaluator-mutation discipline. Candidate-controlled policy changes retain the original witness and require independent property evidence plus held-out positive, negative, boundary and adjacent discrimination.

Planning and acceptance now separate consumer value, actionable information, cost and stopping conditions. Experiments stop when the claim is settled or remaining information cannot change a capable consumer’s action.

What was already native

IMPLEMENTAUDIT already required evidence populations, examined counts, deterministic fixtures, negative controls, independent review and claim-specific acceptance. Those owners supplied substantial statistical discipline before this family label was added.

What was rejected

No universal score, confidence threshold, sample-size ceremony, test-count target or metric dashboard was adopted. More runs, reviewers or replicas do not by themselves create independence. Optimising a proxy cannot replace the protected consequence.

What remains unverified

This route does not claim calibrated probabilities, population representativeness, evaluator validity across models and hosts, causal effects or universal statistical power. Repository fixtures prove only their encoded classifications and held-out mutations.

Provenance
