From Toyota-specific production history to portable engineering properties—without turning IMPLEMENTAUDIT into a Lean programme or toolkit.

Genealogy

  1. Toyoda loom defect-stop and Kiichiro Toyoda's needed-quantity/time ideas preceded the later jidoka and just-in-time system.
  2. Toyota combined flow with kanban, levelling, quality, maintenance, training, labour institutions, supplier relations, and bounded response to abnormality.
  3. Statistical quality and Training Within Industry are shared ancestries, not proof that either generated the complete Toyota Production System.
  4. Lean production became an external comparative category; Lean Thinking abstracted value, value stream, flow, pull, and perfection.
  5. Product-development, learning-system, and socio-technical interpretations exposed option, sustainment, worker, and power boundaries hidden by toolkit accounts.
  6. Lean software and software Kanban translated selected properties. Continuous Delivery and DevOps are hybrids and convergences, not simply direct Toyota descendants.

Surviving properties

  • Inspect actual work and triangulate it with traceable system evidence.
  • Expose abnormality, then supply bounded stop, containment, escalation, recovery, and recurrence learning.
  • Prefer system flow and queue-aware capacity to maximum local utilisation.
  • Use WIP, batch, cadence, and buffers only when their live economics justify them.
  • Maintain a revisable baseline with challenge, qualification, expiry, and retirement.
  • Retain options only while uncertainty and irreversible commitment justify their carrying cost.

Rejected caricatures and ceremonies

Historical TPS, Lean production, Lean Thinking, learning-system Lean, and toolkit Lean are not interchangeable. Waste elimination is not the master objective. Zero inventory, maximum utilisation, zero slack, the smallest possible batch everywhere, and exact Toyota-tool conformance are rejected universals.

5S, value-stream maps, kanban boards, A3 sheets, exactly five whys, kaizen events, and standard-work forms may instantiate a property in context. Their presence is not evidence that the property works. Labour and trust language also cannot be converted into unsupported claims of autonomy, humane workload, or fair distribution.

EL-001 — Outcome and value definition with named beneficiary, constraints and time horizon
PROPERTY ID
EL-001
PROPERTY NAME
Outcome and value definition with named beneficiary, constraints and time horizon
HISTORICAL ORIGIN
Toyota's customer-first quality doctrine; Lean Thinking's value principle; later service/product critiques.
LINEAGE CLASSIFICATION
LEAN_PRODUCTION_GENERALISATION
ORIGINAL FORM
Define value from the customer and orient production toward it.
PROBLEM IT ADDRESSED
Organisations can efficiently produce outputs that do not improve the beneficiary's outcome or that externalise cost.
FAILURE MODE IT TRIES TO PREVENT
Efficient delivery of low-value, harmful or locally beneficial work.
MECHANISM
Name the beneficiary and desired outcome; state safety, labour, resilience and externality constraints; choose a time horizon; test whether work changes the outcome.
TRIGGER OR CONTEXT
Multiple plausible outputs compete, resources are material, or local efficiency could displace customer/system value.
NON TRIGGER OR CHEAP PATH
For trivial, reversible work, record a one-sentence purpose and acceptance condition rather than create a value-stream artefact.
DEPENDENCIES OR PRECONDITIONS
Legitimate beneficiary representation; outcome observability; explicit non-negotiable constraints; authority to stop low-value work.
EXPECTED ENGINEERING PAYOFF
Better selection, fewer abandoned outputs, reduced proxy optimisation and clearer trade-offs.
KNOWN FAILURE MODES
Customer rhetoric masks managerial cost goals; one stakeholder's value harms another; short-horizon metrics; unmeasurable outcomes; retrospective rationalisation.
IMPORTANT CRITICISMS
Lean literature specifies waste more richly than value; customer language can ignore workers, suppliers, public risk and long-term resilience.
HOW THE PROPERTY EVOLVED
Moved from production value and waste categories toward multi-stakeholder outcome framing, product discovery and system constraints.
MATURE OR EVOLVED FORM
Use an explicit value hypothesis with beneficiary, outcome, constraints, evidence threshold, review date and abandonment rule.
CEREMONY VS PROPERTY
No Lean vocabulary, value workshop or value-stream map is required; the property is a decision-relevant outcome model.

PROPERTY ID
EL-002
PROPERTY NAME
End-to-end system boundary and flow/queue/handoff visibility
HISTORICAL ORIGIN
TPS material/information flow; Lean value-stream generalisation; later service/software translations.
LINEAGE CLASSIFICATION
LEAN_PRODUCTION_GENERALISATION
ORIGINAL FORM
See the whole path from demand through production and delivery rather than optimise isolated machines or departments.
PROBLEM IT ADDRESSED
Local gains create downstream queues, rework, handoff loss or delayed customer outcomes.
FAILURE MODE IT TRIES TO PREVENT
Local optimisation and invisible waiting across organisational boundaries.
MECHANISM
Define the relevant path and work classes; observe arrivals, queues, processing, rework, handoffs, dependencies and outcome latency; assign end-to-end ownership.
TRIGGER OR CONTEXT
Repeated handoffs, unexplained elapsed time, departmental optimisation or customer delay dominate processing time.
NON TRIGGER OR CHEAP PATH
For a short single-owner path, a simple trace or sampled timeline is enough; no wall-sized map.
DEPENDENCIES OR PRECONDITIONS
Stable item identity; traceability across boundaries; truthful timestamps/states; an owner able to resolve cross-boundary causes.
EXPECTED ENGINEERING PAYOFF
Reduced total lead time, fewer handoff failures, better bottleneck and queue decisions.
KNOWN FAILURE MODES
Map theatre; stale diagrams; excessive instrumentation; forced linear path for exploratory work; omission of externalities; local teams unable to change system constraints.
IMPORTANT CRITICISMS
Value-stream maps are frequently local snapshots and can mistake visible factory-like sequence for the real distributed causal system.
HOW THE PROPERTY EVOLVED
Value-stream mapping was generalised beyond factories; modern software combines traces and delivery telemetry, while discovery work may require networks rather than linear streams.
MATURE OR EVOLVED FORM
Maintain the cheapest decision-useful end-to-end model, including feedback/rework loops, queues, dependencies and outcome—not just touch time.
CEREMONY VS PROPERTY
A value-stream map is one implementation. Event logs, service blueprints, dependency graphs or sampled traces may be stronger.

PROPERTY ID

PROPERTY NAME
Conditional pull/release control and explicit WIP limits
HISTORICAL ORIGIN
Toyota supermarket/kanban; operations-science formalisation; software Kanban translation.
LINEAGE CLASSIFICATION
DIRECT_TPS_LINEAGE
ORIGINAL FORM
Downstream withdrawal and explicit limits prevent premature production and excess work in process.
PROBLEM IT ADDRESSED
Unbounded release creates congestion, long feedback, priority churn and hidden defects.
FAILURE MODE IT TRIES TO PREVENT
Queue growth, overproduction, premature commitment and excessive context switching.
MECHANISM

EXPECTED ENGINEERING PAYOFF

EL-005 — Batch-size economics and small reversible increments
PROPERTY ID
EL-005
PROPERTY NAME
Batch-size economics and small reversible increments
HISTORICAL ORIGIN
TPS setup reduction and small lots; Lean product development and Continuous Delivery translation.
LINEAGE CLASSIFICATION
HYBRID
ORIGINAL FORM
Reduce lot size to shorten feedback and lower inventory once setup costs permit.
PROBLEM IT ADDRESSED
Large batches delay information, amplify rework and make failures expensive.
FAILURE MODE IT TRIES TO PREVENT
Late defect discovery, large release risk, long queue time and irreversible commitment.
MECHANISM
Model batch benefits against setup, validation, coordination and transport costs; reduce setup first; choose a reversible economic batch.
TRIGGER OR CONTEXT
Feedback delay or failure blast radius is material and switching/release cost can be reduced.
NON TRIGGER OR CHEAP PATH
If changes are already tiny or validation has a fixed high cost, keep the economic batch and improve feedback within it.
DEPENDENCIES OR PRECONDITIONS
Measurable setup/release costs; modular work; validation capability; rollback/recovery; ability to integrate increments.
EXPECTED ENGINEERING PAYOFF
Faster learning, smaller failures, lower inventory and release risk.
KNOWN FAILURE MODES
One-piece-flow dogma; fragmentation overhead; excessive coordination; local micro-batches accumulating into system batches; unsafe partial delivery.
IMPORTANT CRITICISMS
Smallness is not value; a minimum batch can be uneconomic, and knowledge work may require coherent experimental units.
HOW THE PROPERTY EVOLVED
Generalised from physical lots to information, decisions and software changes; product development added option preservation rather than indiscriminate decomposition.
MATURE OR EVOLVED FORM
Select batch size from total economics and reversibility, not a universal minimum; make delayed feedback and blast radius explicit.
CEREMONY VS PROPERTY
One-piece flow, daily releases or story slicing are implementations, not the property.

EL-007 — Demand-capacity pacing and production levelling
PROPERTY ID
EL-007
PROPERTY NAME
Demand-capacity pacing and production levelling
HISTORICAL ORIGIN
Kiichiro/Ohno JIT, takt and heijunka in repetitive automotive production.
LINEAGE CLASSIFICATION
DIRECT_TPS_LINEAGE
ORIGINAL FORM
Use demand pace and mix levelling to coordinate repetitive production and reduce unevenness.
PROBLEM IT ADDRESSED
Uncoordinated release creates surges, starvation, overtime and supplier amplification.
FAILURE MODE IT TRIES TO PREVENT
Demand/capacity mismatch, violent mix changes, uneven workloads and hidden overburden.
MECHANISM
Estimate sustainable pace by work class; level the predictable component; explicitly buffer residual variation; replan when assumptions break.
TRIGGER OR CONTEXT
Repeatable work, measurable aggregate demand, flexible capacity and feasible mix sequencing.
NON TRIGGER OR CHEAP PATH
For unique, exploratory or very low-volume work, use finite-capacity planning, rolling forecasts and explicit priority rather than takt ceremony.
DEPENDENCIES OR PRECONDITIONS
Demand information; cross-trained/flexible resources; setup capability; exception policy; workload and ergonomic safeguards; buffers.
EXPECTED ENGINEERING PAYOFF
Smoother flow, less oscillation, clearer overload and more stable supplier/capacity planning.
KNOWN FAILURE MODES
False precision; backlog or overtime used to hide variation; forced pace and work intensification; brittle high-mix schedules; supplier burden transfer.
IMPORTANT CRITICISMS
Takt can become a coercive pace standard; conventional levelling has weak applicability in high-mix/low-volume or discovery work.
HOW THE PROPERTY EVOLVED
Later research narrows conventional levelling to stable/repetitive contexts and develops HMLV adaptations; resilience hybrids preserve reserves.
MATURE OR EVOLVED FORM
Level only forecastable aggregate demand, maintain capacity/time buffers and protect workload limits; treat takt as diagnostic, not a metronome.
CEREMONY VS PROPERTY
Takt boards, heijunka boxes and fixed pitch are manufacturing implementations, not general requirements.
