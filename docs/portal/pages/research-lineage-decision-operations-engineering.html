Genealogy

  1. Early teletraffic theory treated calls, capacity, loss and waiting as stochastic operating phenomena. Inventory theory formalised the cost of replenishment, shortage and holding. These were distinct operational lineages, not one optimisation doctrine.
  2. Wartime operations research joined multidisciplinary analysis to mission-facing decisions; post-war operations research and management science broadened into a plural field concerned with formulation, evidence, implementation and formal methods.
  3. Linear, integer and network programming made objectives, alternatives, scarce resources and constraints explicit. Dynamic programming and Markov decision models made the distinction between a fixed plan and a state-contingent policy explicit.
  4. PERT, CPM and machine-scheduling traditions represented precedence, resources and timing. Later resource-constrained, stochastic and rolling methods exposed why a published critical path is conditional and can become stale.
  5. Monte Carlo and discrete-event simulation enabled controlled counterfactual experiments when analytical solutions were unavailable. Decision analysis formalised alternatives, consequences, preferences and information value; multi-criteria schools made value conflict visible without making it objective.
  6. Stochastic, robust, distributionally robust, online and data-driven optimisation added different treatments of uncertainty and feedback. Reinforcement learning and decision-focused learning are modern translations, not replacements for problem formulation, authority, validation or implementation.

What survives after criticism

The mature residue begins with a named decision owner, consumer, deadline, consequence and authority to act. It keeps meaningful alternatives open long enough to compare them, separates enforceable hard constraints from preferences, resists proxy substitution, and treats objectives and metrics as challengeable representations rather than neutral facts. A model is selected only after that frame exists.

  • Prefer a cheap authoritative discriminator or validated simple rule when it settles the decision.
  • State assumptions and validity domain as part of every analytical claim; separate solver optimality from real-decision validity.
  • Represent arrivals, service, capacity and variability before claiming that utilisation or staffing is efficient.
  • Optimise end-to-end consequence rather than local busyness; price slack and reserve as both cost and recovery option.
  • Schedule parallel work from real precedence, resource and acceptance conflicts, then recompute after material state change.
  • Distinguish variability, parameter uncertainty, ambiguity, model-form error and non-stationarity; preserve recourse and reopening where credible.
  • Validate models in the deployed decision loop, monitor realised consequences, and retire a forecast, schedule or metric that no longer has a live consumer.

Criticism changed the field from deterministic one-shot optimisation towards uncertainty-aware, adaptive decision engineering. Stochastic methods model probability and recourse; robust methods protect against bounded ambiguity; distributionally robust methods add uncertainty over probability laws; rolling and online methods re-decide as state changes. Each adds a different assumption and evidence burden. Formal guarantees under a representation do not prove that its objective, probabilities, ambiguity set or deployment feedback fits reality.

The hard tensions cannot be solved by ceremony. Near-full utilisation may reduce unit cost while exploding waiting time. Stability protects coordination while replanning responds to changed state. Robustness limits downside while over-conservatism destroys opportunity. Expected value permits trade-offs while irreversible or tail consequences may be non-compensable. A simple rule is legible and cheap but can drift; a complex model can discriminate close alternatives but amplifies data and governance errors.

How IMPLEMENTAUDIT translates it

IMPLEMENTAUDIT translates this lineage into factor-derived action depth, a live consequence-bearing owner, explicit alternatives and STOP conditions, the cheapest discriminating evidence, and schedules derived from current dependencies, shared writers, capacity and acceptance conflicts. Independent work may proceed in parallel; shared writes and dependent conclusions wait for current inputs. Failed hypotheses reopen alternatives, while additional analysis stops when it cannot change action enough to justify cost or delay.

These are bounded convergences with the lineage, not proof that IMPLEMENTAUDIT contains an optimiser or that its scheduling rules are globally optimal.

What was already native

IMPLEMENTAUDIT already required a live consumer, a bounded objective, explicit acceptance, evidence before transition, alternatives after a failed hypothesis, and refusal when evidence cannot support the next action. Its ready-work logic already distinguished real dependency or write conflict from mere membership in the same issue, and its proportionality rules already kept low-consequence reversible work on a short path.

The crosswalk therefore composes decision properties with existing planning, phase, capacity, evidence and closure owners rather than duplicating them. A solver, model or agent may establish a bounded fact or recommendation; the authorised owner still decides whether the consequence warrants action.

What was deliberately not imported

No optimisation oracle, mandatory scorecard, fixed utility function, universal reserve percentage, one-hundred-percent utilisation target, deterministic Gantt authority, fixed fan-out target, simulation-run quota, numerical value threshold or planning worksheet was added. Process volume, worker count and utilisation are not value evidence.

A named solver is a technique, not a general engineering property. Reinforcement learning, predictive maintenance, giant integrated models and parametric queue formulae remain domain translations that require credible state, failure, workload and feedback models. For knowledge or agent work, direct backlog, elapsed-time, bottleneck and conflict observations are safer than unvalidated distributional formulae.

Tensions, assumptions and evidence limits

Formal results are often strong inside their assumptions; external validity is the weaker bridge. Queueing formulae depend on arrival, service and regime assumptions. Forecast error can be amplified by the optimisation layer. PERT-style estimates understate correlation and resource coupling. Multi-criteria rankings can reverse with weights, scales, normalisation or candidate set. Simulation replications reduce Monte Carlo error, not input or structural error. Implementation resistance may reveal missing constraints rather than irrational users.

This route does not prove globally optimal choices, calibrated utilities, complete stakeholder representation, stable deployed preferences, universal scheduling gains or safe transfer of OR formulae across domains. It also does not establish that distributionally robust optimisation, decision-focused learning, reinforcement learning, digital twins or AI–OR integration is a mature universal endpoint.
