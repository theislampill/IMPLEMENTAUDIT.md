Reliability & Maintainability Engineering

Use this lineage to keep required function, failure propagation, degraded operation, repair and restoration visible without turning a passing check into a reliability claim.

What this lineage contributes

Reliability & Maintainability Engineering starts with an explicit required function, operational context and failure boundary. It challenges single points, common-cause dependencies, diagnostic coverage, graceful degradation, maintainability, repair and restoration against the current configuration.

Redundancy counts only when independence, switchover and coverage are credible. A healthy component or repeated run is not evidence that the composed function survives the relevant failure.

What changed

The landed v0.4.0 composition sharpens consequence-aware degraded operation and recovery. High-consequence continuation requires a current target state, feedback, containment and recovery; degraded operation is bounded to cases where stopping is itself hazardous and the envelope remains observable.

Evidence currentness is bound to interface, schema, configuration, environment and authority identity. A material identity change invalidates only dependent evidence until requalified.

What was already native

IMPLEMENTAUDIT already retained failures, reopen conditions, rollback plans, STOP markers, exact package identity and post-action verification. Those controls provided substantial native failure and recovery coverage before this lineage was composed.

What was rejected

No reliability score, uptime target, mandatory FMEA, redundancy quota or permanent maintenance worksheet was adopted. Repetition, replica count and nominal success cannot substitute for failure-domain independence or required-function evidence.

What remains unverified

This route does not report measured reliability, availability, maintainability, diagnostic coverage, recovery time or service durability. The frozen population includes unresolved static, behavioural and fixture gaps; those remain bounded rather than being promoted by the family label.

Provenance

The frozen Reliability & Maintainability Engineering population and its unresolved dispositions are retained through the R49 composition. Current public claims resolve only to landed operating-discipline and action-selection controls. See the Evolved-DRF synthesis for family placement.
