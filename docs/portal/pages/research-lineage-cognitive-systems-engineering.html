Cognitive Systems Engineering

Use this lineage when consequential work depends on people, agents, tools and representations coordinating as one joint cognitive work system.

What this lineage contributes

Cognitive Systems Engineering contributes decision-relevant state, mode and transition legibility; authority and capability alignment; handoff and re-entry; attention routing; coordination; adaptation; and cognitive fit. It looks for interaction failures and hidden coordination work that component-level passes can miss.

The useful boundary is the smallest joint system that explains the consequential work. Authority remains assignable even when cognition or control is distributed.

What changed

The landed v0.4.0 composition adds conditional decision-state synthesis for material currentness, receiver, evaluator, proof, consequence or recovery risk. It records only state that can change a decision: consumer, required function, current authority, evidence limits and recovery or reopen conditions.

Handoff acceptance now treats transmitted state as distinct from receiver-current verification, while issue and plan guidance require a cold reader to reconstruct action without the authoring conversation.

What was already native

IMPLEMENTAUDIT already used durable state, explicit ownership, STOP markers, receiver readback, independent review and bounded delegation. The lineage composition sharpened their coordination meaning rather than creating a separate human-factors subsystem.

What was rejected

No mandatory cognitive-work worksheet, workload score, dashboard, review quota or methodology mode was adopted. Low-consequence single-actor work with transparent deterministic effect remains direct. A nominal human in the loop is not genuine intervention capability.

What remains unverified

This route does not prove better human-agent performance, synergy, workload reduction, complete common ground or universal cognitive fit. It does not establish that every mapped property changed the runtime. Cross-repository and live-host effectiveness remain unverified.
